Oscar's Two-Block Protocol
The Gorilla Stack for recomposition, and a separate repair block for the bicep once surgery is behind you.
Cycle 1
The full Gorilla Stack 2.0 — a 12-week main phase with a 4-week HGH taper on the end. Five compounds working the growth hormone axis, direct muscle signalling and the fat side all at once.
Cycle 2
A dedicated repair block for the torn bicep tendon, built to run after your operation. Four compounds pointed at tendon, inflammation, collagen and the bone the tendon anchors into.
The sequencing question
These two are not meant to overlap, and the surgery decides the order. If your operation is soon, run cycle 2 first and start the Gorilla Stack once you are healed and back to training properly — there is no point running a 16-week recomp block through a period where you cannot load the arm.
Talk to your surgeon first
This matters more than anything else on the page. Compounds that affect blood vessel growth and tissue healing belong in a conversation with the person operating on you — before the operation, not after. Do not start cycle 2 until they have cleared you, and follow their timing over ours.

Notes & Tips
Running This Well
Before anything else
The Surgery Comes First

Talk to your surgeon before you start cycle 2, and tell them you intend to use recovery peptides. Compounds that drive blood vessel growth and tissue healing are directly relevant to an operation — both to how it goes and to how they want you to recover. That is their call on timing, not ours.

The same applies in reverse: if the Gorilla Stack is already running when surgery is scheduled, tell them what you are on. Growth hormone and IGF-1 are not things a surgeon should find out about afterwards.

The IM injection
L-Carnitine Is the Different One

Every other compound on this page goes subcutaneously with an insulin needle. L-Carnitine is intramuscular — 0.85 mL through a 25G 1.5-inch needle into glute, quad or delt.

Push slowly. A fast IM push is what makes people sore for days. Rotate sites every single shot, and with a bicep repair in the picture, keep it well away from the arm you are rehabbing.

Reconstitution
Mixing Without Wrecking the Peptide

Aim the water at the side wall of the vial, not down onto the powder, and let it run down. Swirl gently or roll between your palms. Never shake — agitation breaks the peptide chains.

HGH is the fussy one. It must be refrigerated after mixing and it is the least tolerant of rough handling on this page. 2.4 mL per vial gives you the clean 1 IU = 0.10 mL maths that makes the taper easy to read.

What to expect
Cycle 1, Realistically

Weeks 1–4: sleep quality and recovery shift first — that is the HGH and CJC/Ipamorelin. Very little visible.

Weeks 4–8: body composition starts moving. Water retention in the hands or wrists early on is normal with HGH and usually settles.

Weeks 8–12: the recomposition becomes obvious. The 5-Amino ramp in Weeks 11–12 is timed for this stretch.

Weeks 13–16: HGH only, stepping down. You should not lose what you built during the taper — that is exactly why it steps rather than stops.

Cycle 2
Healing a Tendon Takes Longer Than You Want

Tendon is poorly supplied with blood, which is why it heals slowly and why BPC-157 — whose main mechanism is driving blood supply into tissue — is the centrepiece here.

Do not let feeling better become loading it early. These compounds can make the arm feel ready before the repair is structurally ready, and a re-tear costs far more than the weeks you save. Your surgeon and physio set the loading schedule. The peptides support that timeline; they do not shorten it on their own authority.
