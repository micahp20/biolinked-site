Eli's Lean, Strong & Durable Stack
Four compounds — strip fat, build muscle, and recover fast enough to keep up with the job.
The idea
get lean, add usable muscle, and stay durable — built for a job that demands you show up physically ready every day. Four compounds, split across a morning block and a pre-bed shot.
How they work together
Retatrutide is the fat-loss engine — a triple agonist (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon) that burns visceral fat and raises your calorie burn. 5-Amino-1MQ backs it up from a different angle, blocking the fat-storage enzyme NNMT and raising cellular NAD+ so you lean out faster. CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin is the muscle + recovery driver — a growth-hormone releasing pair taken pre-bed that lifts your nightly GH pulse for lean-muscle gain, deeper sleep, and better recovery. Wolverine (BPC-157 + TB-500) is the durability layer — it heals and protects tendons, joints, and soft tissue so the physical demands of the job don't wear you down.
The rhythm
mornings are fasted — Reta once a week on Monday, 5-Amino and Wolverine on Mon/Wed/Fri. CJC/Ipamorelin goes in pre-bed, fasted, every weekday for the nightly GH pulse. Weekends off. Run everything till the vials are empty, then reassess.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — especially the 5-Amino and Wolverine, so you crack a fresh vial only as the last one empties.
Reconstitution amounts
Reta 3 mL, CJC/Ipamorelin 2 mL, 5-Amino 1 mL each, Wolverine 1 mL each. Add the water slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, don't shake.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Daily Routine
A Morning Block + One Pre-Bed Shot
Two blocks a day, all fasted. AM (Mon/Wed/Fri): 5-Amino (20u) + Wolverine (10u). Add Reta Monday AM — 10u for Weeks 1–2, then 20u (Wk 3), 30u (Wk 4), 40u from Wk 5 on. PM every weekday: CJC/Ipamorelin (5u) right before bed. Weekends off.
Draw marks
Reta 10u → 40u (see the ramp), CJC/Ipa 5u, Wolverine 10u, 5-Amino 20u. Glance at the line each time — the 10u shots are small and easy to overshoot.
Tip 02 — CJC / Ipamorelin
Pre-Bed, Empty Stomach
CJC/Ipamorelin works by amplifying the growth-hormone pulse your body releases in deep sleep — so timing is everything. Take it right before bed, on an empty stomach (at least 2 hrs after your last meal). Carbs and fat spike insulin, which blunts GH release and wastes the dose.
A brief warm flush or light tingling right after is normal and passes in a minute. Deeper, better sleep is usually the first thing you notice; leaner recovery and body-composition changes build over the weeks.
Tip 03 — Reta: Start-of-Week Fat Loss
The Fat-Loss Anchor
Reta is once weekly, Monday AM — the fat-loss anchor. You start light and build: 1 mg (10u) Weeks 1–2, 2 mg (20u) Week 3, 3 mg (30u) Week 4, then 4 mg (40u) from Week 5 on — that's the cap. Expect appetite to drop through the week. Some mild nausea or fullness the first Monday or two is normal; eat lighter, lower-fat meals that day and hydrate hard (Reta blunts thirst). If a week hits hard, hold that dose another week before stepping up — message us and we adjust.
Tip 04 — Week-by-Week
What to Expect
Weeks 1–2
appetite drops from Reta and sleep deepens from the pre-bed CJC/Ipa. 5-Amino and Wolverine are working under the hood — don't expect big scale moves yet.
Weeks 3–5
fat loss picks up (Reta + 5-Amino together), recovery and muscle fullness improve from the nightly GH, and nagging joints/tendons start feeling more solid from Wolverine.
Weeks 6–10+
the lean-and-strong window. CJC/Ipa empties first (~4 weeks) — reorder it to keep the GH support going; Reta, 5-Amino, and Wolverine run longer. Run everything till the vials are empty, then reassess.
Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
Hold Reta if
persistent nausea/vomiting past 24 hrs, or severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. Don't push through — we drop the dose or pause.
CJC/Ipamorelin
a brief flush or tingling is normal; water retention or hand tingling that lingers means back the dose down.
Injection sites
a little redness at the pinhole is normal; spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain = possible infection — stop that site and message us.
Anything that feels off, message first
— don't push through or restart on your own.
