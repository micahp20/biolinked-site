Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time

Dry vials: refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.

After mixing: refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — open a fresh vial only as the last one empties.

Reconstitution amounts: Reta 3 mL, MOTS-c 4 mL, 5-Amino-1MQ 3 mL, PT-141 2 mL each, Oxytocin 10 mL each (two-step transfer — see the Oxytocin card). Add the water slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, don't shake.

Every shot: fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.

Tip 01 — The Daily Routine
One Fasted Morning Block

Everything is a morning shot, all fasted. Every weekday: MOTS-c (10u) + 5-Amino (10u). Add on Mon/Wed/Fri: PT-141 (5u) + Oxytocin (10u). Add on Mon + Thu: Retatrutide (10u). Wait 30–45 min before eating. Weekends off.

Draw marks: MOTS 10u, 5-Amino 10u, Oxytocin 10u, Reta 10u — all pull to the 10 line. PT-141 is the small one at 5u. Different site for each; rotate quadrants.

Tip 02 — PT-141 + Oxytocin
The Two Halves of Sexual Health

These two cover libido from different angles. PT-141 works in the brain on the desire/arousal pathway — it turns the want back on, even when stress or fatigue have flattened it. Oxytocin adds the connection side — closeness, sensitivity, a calmer warmer state. Micro-dosed on M/W/F, they build a steady baseline rather than a one-off spike.

These come at it differently than your Horny Goat Weed. That supplement works on blood flow (the plumbing); PT-141 and oxytocin work on the brain and connection side (the wiring). No overlap and no conflict — keep taking the Horny Goat Weed as you do.

First-dose note: a little flushing or mild nausea in the first hour of PT-141 can happen — the small 250 mcg micro-dose keeps that minimal, and it fades. Want an on-demand boost for a specific night? Take one 250–500 mcg PT-141 dose ~45 min before — message us first and we'll walk it through.

Tip 03 — Coming Off the GLP-1
Bridging Onto Retatrutide

You're transitioning off your old GLP-1 onto Reta. Start Reta about a week after your last GLP-1 dose — the appetite control carries over, so there's no gap where hunger comes roaring back. Begin at 10u Mon + Thu.

Hydrate hard. Reta blunts thirst just like your GLP-1 did — aim for ~3 L of water a day, add electrolytes, and that alone prevents most of the headaches and low energy people blame on the peptide. This also directly helps the goal of keeping your blood pressure down.

The last 10 lbs is slower — that's normal. 230 → 220 comes off at ~1 lb/week, not the faster early drop. MOTS + 5-Amino keep energy and fat-burning up so the scale keeps moving without the drained feeling.

Tip 04 — Week-by-Week
What to Expect

Weeks 1–2: energy comes first from MOTS + 5-Amino; Reta keeps appetite in check as you bridge off the GLP-1. PT-141 + Oxytocin start warming up the libido side — often noticeable within the first couple weeks.

Weeks 3–6: the scale moves steadily toward 220 and the sexual-health effects settle into a reliable baseline. 5-Amino finishes around Week 6 — the fat-loss + energy work carries on with Reta + MOTS.

Weeks 7–15: MOTS finishes ~Week 8; Reta runs the longest (~15 weeks) to carry you to goal. PT-141 + Oxytocin have months of supply. Once you hit 220, we talk maintenance — drop Reta to once weekly or pause, and keep the sexual-health pair going as long as you like.

Tip 05 — Your TRT, BP & Ankles
How This Fits the Rest of Your Health

Runs alongside your prescribed TRT. None of these five conflict with your weekly testosterone — keep taking it exactly as your doctor prescribed. The stack targets fat loss and libido; your TRT covers the low-T baseline underneath.

Blood pressure: you already saw weight loss bring it down — getting to 220 continues that. Stay on top of hydration + electrolytes on Reta, and keep monitoring at home. Nothing here raises blood pressure.

Ankle inflammation: dropping the last 10 lbs takes real load off the joints, and 5-Amino / MOTS lower systemic inflammation a little as a bonus. If the ankles still bug you once you're at goal, BPC-157 is the targeted soft-tissue / anti-inflammatory add-on for exactly that — just say the word and we'll slot it in.

Tip 06 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us

Hold Reta if: persistent nausea/vomiting past 24 hrs, or severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. Don't push through — we drop the dose or pause.

PT-141: strong flushing, a bad headache, or chest tightness means back off or skip — the micro-dose is meant to stay gentle.

Oxytocin: harmless at this micro-dose; if you ever feel lightheaded, sit down and hydrate.

Injection sites: a little redness at the pinhole is normal; spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain = possible infection — stop that site and message us.

Anything that feels off, message first — don't push through or restart on your own.
