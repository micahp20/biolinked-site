Jerrod's Two-Cycle Protocol
Fat loss and recomp on a fixed sixteen-week run, plus an as-needed sexual-health block you reach for when you want it.
Where you are
255 down to 230 already, off the old GLP-1. Cycle 1 finishes the last 10 pounds to 220 and changes the shape of what is left — that is the part a scale number alone will not tell you. Cycle 2 is the sexual-health block, and it is as-needed — no fixed weeks, used on the occasions you want it.
Cycle 1 — four compounds, two axes
Retatrutide is the appetite and deficit engine, titrated up one step a week rather than started at a working dose. Tesamorelin is the overnight growth-hormone arm — it goes specifically at visceral fat and at holding lean tissue through the cut, which is what turns 220 into a different-looking 220. Cagrilintide is a Friday-evening amylin shot that carries appetite control across the weekend on a receptor Reta does not touch. MOTS-c keeps daytime energy from falling out from under the deficit.
Cycle 2 — as needed, the two halves of libido
PT-141 works centrally on the desire and arousal pathway; Oxytocin adds the connection, warmth and sensitivity side. This block is as-needed, not scheduled — there is no week range and nothing goes on the calendar. Take the two together about 45 minutes before intimacy; that pairing is the whole point of having both.
Why they are sequenced, not stacked
The old page ran six compounds at once on a fixed grid. Splitting them means Cycle 1 never has more than four going, and the sexual-health pair comes off the calendar entirely — you use it on the occasions you want it rather than injecting it three times a week whether or not it is useful. Cycle 1 is a morning shot and an evening shot; Cycle 2 is two draws, together, when you want them.
Retatrutide is yours to steer
It opens at 5u, then steps up 5 units roughly every two weeks — 10u, 15u, 20u and onward — with a hold at each step to see how it sits. The ceiling is 60u (8 mg), raised from the old 20u cap so the ladder has room to keep going as long as you are tolerating it. How fast you climb is yours to drive. If nausea or reflux is changing how you eat, hold at the current step or drop back one and stay there — a dose you run every week beats a bigger one you skip.

Nutrition
Fueling 230 → 220 · And The Recomp After It
The frame

The last 10 pounds is a different problem from the first 25, and it is mostly a protein problem. You are running two compounds that suppress appetite and one that works on the growth-hormone axis — the risk in Cycle 1 is not overeating, it is quietly under-eating protein and losing lean tissue along with the fat. Three things decide the outcome: a calorie target you can actually live at, a protein number you defend on the days you are not hungry, and enough water that Retatrutide does not leave you flat. These targets hold across all sixteen weeks of Cycle 1 and stay useful after it.

Meal Structure
Three Meals + One Shake · Protein First
Anchor
A protein-forward breakfast every day. It sets 220 g up to be hit rather than chased at 9pm, and it is the single habit that makes the rest of this easy. On the mornings you take MOTS-c and Reta fasted, wait 30–45 minutes after the shot, then eat properly.
Protein first, then the vegetable, then the carb
Literally the order you eat the food on the plate. It blunts the glucose rise after the meal and it means you fill up on the thing you most need. Free, and it works at restaurants.
The shake is a real meal on this protocol
When Reta and Cagri have genuinely removed the appetite, 50 g of protein in a glass beats skipping the feeding entirely. Keep whey in the house specifically for the Monday and Tuesday after the shot, and for the weekend after the Friday Cagrilintide.
Dinner earlier and lighter than you are used to
Tesamorelin goes in pre-bed and needs two clear hours with no food — carbohydrate in particular blunts the GH pulse. Practically that means finishing dinner by around 8pm if you inject at 10. Push the day's carbs into breakfast and lunch to make that easy.
Whole foods, nothing eliminated
Protein at every feeding, vegetables at two of them, most carbs unprocessed. That is the whole rule — the rest is portion size. A plan you are still running in Week 16 beats a stricter one you abandon in Week 3.

Eating around the shots

Monday and Tuesday are the Reta days. Every evening is a Tesamorelin evening — two clear hours after your last bite, every weeknight, no exceptions on that one.

How To Run It
Three Things That Decide The Outcome
The Real Lever
Protein Is The Only Thing You Have To Defend
  • If a day falls apart, hit the protein number and let everything else go. Calories can be sloppy for a day. Protein should not be — it is what decides whether the last 10 lb comes off fat or off lean tissue.
  • Front-load it. 40–50 g at breakfast means you are a fifth of the way there before the day has a chance to go sideways. Chasing 220 g starting at dinner does not work.
  • Cheap high-protein staples: eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, chicken thighs and breast, canned tuna and salmon, lean ground turkey, frozen shrimp, whey. None of these need a recipe.
  • Weigh the protein for the first two weeks. Not forever — just long enough to learn what 8 oz actually looks like. Nearly everyone is 30–40% under when they guess.
  • Protein matters more here than on a plain GLP-1 run, because Tesamorelin and your TRT are both signalling the body to build. That signal needs raw material or it does nothing.
Hydration & Energy
The Two Things That Read As “The Peptide Isn't Working”
  • Under-drinking and under-eating both feel exactly like a flat, foggy afternoon. Retatrutide removes the signals that would normally tell you either one is happening.
  • 110–120 oz, mostly between meals rather than during them. Drinking a lot with food makes the fullness worse on Reta days without helping hydration much.
  • Do not drop below about 1,900 calories. If the appetite suppression is so strong that eating is a chore, that is a reason to hold or drop the Reta step, not to run a bigger deficit.
  • Caffeine cutoff by early afternoon. Tesamorelin works on the depth of the first sleep cycles — protecting sleep is protecting the compound you are paying the most for.
  • Alcohol is the cheapest thing to cut on a week you want the scale to move. Nearly pure calories with no protein, and it fragments the second half of the night, which is exactly the window Tesamorelin is working in.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Weekly Average
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 3–5 lb on water alone at your size.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? In this order: add 2,000 steps a day, then drop about 150 calories from the carb side, then step the Reta up early. Do not cut protein and do not cut fat to get there.
  • Losing faster than 2 lb a week? Add 200 calories back. Faster than that is water and muscle, and it comes straight back.
  • Take the waist measurement seriously from Week 7. The scale will slow before Tesamorelin is finished working. A waist that keeps dropping on a flat scale is the recomp doing exactly what it is supposed to.
  • At 220, calories come up, not down. Maintenance at 220 is lower than it was at 230, but the goal changes from deficit to holding — add back roughly 300–400 calories and keep the protein and the three sessions exactly where they are.

Notes & Tips

Tip 01 — The Daily Shape
One Fasted Morning, One Pre-Bed Evening
  • Morning, fasted: MOTS-c 15u (2 mg) every weekday through Week 8, plus Retatrutide on Monday at whatever step the ladder is on. Wait 30–45 minutes before eating.
  • Evening, pre-bed: Tesamorelin 20u every weeknight, at least two hours after your last food. Cagrilintide 10u joins it on the Friday.
  • From Week 9 the morning shrinks to Monday only and the Friday Cagrilintide is done — Tesamorelin carries the rest of the cycle on its own.
  • Different quadrant for each shot, and rotate quadrants week to week. On the Fridays you have two evening shots, they do not go in the same spot.
  • Weekends are off in Cycle 1 except the Friday-night Cagrilintide, which is doing its work across them.
Tip 02 — The One Rule That Matters
Tesamorelin Goes In On An Empty Stomach
Tesamorelin works by triggering your own overnight growth-hormone pulse, and food — carbohydrate above all — blunts that pulse directly. Everything else in this protocol is forgiving about timing. This one is not.
  • Two clear hours after your last bite, last thing before bed. If you inject at 10pm, dinner is done by 8.
  • Push the day's carbs into breakfast and lunch so the evening is naturally light. The Nutrition tab is built around this.
  • Deeper sleep is usually the first thing you notice, often inside the first week — and it is a decent sign the timing is right.
  • Mild puffiness in the hands or fingers in the mornings of Weeks 1–2 is the known GH ramp profile. It settles by Week 3.
  • Missed the window and already ate? Skip that night rather than injecting on a full stomach. A missed dose costs less than a blunted one teaches you the wrong lesson.
Tip 03 — Retatrutide
Climb The Ladder, Don't Jump It
You are coming off a GLP-1, so the appetite control carries over — there is no gap where hunger comes roaring back while Reta ramps. Start Reta about a week after your last dose of the old compound, at 5u.
  • 5u steps, about two weeks at each, Monday mornings: 5u in Week 1, 10u in Weeks 2–3, 15u in Weeks 4–5, 20u in Weeks 6–7 and onward. The ceiling is 60u (8 mg), raised from the old 20u cap — but it is a ceiling, not a target, and the pace is yours.
  • Hydrate hard on Monday and Tuesday. Reta blunts thirst as much as hunger, and dehydration is what most people are actually feeling when they say the peptide made them flat.
  • Lighter, lower-fat meals on Monday and Tuesday. That is the 48-hour window where nausea is most likely, and fat is the macro that makes it worse.
  • If a step is rough, hold it rather than climbing. Two weeks at a dose you tolerate beats one week at a dose you skip.
  • The last 10 lb comes off at about a pound a week, not the faster early drop. MOTS-c and Tesamorelin are what keep energy and lean mass intact while it does.
Tip 04 — Cagrilintide
The Friday Shot Is For The Weekend
  • Friday evening, once a week. Not before a meal — before the weekend. Its 24–48 hours of appetite suppression is meant to land on Saturday and Sunday.
  • It works on amylin, not GLP-1, so it adds appetite control without adding to what Retatrutide is already asking of your stomach.
  • Mild nausea on the first one or two Fridays is the known profile and it settles. If it does not, drop to 5u (250 mcg) and hold there.
  • Fresh vial on the Friday of Week 1 and Week 5. Two vials is eight Friday shots — Cagrilintide finishes at the end of Week 8.
Tip 05 — Cycle 2
Take Them Together, About 45 Minutes Before
Cycle 2 is as-needed — no week range, no weekly cadence, nothing on the calendar. The two compounds are meant to be run as a pair: both draws together, roughly 45 minutes before intimacy. Two separate draws, different abdomen quadrants, same sitting.
  • The pairing is the point. PT-141 drives desire through the brain's arousal pathway; Oxytocin adds connection, warmth and physical sensitivity. Neither covers the other's half, which is why they run together rather than one or the other.
  • PT-141 15u (500 mcg) and Oxytocin 5u (50 mcg), both about 45 minutes ahead. The effect builds over 45–60 minutes and rides for several hours.
  • Both of those are starting doses, and there is room to move up if you tolerate them well. PT-141 goes 500 mcg → 750 mcg (22.5u) → 1 mg (30u); Oxytocin goes 50 mcg → 75 mcg (7.5u) → 100 mcg (10u). Change one compound at a time so you know which one moved the needle.
  • Does not need to be fasted and does not need to be a morning. This block is off the calendar entirely — use it on the occasions you want it.
  • Up to about twice a week. Spacing keeps the response sharp; back-to-back days dull it.
  • Mix one vial of each, not all four. A mixed vial keeps about 30 days, and at these draws one PT-141 vial is 20 doses and one Oxytocin bottle is 200 — open the second only when the first is done.
  • It can run during Cycle 1 or after it. Nothing in Cycle 1 conflicts with either compound.
