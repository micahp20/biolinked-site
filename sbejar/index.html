Stephany's Cycle 1 · Fat Loss & Metabolic Reset
Three compounds, one morning window. Retatrutide handles appetite; MOTS-c and NAD+ work on how well the cell turns what is left into energy.
Your primary goal
Hormonal balance, with fat loss right behind it — 210 lb toward 175, about 35 lb. This cycle is the fat-loss half, because 35 lb is the longer job and it is easier to start while motivation is highest. The hormonal side is a separate conversation once this one is running.
Menopause is the frame
At 47 and in menopause, fat loss is harder for reasons that have nothing to do with effort — the metabolic brake is real. That is exactly why this cycle is not appetite alone: MOTS-c and NAD+ target the mitochondrial side of the brake while Retatrutide handles intake. Expect the waist and the way clothes fit to move ahead of the scale.
First time on compounds
You have some injection experience, but this is your first peptide protocol. Nothing starts on the same day — the on-ramp in the Notes tab staggers every compound so a reaction has one obvious cause. The Retatrutide ladder also has an explicit instruction to stop climbing wherever it is working rather than chasing the top rung.
Five hours of sleep, stress at 5 of 5
That combination is its own headwind — it raises appetite, holds water and blunts fat loss on its own. Nothing in this cycle fixes it, so the sleep and stress section on the Nutrition tab is not filler; on this protocol it is a lever with as much leverage as any vial.
You are under a doctor's care
You are on prescribed thyroid and hormone therapy and an antidepressant. Nothing on this page replaces or changes any of it — keep taking everything exactly as prescribed, and tell your PCP you are starting this protocol before Cycle 1.

Menopause
Two Things That Change After 45
Muscle is the priority
Lean mass falls faster after menopause, and it is what holds the metabolic rate up. That is why the protein number is the one target on this page worth defending. Add resistance work twice a week as tolerated — you are at 1–2 light sessions now, so two short full-body sessions is a real step, not a big one. Bodyweight, bands or dumbbells all count.
Fiber does double duty
It handles the Reta constipation and it supports hormone metabolism from the food side. Vegetables at every meal is the whole instruction — no supplement needed.
Sleep & Stress
Five Hours And 5-Of-5 Is The Real Headwind

Short sleep and high stress raise appetite, hold water and slow fat loss on their own — independent of anything you eat or inject. Four things, in order of leverage: a fixed wake time, seven days a week, which does more for sleep than a fixed bedtime; no screens or work email in the last 45 minutes; an early dinner, which Cycle 1 requires anyway; and ten minutes of something deliberately unproductive in the evening — a walk, a bath, sitting outside. If you get sleep from five hours to six and a half, that alone will show up on the scale. Cycle 2 is aimed at the same target from the other side.

One thing already in your favour

You do not drink. Alcohol is usually the biggest single lever on a fat-loss page — empty calories, broken sleep, next-morning water retention. It is already handled, which means the levers left are protein, water, sleep and stress.

Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01 — Your First Protocol
What The First Two Weeks Actually Feel Like
  • You have injected before, but not these. The mechanics are the same: pinch, 45–90°, subcutaneous into the abdomen or the outer thigh, done in seconds.
  • Three things that make it comfortable: a fresh 31G needle every time, let the alcohol dry completely before the needle goes in, and let the syringe sit out of the fridge for a minute — cold liquid stings and bruises more.
  • Rotate properly. Move an inch or two each time and work around the abdominal ring. Where two shots share a morning, put them in opposite quadrants.
  • A small red mark or a little bruise is normal. A raised welt that itches is worth noting — write down which compound, which site, which day, and send it over.
  • Nausea in the 24–48 hours after the Monday Retatrutide is the known profile, loudest in the first two weeks. Lighter, lower-fat meals on Monday and Tuesday make a real difference.
  • Nothing here is judged in week one. Appetite is the only thing that shows up that fast.
Note 02 — The On-Ramp
Nothing Starts On The Same Day

Three compounds, started one at a time, so that if something does not agree with you there is one obvious cause rather than three candidates.

  • Week 1, Monday. Retatrutide only — 10u. Nothing else this week.
  • Week 2, Monday. Add NAD+ at 50u on the Monday / Wednesday / Friday mornings. Push it slowly.
  • Week 3, Monday. Add MOTS-c at 15u on the same Monday / Wednesday / Friday mornings.
  • From Week 3 the full week is running: three shots Monday, two on Wednesday and Friday, all in the same fasted morning window.
If you would rather start everything at once

You can — none of these three conflict. The stagger is only there to make a reaction easy to trace. If you start together and something feels off, you will not know which one to drop.

Note 03 — Retatrutide
The Ladder, And Where To Get Off It
  • One shot a week, Monday morning, up a milligram each week. 10u, 20u, 30u, 40u, 50u, 60u — mixed at 10 mg/mL so the ladder reads straight off the barrel.
  • The top of the ladder is a maximum, not a destination. If appetite is controlled and the waist is moving at 2 or 3 mg, stop climbing and hold there. Spending side effects you do not need to spend is the most common way a good ladder goes wrong — and on a first protocol there is no reason to.
  • If a rung is not tolerable, go back one and stay there. Do not push through a step that changes how you eat.
  • You have run semaglutide and tirzepatide before, so the class is familiar — but Retatrutide adds a glucagon arm the other two do not have. Treat the early rungs as new rather than assuming your old tolerance carries over.
  • Constipation is the other common one and it is mostly water and fiber. The 90–100 oz target and the vegetables on the Nutrition tab are the fix.
  • Never double a missed dose. If a Monday is missed, take it within 24 hours; past that, skip it and resume the next Monday at the same rung.
Note 006 — Missed Doses
Skip It, Do Not Stack It

The rule is the same for all three: a missed dose is skipped, never doubled.

  • Missed the Monday Retatrutide? Take it within two days if you catch it. Past that, skip it and resume the next Monday at the same rung — do not climb after a missed week.
  • Missed a MOTS-c or NAD+ morning? Skip it and carry on at the next scheduled day. One gap changes nothing.
  • Never take two in one day to catch up. That is the only way to turn a missed dose into a bad day.
  • If you miss more than a week of Retatrutide, drop back one rung when you restart rather than picking up where you left off.
Note 07 — Your Prescriptions
Nothing Here Replaces What Your Doctor Gave You
  • Keep taking every prescription exactly as written. Nothing on this page substitutes for your thyroid medication, your hormone therapy or your antidepressant, and none of them should be changed or spaced differently because of this protocol.
  • Tell your PCP you are starting a peptide protocol before Cycle 1, and again before Cycle 3. They are managing your hormones; they should know what else is going on.
  • Your thyroid tablet keeps its own routine — first thing in the morning, on an empty stomach, well clear of food and other supplements. The morning injections do not interfere with that and do not change the timing.
  • If anything changes on the prescription side during these 23 weeks — a dose adjustment, a new medication, a lab result — send it over, because it may change the ordering of the cycles.
  • Anything that feels wrong is worth reporting the same day, to us and, if it is more than mild, to your doctor.
