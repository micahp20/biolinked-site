Personalized Protocol
Stephany's Four-Cycle Protocol
One goal per cycle, run back to back — so you can always tell which compound is responsible for what you are feeling.
Your primary goal
Hormonal balance, with fat loss right behind it — 210 lb toward 175, about 35 lb. Cycle 3 is the one built for the hormonal side; fat loss runs first because 35 lb is the longer job and it is easier to start while motivation is highest.
Menopause is the frame
At 47 and in menopause, fat loss is harder for reasons that have nothing to do with effort — the metabolic brake is real. Cycle 1 pairs Retatrutide with Tesamorelin so one creates the deficit while the other works overnight on deep abdominal fat, and KLOW protects connective tissue while you lose. Expect the waist and the way clothes fit to move ahead of the scale.
First time on compounds
You have some injection experience, but this is your first peptide protocol. Nothing starts on the same day — the on-ramp in the Notes tab staggers every compound so a reaction has one obvious cause. The Retatrutide ladder also has an explicit instruction to stop climbing wherever it is working rather than chasing the top rung.
Five hours of sleep, stress at 5 of 5
That combination is its own headwind — it raises appetite, holds water and blunts fat loss on its own. Cycle 2 is a bedtime block aimed squarely at it, and the sleep and stress section on the Nutrition tab is not filler; on this protocol it is a lever with as much leverage as any vial.
You are under a doctor's care
You are on prescribed thyroid and hormone therapy and an antidepressant. Nothing on this page replaces or changes any of it — keep taking everything exactly as prescribed, and tell your PCP you are starting this protocol before Cycle 1.
One goal per cycle
Cycle 1 — Fat Loss. Cycle 2 — Longevity & Anti-Aging. Cycle 3 — Sexual Health & Libido. Cycle 4 — Cognitive, run as needed. They run in sequence with no overlap.

Menopause
Two Things That Change After 45
Muscle is the priority
Lean mass falls faster after menopause, and it is what holds the metabolic rate up. That is why the protein number is the one target on this page worth defending. Add resistance work twice a week as tolerated — you are at 1–2 light sessions now, so two short full-body sessions is a real step, not a big one. Bodyweight, bands or dumbbells all count.
Fiber does double duty
It handles the Reta constipation and it supports hormone metabolism from the food side. Vegetables at every meal is the whole instruction — no supplement needed.
Sleep & Stress
Five Hours And 5-Of-5 Is The Real Headwind

Short sleep and high stress raise appetite, hold water and slow fat loss on their own — independent of anything you eat or inject. Four things, in order of leverage: a fixed wake time, seven days a week, which does more for sleep than a fixed bedtime; no screens or work email in the last 45 minutes; an early dinner, which Cycle 1 requires anyway; and ten minutes of something deliberately unproductive in the evening — a walk, a bath, sitting outside. If you get sleep from five hours to six and a half, that alone will show up on the scale. Cycle 2 is aimed at the same target from the other side.

One thing already in your favour

You do not drink. Alcohol is usually the biggest single lever on a fat-loss page — empty calories, broken sleep, next-morning water retention. It is already handled, which means the levers left are protein, water, sleep and stress.

How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water into a syringe, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
It looks like nothing
The powder is often just a faint film at the bottom — that is normal, not a short vial. Everything here mixes clear except the KLOW, which carries a blue tint. That is the GHK-Cu and it is correct.
The Oxytocin is different
10 mL of water into one vial — five times what anything else takes. Mixed at 2 mL by habit, the same 10u draw would be five times the dose. Measure it, and label that vial first.
Mix only what you are starting
One cycle at a time. Later cycles stay dry and in the box. Within Cycle 1, mix Reta, Tesamorelin and KLOW on the first Monday and the 5-Amino-1MQ on the Monday of Week 2.
Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days. Keep them toward the back of the fridge rather than the door, where the temperature swings.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix. On the Retatrutide, write the week as well as the units — the ladder changes every Monday. Kisspeptin and PT-141 look identical in a fridge and land in the same cycle; so do the two amidate vials. Those are the two pairs worth labelling carefully.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin, let the alcohol dry, rotate sites. Let the syringe sit out of the fridge for a minute first — cold liquid stings more, which matters while you are still getting used to injecting.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01 — Your First Protocol
What The First Two Weeks Actually Feel Like
  • You have injected before, but not these. The mechanics are the same: pinch, 45–90°, subcutaneous into the abdomen or the outer thigh, done in seconds.
  • Three things that make it comfortable: a fresh 31G needle every time, let the alcohol dry completely before the needle goes in, and let the syringe sit out of the fridge for a minute — cold liquid stings and bruises more.
  • Rotate properly. Move an inch or two each time and work around the abdominal ring. Where two shots share a morning, put them in opposite quadrants.
  • A small red mark or a little bruise is normal. A raised welt that itches is worth noting — write down which compound, which site, which day, and tell Micah.
  • Nausea in the 24–48 hours after the Monday Retatrutide is the known profile, loudest in the first two weeks. Lighter, lower-fat meals on Monday and Tuesday make a real difference.

Note 06 — Missed Doses
Skip It, Do Not Stack It
  • Missed the Monday Retatrutide? Take it within 24 hours, then go back to Mondays. Past that, skip it and resume next Monday at the same rung. Never double it.
  • Missed a Tesamorelin night? Skip it — do not take it in the morning instead. The overnight pulse is the point.
  • Missed a KLOW or 5-Amino morning? Skip it and resume the next weekday. One gap changes nothing.
  • Missed a day of a 20-day block? Continue the count the next day and finish day 20 one day later. Do not take two in a day to catch up.
  • Missed a Kisspeptin, Oxytocin or PT-141? Skip it. None of them has a loading effect one gap undoes.
  • Missed a whole week? Pick up where you left off — that cycle runs a week longer and everything after shifts by a week. Nothing breaks.
