Reconstitution & Storage
Mixing, Then Keeping It Cold
💧 Mixing — Two Sets Of Three
Every vial
3 mL of BAC water — Retatrutide, 5-Amino-1MQ and MOTS-c all the same. Mix all three on the Monday of Week 1, and the second set on the Monday of Week 5.
How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Storage
Dry vials at 36–46°F, out of light, never frozen. Mixed vials go straight back in the fridge and are finished inside 30 days. The second set stays dry in the box until Week 5.
Label them
Write the compound and the dose on every vial you mix. All three are morning shots and they look identical once clear.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01
Monday Is The Big Morning
Monday carries all three shots — Retatrutide is Monday only. Wednesday and Friday are just the 5-Amino-1MQ and the MOTS-c. Three separate shots, three separate spots. Never combine them in one syringe.
Note 02
Injection Basics
  • Subcutaneous — abdomen, love handle or outer thigh. Pinch the skin, 45–90°, slow push. Fresh needle every time.
  • Rotate sites so no spot takes a shot two sessions running.
  • Read the small draws at eye level. 6u and 7.5u are short pulls — it is easy to overshoot.
  • A small bruise or a bead of blood is normal. Spreading redness, heat or a lump that grows is not — stop and call.
Note 03
If You Miss One
Skip it and carry on with the next scheduled dose. Never double up. If the Monday Retatrutide slips a day, take it Tuesday and put the following week's back on Monday — do not take two in one week.
Note 04
What To Expect
Appetite usually drops on the first or second Retatrutide shot. Mild nausea in the 24–48 hours after the Monday shot is the known profile and normally settles by the second or third week. Eat protein, drink water, and do not chase the scale daily — the waist moves before the number does.
Note 05
Adjusting The Retatrutide
2 mg is the working dose for this cycle. If the nausea or food aversion is changing how you eat, drop to 1.5 mg (11u) or 1 mg (7.5u) and hold there — eight weeks at a tolerated dose beats four at an intolerable one. If appetite control has faded by Week 4 and the shot is easy, 2.5 mg is 19u.
