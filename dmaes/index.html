Personalized Protocol
Donovan's Fat-Loss Cycle
Two compounds, eight weeks — three fasted mornings a week and nothing else to remember.
The goal
225 to 190 — about 35 lb — and to come out the other side leaner and stronger rather than just smaller. You said no rush, and this is built that way: a moderate deficit, a slow dose ladder, and a schedule light enough to survive work, family and two coaching seasons.
How the two work together
Retatrutide is the fat-loss engine — a triple agonist (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon) that quiets appetite, burns visceral fat and raises your calorie burn. 5-Amino-1MQ attacks the same goal from the opposite side, blocking NNMT — the enzyme inside fat cells that acts as a brake on burning stored fat. Reta reduces what goes in; 5-Amino makes what you already carry easier to get rid of.
The rhythm
Mornings are fasted. 5-Amino on Mon/Wed/Fri, with Retatrutide alongside it on Monday only. Nothing in the evenings, nothing on weekends — four shots a week total.
You've had a GLP-1 before
Semaglutide and tirzepatide both gave you nausea and headache, so the Retatrutide ladder here starts on the lowest rung, 1 mg, and climbs in half-milligram steps rather than whole ones. Slower up is the whole point — you have the time.
How long it runs
You asked us to pick the length, so it runs on the house standard: 8 weeks. Week counts are nominal — the cycle finishes when the bottles finish, and a week either side is normal.

Nutrition
Eating For 225 → 190
The short version

Hit the protein, keep the deficit moderate, and make the default meal easy. Between work, family and two coaching seasons you are not going to meal-prep like a bodybuilder — so this tab is built around food you can get in under five minutes.

Daily Targets
Protein
180–200 g a day — about 0.8–0.9 g per pound at your current weight, and roughly 1 g per pound of your goal weight. This is the number that decides whether the 35 lb comes off as fat or as fat and muscle together. Spread it over 3–4 feedings rather than loading dinner.
Calories
A moderate deficit — roughly 500 below maintenance, which is about 1–1.5 lb a week and puts 190 lb somewhere in the 6–8 month range. You said no rush; this is what no-rush looks like and it is the pace that holds muscle.
Carbs
The adjustable lever. Put most of them around training and around the days you are on your feet coaching. Rest days can run lower without touching protein or fat.
Fat
Do not cut fat to the floor — it runs your hormones. Olive oil, eggs, nuts, avocado, fatty fish; roughly a quarter to a third of calories. Keep it lower on Monday and Tuesday, though — fat is the macro that makes early Reta nausea worse.
Water
110+ oz a day, and add 20–30 oz Monday and Tuesday after the Reta shot. Reta blunts thirst as well as appetite, and dehydration is the most common cause of a GLP-1 headache — which is exactly what bothered you on semaglutide and tirzepatide.
Fibre
30–35 g. Slowed gastric emptying on a GLP-1 plus low fibre is what makes people constipated and blame the peptide. Vegetables, berries, beans, oats.
How To Run It
Practical, Not Perfect
On The Go
Food You Can Get In Five Minutes
  • Keep three grab-and-go proteins in the house at all times. Rotisserie chicken, pre-cooked ground beef or turkey, and a tub of Greek yogurt or cottage cheese. That is 40–50 g of protein without cooking anything.
  • A shaker bottle and a tub of whey lives in the truck or the coaching bag. One scoop plus water is 25 g and closes the gap on the nights you get home at 9.
  • Eggs, jerky, tuna pouches, string cheese, protein shakes, deli turkey. These are the entire emergency list — none of them need a plate.
  • Drive-thru is fine if you order like an adult: grilled chicken, double the meat, skip the fries and the soda. A grilled chicken sandwich plus a side salad beats skipping the meal and eating everything at 10pm.
  • Cook once, eat three times. A tray of chicken thighs and a tray of potatoes on Sunday covers the three worst weeknights. That is the only prep this plan asks for.
A Default Day
What This Actually Looks Like
  • Breakfast — after the morning shot, 30–45 min later. 4 eggs plus a cup of Greek yogurt and fruit, or a 3-egg scramble with leftover meat. ~45 g protein.
  • Lunch. Rotisserie chicken or ground beef over rice with a bag of microwave vegetables. ~50 g.
  • Practice-time snack. Shake, jerky, or a tuna pouch on the way to the field or the mat. ~30 g.
  • Dinner. Protein, a starch, vegetables. ~50 g. Earlier and lighter beats late and heavy — both for the scale and for how you sleep.
  • Cottage cheese or casein before bed if you are short on the day's protein. Slow protein overnight, and it is the easiest 25–30 g you will get all day.
Retatrutide
Eating On A GLP-1
  • Hit the protein number whether or not you feel like eating. If a full meal is too much volume, a shake covers 40 g without the bulk. Under-eating protein is the single biggest risk on a GLP-1.
  • Monday and Tuesday: smaller, lower-fat meals. That is the nausea window, and it shrinks as you go up the ladder slowly instead of fast.
  • Eat slowly and stop at comfortably full, not stuffed. Gastric emptying is slowed — the “I ate too much” signal arrives late.
  • Do not chase a bigger deficit just because appetite is easy. The appetite suppression is the tool, not the target.
Sleep
The Half Of This That Happens In Bed
  • You are at seven hours and rating it a 2. Depth is the problem, not just length — and poor sleep drives up appetite and drives down recovery, which makes a deficit harder than it needs to be.
  • Caffeine cutoff at noon. Five-to-six hour half-life means a 3pm coffee still has a quarter of its dose working at bedtime.
  • A light, early dinner sleeps better than a big late one. It is also the easier way to land the day's calories.
  • Same wake time, weekends included. With coaching schedules the bedtime moves; the wake time is the one you can hold, and it is the one the body clock keys off.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Weekly Average
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? Drop about 150 calories — a smaller carb portion at dinner covers it. Do not cut protein to get there.
  • Losing faster than 1.5–2 lb a week? Add calories back. Faster than that starts costing lean tissue.
  • Lift 2–3 times a week if you can. You are at 1–2 sessions now; the lifting is what tells your body to keep the muscle while the deficit takes the fat.
  • Watch the tape and the mirror, not just the scale. At 33 with a lifting stimulus the scale can sit still for a couple of weeks while the waist keeps dropping.

Reconstitution
Mixing, Step By Step
💧 Mix One Vial At A Time
1 · Clean
Alcohol pad on the rubber top of both the peptide vial and the BAC water vial. Let them air dry — do not blow on them or wipe them off.
2 · Draw the water
Pull the exact BAC volume from the table above — 3 mL for the Reta, 1 mL for a 5-Amino vial. Do not reuse the last one's number.
3 · Down the side
Angle the needle so the water runs down the inside wall of the vial onto the powder, not straight into it. Peptides are fragile; a jet of water shears them.
4 · Never shake
Swirl gently, or roll the vial between your palms. Cloudiness that clears in a minute or two is normal — give it time rather than agitating it.
5 · Label it
Write the compound and the mix date on the vial. Mix one 5-Amino vial at a time — crack a fresh one only as the last empties.
Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
Unmixed powder
Fridge at 36–46°F is fine; freezer is better for anything you will not open for months. Keep it dark and dry.
Mixed vials
Fridge, always — back shelf, not the door. The door swings through room temperature every time you open it. Use within about 30 days of mixing.
Never freeze a mixed vial
Freezing and thawing a reconstituted peptide destroys it. If a vial freezes solid, it is gone.
One needle, one shot
A fresh syringe every time — for the draw and the injection both. They dull immediately, and a dull needle is what makes an injection hurt.
Notes & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Routine
Three Mornings A Week
  • Mon/Wed/Fri, fasted, first thing: 5-Amino-1MQ 20u. On Monday add Reta at 10u, climbing to 25u by Week 7.
  • Wait 30–45 minutes before eating. Water and black coffee are fine in the gap.
  • Nothing in the evenings, nothing on weekends. Four shots a week, and two of the seven days are completely clear.
  • Glance at the barrel line each time. You have shot before, but the 10u Reta draw is small and easy to overshoot — the 20u 5-Amino is the more forgiving one.
  • Rotate sites. The abdomen has four quadrants; never the same one-inch spot twice in a day. Monday is two shots — two different spots.
Tip 02 — Retatrutide
Going Up Slowly Is The Plan
  • Semaglutide and tirzepatide both gave you nausea and headache — so this ladder starts at the lowest rung and moves in half-milligram steps: 1 mg for two weeks, then 1.5, then 2, then 2.5. Most people who react badly to a GLP-1 reacted to the speed of the climb, not the drug.
  • Two clean weeks before every step up. If a week is rough, hold that dose another week — or drop back to 0.75 mg (7.5u) or 0.5 mg (5u). There is no prize for being at a higher number.
  • Headache is usually water. Reta blunts thirst, so you will not feel the dehydration coming. Add 20–30 oz Monday and Tuesday and most of it disappears.
  • Nausea is usually fat and volume. Smaller, lower-fat meals Monday and Tuesday; eat slowly; stop at comfortably full.
  • Message us before you change anything. The ladder is meant to be tuned to how you actually feel, and that is a conversation, not a guess.
Tip 03 — 5-Amino-1MQ
The Quiet One
  • You will not feel this one, and that is normal. It works by lifting an enzymatic brake inside the fat cell — there is no appetite change and no immediate sensation to track. It shows up in the mirror, not on the day.
  • Same fasted morning slot as the Reta. Keeping both in one window is what makes the routine stick.
  • Mix one vial at a time. Four small vials, each about ten doses — open a fresh one only when the last is empty, so nothing sits mixed longer than it should.
  • It runs out before the Reta does, around Week 7 at three mornings a week. That is expected.
Tip 04 — Week By Week
What To Expect
Weeks 1–2
appetite starts easing from the Reta — usually the first thing you notice, within a few days. 5-Amino is working under the hood. Do not expect big scale moves yet.
Weeks 3–5
fat loss picks up as the ladder climbs and the two compounds stack. This is where the weekly average starts moving consistently.
Weeks 6–8
the ladder is at its highest and the deficit is doing real work. 5-Amino empties around Week 7; the Reta vial carries past Week 8. Take stock at the end and message us to plan what comes next.
