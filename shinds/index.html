Tip 01 — Reta Onboarding
What the First Few Weeks Look Like
Week 1
appetite suppression starts within 3–5 days. Mild nausea or "full quickly" feeling on shot days (Mon/Wed/Fri) is normal — manageable with smaller meals and lower fat content for 24 hrs after the shot. Skip greasy / fried food the day after a dose day. Hydrate aggressively.
Week 2–3
initial GI side effects settle. Appetite control feels like baseline. Food noise quiets noticeably. Some mild fatigue is normal as the body acclimates to the deficit.
Weeks 4–6
fat loss curve takes hold. Aim for 1–1.5 lbs/wk — faster than 2 lbs/wk sustained means the deficit is too aggressive (or you're losing more water than fat). GHK is doing its skin work in the background — you may notice clearer skin tone or improved elasticity around Week 4.
Weeks 7+
the loss continues at a steady pace. Watch for the curve to flatten — that's the signal it might be time to drop to Tier 2 (M/Thu) and let the body re-sensitize.
Tip 02 — The Tier-Down Ladder
When to Drop Tiers (And Why)
The tier-down isn't on a fixed calendar — it's response-driven. Here's the decision framework:
Tier 1 (M/W/F at 1 mg) — stay here as long as
the loss curve is steady at 1–1.5 lbs/wk, GI tolerance is solid, energy is steady. This is the active loss phase. Most clients stay on Tier 1 for the bulk of their cycle.
Drop to Tier 2 (M/Thu) when
the loss curve flattens for 2+ consecutive weeks even though diet and effort haven't slipped, OR you're 5–10 lbs from your goal weight, OR the deficit is starting to feel hard to sustain (energy crashing, sleep getting wrecked, mood dropping). Tier 2 stretches the Reta supply, gives the body a metabolic breather, and ramps gently into maintenance.
Drop to Tier 3 (once weekly) when
you hit your desired weight and want to lock it in. Once-weekly Reta keeps appetite quiet and prevents the rebound that normally hits when GLP-1 signaling drops off entirely. Pick a fixed day — Sunday or Monday is common. Hold this for as long as you want to keep body comp stable.
What NOT to do
don't drop tiers because you're impatient. The 3×/week cadence is what drives the loss curve — dropping early just slows the loss. Tier-downs are response-driven, not boredom-driven.
Tip 03 — Hydration on Reta
The Thing Most People Underestimate
Target: 2.5–3 L of water per day
Reta suppresses thirst signaling alongside appetite — you won't feel thirsty even when you're dehydrated. That's how people end up with headaches, constipation, fatigue, and poor energy and blame "the peptide" when it's actually water deficit.
Add electrolytes once daily
Sodium, potassium, magnesium. LMNT, Liquid IV, or similar work fine.
Coffee doesn't count
Drink it on top of your water target, not instead of.

Tip 05 — Missed Doses
What to Do If You Skip
Reta (M/W/F)
if you miss a shot day, take it within 2 days of the missed day and resume the original schedule. If more than 2 days have passed, skip that dose and resume next scheduled day. Don't double up.
GHK-Cu (daily)
miss one day, skip it. Resume the next day. Missing 1–2 days across the cycle doesn't move the needle on the collagen signal.
Tip 06 — Red Flags
When to Pause

Hold the next Reta dose if any of the following:

· Persistent nausea or vomiting that doesn't resolve within 24 hours
· Severe abdominal pain, especially radiating to the back — rule out pancreatitis
· Gallbladder-area pain or RUQ pain after fatty meals
· Rapid weight loss above 3 lbs/week sustained — deficit is too aggressive, eat more
· Lightheadedness or dizziness on standing — could be blood sugar or dehydration; hydrate, eat, monitor
Hold GHK-Cu if
significant injection-site reaction (redness, heat, swelling past 24 hrs) or any allergic-type symptom (hives, breathing changes). GHK is well-tolerated but copper-allergy reactions can occur rarely.
