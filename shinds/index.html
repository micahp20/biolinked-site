Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Sealed powder keeps for months — which is exactly why there's no rush to mix all four.
Reconstitution
Reta 3 mL per vial, GHK-Cu 3 mL per vial. Add the water slowly down the inside wall, swirl gently, never shake.
Mix one at a time
Open a vial only as the one before it empties. With four vials in hand and a 30-day window once mixed, this is the difference between using your supply and wasting it.
Label them
Write the compound on each vial. Both draw at 10u, so the label is the only thing telling them apart in a fridge door at 6am.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Notes & Tips
How To Run It Well
Tip 01 · Reference
Common Reta Cadences
You run Reta on your own cadence. This is just what the frequencies tend to be used for, if it's a useful reference.
  • M/W/F is the usual active-loss frequency — used while the curve holds at 1–1.5 lbs/wk with solid GI tolerance and steady energy.
  • M/Thu is where people usually go when the curve flattens for 2+ weeks with the diet unchanged, they're 5–10 lbs from goal, or the deficit starts wrecking sleep and mood.
  • Once weekly is the maintenance frequency — enough signal to keep appetite quiet and prevent rebound, without the deficit pressure.
  • Worth knowing: frequency is what drives the loss curve — easing off early mostly just slows it down.
Tip 02 · Hydration
The Thing Most People Underestimate
  • Target 2.5–3 L of water a day. Reta suppresses thirst signalling alongside appetite — you won't feel thirsty even when you're dry. Headaches, constipation and flat energy usually trace back to water, not the peptide.
  • Electrolytes once daily — sodium, potassium, magnesium. LMNT, Liquid IV or similar.
  • Coffee doesn't count toward the target. Drink it on top.
Tip 03 · GHK-Cu
What To Expect From The Skin Work
  • Mon–Fri AM, 10u (~1.67 mg), SubQ. Doesn't need to be fasted — eat normally around it. Weekends off.
  • Skin tone and elasticity usually show up around week 3–4, face first. Minor cuts and scrapes healing faster is the same signal working.
  • Mild redness at the site for an hour is normal. Redness, heat or swelling still there past 24 hours is not — hold it and text Micah.
  • Miss a day, skip it and resume the next. One or two missed days won't move the collagen signal.
Tip 04 · Missed Doses
What To Do If You Skip
  • Reta: take it within 2 days of the missed day and resume the original schedule. Past that, skip it and pick up on the next scheduled day. Never double up.
  • GHK-Cu: skip it and resume the next day.
Tip 05 · Red Flags
When To Pause
Hold the next Reta dose and text Micah first — don't push through and don't restart on your own.
  • Persistent nausea or vomiting that doesn't settle within 24 hours.
  • Severe abdominal pain, especially radiating to the back — needs pancreatitis ruled out.
  • Gallbladder-area or right-upper-quadrant pain after fatty meals.
  • Sustained loss above 3 lbs/week — the deficit is too aggressive; eat more.
  • Lightheadedness on standing — usually blood sugar or dehydration. Hydrate, eat, monitor.
