Storage & Handling
Refrigerated · One Vial at a Time

Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — open the next only when the current one runs dry.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites. For Wolverine, keep the shots grouped toward the area you're targeting.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Each Cycle
Tip 01 — Wolverine (New)
Inject Toward the Site · 20 Days Straight
One shot a day, 10u (1 mg), for 20 days in a row. Inject SubQ toward the area you're targeting — BPC-157 does its best repair work locally, so the closer to the injury site, the better. Rotate the exact spot around the area day to day.
  • Two vials cover the sprint — each vial is 10 daily doses; the other two are backup or a second round.
  • Mix one vial at a time. Open vial 2 only when vial 1 runs dry.
  • Miss a day? Resume the next day — don't double up.
Tip 02 — Injection Basics
SubQ, Short Needle, Barely Noticeable
Everything is SubQ with a short, thin insulin needle. Wash hands, wipe the stopper and skin with alcohol, pull to the right unit mark, pinch an inch of skin, insert at 45°, push slowly, drop the syringe in the sharps container. Rotate sites.
  • Draw marks (active): Wolverine 10u, Reta 10u, SS-31 10u, KPV 20u, MOTS-C 20u (10 mg vial) or 10u (40 mg vial). Glance at the line every shot.
Tip 03 — Reta Dose Ladder
Start Low, Titrate If Needed
Reta starts at 1 mg (10u) M/W/F — a conservative entry to build GI tolerance. If response stalls after a few weeks and you're tolerating it well, the next steps are 1.5–2 mg. Check in before climbing. Hydrate well (3–4 L/day) — Reta blunts thirst.
