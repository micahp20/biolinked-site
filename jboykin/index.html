Jonah's Wrist Recovery Protocol
Two compounds, run together — one in the morning, one at night.
The idea
Both compounds are soft-tissue repair, working the wrist from two angles. Wolverine 2.0 is the three-peptide repair blend; GHK-Cu is the collagen and remodelling half.
The rhythm
Monday through Friday. Wolverine 2.0 in the morning, GHK-Cu before bed. 10 units each, both subcutaneous. Weekends off.
Targeting the wrist
A SubQ shot in the abdomen works body-wide. You can also place either shot SubQ near the wrist to concentrate it on the injury.

Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Note 01 — The Routine
One Morning Shot, One Before Bed
  • Wolverine 2.0 10u in the morning, GHK-Cu 10u before bed. Monday through Friday, weekends off.
  • Neither needs to be fasted. Pick a time for each and keep it there — consistency matters more than the exact hour.
  • Pinch an inch of skin, 45–90 degrees, push slow. Abdomen is the simplest site; rotate quadrants so you are never in the same one-inch spot twice in a day.
  • For the wrist specifically, either shot can go SubQ near the joint instead of the abdomen to concentrate it locally.
  • Missed a day? Skip it. Back to the normal schedule the next day — never double up to catch up.
Note 02 — Mixing & Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
  • Dry vials: refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
  • Mixing: 2 mL of bacteriostatic water into the Wolverine 2.0, 3 mL into the GHK-Cu. Alcohol-wipe both stoppers first and let them air dry.
  • Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Then swirl gently or roll it between your palms until clear. Never shake.
  • Label both vials with the compound, the 10u draw mark and the date you mixed them. GHK-Cu mixes to a deep blue — that colour is normal.
  • Keep mixed vials refrigerated, back shelf rather than the door.
  • Fresh single-use syringe every shot — for the draw and the injection both. A dull needle is what makes an injection hurt.
Note 03 — Week-by-Week
What To Expect
  • Weeks 1–2 — nothing dramatic. Repair peptides work underneath; the first thing most people notice is that the wrist is quieter at the end of the day.
  • Weeks 3–4 — range of motion and the ache after loading it are usually where the change shows up first.
  • Beyond that — keep loading the wrist sensibly as it comes back. The peptides support the repair; the rehab work is what directs it.
Note 04 — Baseline Labs
Worth Getting Drawn
  • CBC with differential — the basic blood count.
  • Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) — kidney, liver, electrolytes, glucose.
  • Vitamin D, 25-Hydroxy — commonly low, cheap to check, and it matters for tissue healing.
  • Ferritin and CRP — iron stores and a general inflammation marker.
