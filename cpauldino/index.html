Personalized Protocol
MamaP's Foundation + Short Cycle
Three ongoing compounds, plus a 20–30 day anti-aging, sleep and stress cycle split AM / PM.
Three layers, one routine
Your stack runs as an ongoing daily foundation — HGH and GHK-Cu — with two blocks layered on top: an 8-week oral run of Tesofine and SLU-PP-332, Monday–Friday, and a 20–30 day peptide cycle of Cortagen and Cerebrolysin in the AM, Epithalon and Pinealon in the PM. The foundation keeps going; the two blocks each finish on their own clock.
How the days are built
One short AM window holds HGH, GHK-Cu, the two AM cycle peptides, and — on weekdays, fasted, before you eat — the Tesofine capsule and two SLU-PP-332 tablets. The PM window is the peptide pair only, Epithalon and Pinealon, taken in the evening. Both orals are Monday–Friday; the injectables run daily while they are in play.
What changed
HGH stays, on new dosing — a 40 iu vial reconstituted with 1 mL, 10 units (4 iu) per dose. Everything from the prior protocol has moved into Prior Protocol · Reference at the bottom of this tab — still here to look back at, just not part of the current routine.

Anti-Aging · Sleep & Stress
A short, finite block. These four run as one 20–30 day course layered on the daily foundation, then stop — bioregulator peptides are dosed as courses rather than continuously. Protocol Overview — Foundation + 8-Week Orals + Short Cycle
Daily Foundation — HGH · GHK-Cu
HGH (4 iu / 10u, every day AM) as the recovery and body-composition baseline, and GHK-Cu (~1.67 mg / 10u, daily AM) for skin, collagen and tissue repair. These two keep running after everything else has finished.
8-Week Oral Run — Tesofine + SLU-PP-332
Tesofine 300 mcg (1 capsule) and SLU-PP-332 1000 mcg (2 × 500 mcg tablets), both Monday–Friday in the fasted morning slot, both for 8 weeks then done. Tesofine works the appetite side, SLU-PP-332 the mitochondrial and fat-oxidation side. 40 capsules and 80 tablets for the full run.
Short Cycle AM — Cortagen + Cerebrolysin
Cortagen (2 mg / 20u) and Cerebrolysin (5 mg / ~16.7u) go in with the morning foundation shots. Two vials each — ~20 days of Cortagen, ~24 days of Cerebrolysin. These two set the length of the cycle.
Short Cycle PM — Epithalon + Pinealon
Epithalon (10 mg / 40u, 2 vials) and Pinealon (2 mg / 20u, 1 vial) are the evening pair, both landing at ~10 doses. They cover the opening stretch of the cycle and finish before the AM pair does — when they run out, the evening shot simply stops.
How the routine flows: one morning window carries HGH, GHK-Cu, the Tesofine capsule and — while the cycle is running — Cortagen and Cerebrolysin. One evening window carries Epithalon and Pinealon for the first ~10 days. Once the short cycle finishes you are back to three compounds in a single AM window, with Tesofine paused on weekends.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Rotated
Dry (lyophilized) vials
refrigerated at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Reconstituted vials
refrigerate immediately. Use within 30 days. Fresh insulin syringe for each draw — never reuse. Label each vial with the date you mixed it; with six going at once the dates are what keep them straight.
Site rotation
SubQ abdomen rotates between 4 quadrants. Never inject the same 1″ spot two days in a row. The AM window is four shots — spread them across different quadrants (e.g., HGH upper-right, GHK-Cu upper-left, Cortagen lower-right, Cerebrolysin lower-left).

Tip 02 — The Daily Rhythm
One AM Window · One PM Window
Seven compounds, but only two touchpoints in the day:
  • AM (after waking, fasted): HGH 10u + GHK-Cu 10u + Cortagen 20u + Cerebrolysin ~16.7u, plus — Monday–Friday, before food — the Tesofine capsule and two SLU-PP-332 tablets. Four shots spread across four abdomen quadrants.
  • PM (evening): Epithalon 40u + Pinealon 20u, for the first ~10 days until those vials finish.
  • Weekends: identical, minus both orals.
  • After everything finishes: the orals stop at week 8, the evening shot goes away when its vials run out, and the AM window drops back to HGH + GHK-Cu.
Tip 03 — Why the Split Is AM / PM
Morning Drive, Evening Repair
The two windows aren't arbitrary — each compound sits where its effect is useful rather than costly.
  • Tesofine is stimulating, so it belongs in the morning. Taken late it is the single item most likely to cost you sleep, which would undo the evening half of the protocol.
  • SLU-PP-332 wants an empty stomach, which is the other reason both orals sit in the same fasted slot before you eat — food in the stomach is what blunts it.
  • Cerebrolysin is alerting, which makes it a morning compound for the same reason.
  • Epithalon works on melatonin rhythm, so an evening dose lands with the body's own night signal rather than against it.
  • Pinealon pairs with it in the PM block so the whole sleep-and-recovery side of the cycle sits in one evening window.
Tip 04 — Reading the Odd Doses
16.7 Units and the Blue Vial
  • Cerebrolysin at ~16.7 units sits between the 16 and 17 marks. Draw to just past 16 — the gap is six thousandths of a millilitre, so either line is fine.
  • GHK-Cu reconstitutes blue. It is a copper peptide, so a clear blue solution is exactly right and not a sign anything is wrong.
  • Cortagen and Pinealon share a dose — both 20 units at 10 mg/mL. Different vials, same draw, so label them clearly.
  • SLU-PP-332 is two tablets, not one — the 1000 mcg dose comes from a pair of 500 mcg tablets.
  • Epithalon is the big one at 40 units (0.40 mL). A larger volume than the rest — push it at a comfortable pace.
Tip 05 — SubQ Injection 101
The 60-Second Routine
  • Wash hands. Pull a fresh syringe + alcohol pad.
  • Wipe the vial top with alcohol. Let it dry 5 sec.
  • Draw your dose to the right unit mark.
  • Pick an abdomen spot, ~2″ from the belly button. Wipe with alcohol.
  • Pinch a small fold, insert the needle at 45–90°.
  • Push slowly, pull out, light pressure if any bleeding.
  • Drop the syringe in the sharps container. Done.
With multiple shots in one window, use a different quadrant for each so no single spot gets repeated.
