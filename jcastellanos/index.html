Client Protocol
Jaron's 6-Compound Stack
Build strength, recover faster, think clearer — on a schedule that doesn't always cooperate.
Where this starts
You said it plainly: not a shortcut, but support building strength, improving recovery and sharpening cognition while you learn to take better care of yourself. Between short sleep, demanding work and a very active week, the gap isn't effort — it's recovery time. This stack is built to widen that gap in your favor, not to replace the work.
The direction
180 → 190 lb. This is a gain protocol. Muscle is the priority; leaning out is the side effect, not the target. Every dosing decision here is made in that order — including holding Retatrutide low on purpose.
The stack
CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin and KLOW are the build-and-repair layer — GH axis, sleep, and the connective tissue that takes the beating. MOTS-C, NAD+ and Semax are the energy-and-cognition layer. Retatrutide is metabolic support, held at a deliberately small dose.
Cadence
5 days on, 2 off. Mornings are fasted (MOTS-C daily, NAD+ Mon / Wed / Fri, Reta on Monday). Evenings are KLOW and CJC/Ipa, fasted, before bed. Semax is as-needed on the mornings you need the focus. Saturday and Sunday are fully off.
Length
Approximately 8 weeks — not a fixed calendar. MOTS-C and CJC/Ipa are supplied for the full run; KLOW carries roughly five weeks. Run the bottles out rather than watching a date.
Shots per week
19 — 5 MOTS-C, 5 KLOW, 5 CJC/Ipa, 3 NAD+, 1 Reta, plus Semax as needed. Four draw marks total: 10u, 15u, 25u, and ~7u for Semax.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time — which is exactly why you mix one at a time.
Reconstitution
MOTS-C 3 mL, NAD+ 5 mL, Retatrutide 3 mL, CJC/Ipa 2 mL, KLOW 2 mL, Semax 2 mL. Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial, swirl gently, never shake.
Mix one at a time
You have eleven vials but six compounds. Open a second vial only as the first empties — around week 4 for MOTS-C and CJC/Ipa, week 3 for KLOW. The second Retatrutide vial stays dry the whole cycle, and NAD+ is on a monthly rhythm, not a volume one.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on each vial — MOTS-C 15u, NAD+ 25u, Reta 10u, CJC/Ipa 10u, KLOW 15u, Semax 7u. Six vials in a fridge door look identical at 6am, and two of them share the same mark.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days. If a vial has been open longer than that with doses left in it, that is the shelf life talking — not a reason to double up.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Notes & Tips
How To Run It Well
Start Here
Your Two Daily Windows
  • AM, fasted, before breakfast. MOTS-C 15u every weekday; add NAD+ 25u on Mon / Wed / Fri and Retatrutide 10u on Monday. Different quadrant for each, then wait 30–45 min before food.
  • PM, fasted, before bed. KLOW 15u and CJC/Ipa 10u, at least 2 hours after your last food and 30–60 min before lights out.
  • Semax is separate. ~7u in the morning on the days you actually need the focus — not a daily habit.
  • Weekends off. Saturday and Sunday are built in as rest days — nothing to catch up on Monday.
  • Missed a dose? Skip it and carry on. Never double up. The one exception worth planning around is Retatrutide — if Monday gets away from you, take it Tuesday and go back to Mondays the following week.
The Whole Point
Eat Like You're Trying To Reach 190
  • This stack cannot build tissue out of nothing. CJC/Ipa raises the ceiling on what your body will do with food and training; it does not replace either. On a 180→190 goal, a consistent calorie surplus with real protein is the single highest-leverage thing on this page.
  • You flagged inconsistent eating as the problem. That is the honest constraint here. The peptides are the easy part — they take four minutes a day. Getting food in on the chaotic days is the hard part, and it is where the results actually come from.
  • Retatrutide is held at 1 mg for exactly this reason. It is doing metabolic and blood-sugar work, not appetite work. If you ever notice it genuinely blunting your hunger, tell Micah — the answer is to come down, not up.
  • Keep the creatine going. It pairs cleanly with everything here and there is no interaction to think about. Your multivitamin is fine alongside all of it too.
NAD+
Push It Slowly — And It's Adjustable
  • This is the one you'll feel. NAD+ commonly causes flushing, a warm wave, or chest / gut pressure going in. It is dose- and speed-dependent, not dangerous.
  • Thirty to sixty seconds on the plunger is the single biggest thing that keeps it comfortable. Rushing it is what makes it sting.
  • 50 mg (25u) is a starting dose, not a fixed one. If it is comfortable after a couple of weeks you can step to 75 mg (37.5u). If 25u is rough, drop to 12.5u for a few doses and build back up. Tell Micah either way.
  • Hydrate. It goes down noticeably easier when you are not underhydrated — worth knowing on 300–500 mg of caffeine a day.
The Evening Shot
Where CJC/Ipa Gets Paid
  • Food blunts it. CJC/Ipa needs 2+ hours after your last food. If you ate late, skip that night rather than dosing it — a skipped dose costs you nothing, a blunted one is just wasted.
  • Then go to bed. The shot amplifies your overnight GH pulse; getting into bed 30–60 min after it, consistently, is what converts that into muscle and recovery. With sleep at a self-rated 3/5, this is the compound most worth protecting.
  • Alcohol works against this specific compound — it flattens the same overnight pulse. One or two drinks a week is a non-issue; just try not to stack them onto a dosing night.
  • Watch the late caffeine. Nothing in this stack interacts with caffeine chemically, but 300–500 mg a day taken too late undercuts the exact window CJC/Ipa is aiming at. Cutting it off by early afternoon is the cheapest upgrade available to you.
Timeline
What To Expect, Roughly
  • Weeks 1–2 — the routine is the job. Two windows a day, six vials, four marks. Expect the first few mornings to feel fussy and then stop being a thing. Sleep is usually the first shift once CJC/Ipa is in. Semax works from day one on the days you use it.
  • Weeks 3–4 — energy and recovery. MOTS-C and NAD+ tend to land here as steadier days rather than a hit — the tell is that a short-sleep morning costs you less than it used to. KLOW starts quietly closing out the small nagging stuff.
  • Weeks 5–6 — the build shows up. This is where the GH axis and the food start compounding: better session-to-session recovery, more volume tolerated, and the scale beginning to move in the right direction. KLOW's two vials run out around here.
  • Weeks 7–8 — assess. MOTS-C and CJC/Ipa finish out. Weigh in, look at where 190 actually sits, and decide with Micah what the next cycle keeps. Reta and NAD+ will still have dry vials left — they carry forward.
  • Anything that doesn't settle — a persistent injection-site reaction, NAD+ discomfort that worsens across doses rather than easing, or anything that just feels wrong — stop that compound and text Micah.
