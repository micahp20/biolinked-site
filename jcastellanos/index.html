Where this starts
Your words, and they set the whole brief: this is not about appearance — it is about how your body operates. You work a lot, you need to perform at a high level mentally and physically, and efficiency is the thing you actually value. So that is the through-line. Nothing here is a shortcut; every compound is chosen to give you more usable output per day — sharper, stronger, more recovered — not to replace the work you are already doing.
The direction
180 → 190 lb. This is a gain protocol — muscle and strength are the priority, leaning out is the side effect. But the target behind the target is function: keeping up with the demands of your work and your week without running yourself down to do it. Every dosing decision here is made in that order, including holding Retatrutide low on purpose.
What we're working with
5–6 hours of sleep, two larger protein-focused meals a day, a lot of water, a few drinks across the week, training 3–4x. The water and the protein are already right — that part you do not need help with. The constraint is recovery time: you are asking your body to build on five hours. That is why the GH-axis and cellular-energy pieces carry as much weight in this stack as anything aimed at the gym.
The stack
CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin and KLOW are the build-and-repair layer — GH axis, sleep, and the connective tissue that takes the beating. MOTS-C, NAD+ and Semax are the energy-and-cognition layer. Retatrutide is metabolic support, held at a deliberately small dose.
Cadence
5 days on, 2 off. Mornings are fasted (MOTS-C daily, NAD+ Mon / Wed / Fri, Reta on Monday). Evenings are KLOW and CJC/Ipa, fasted, before bed. Semax is as-needed on the mornings you need the focus. Saturday and Sunday are fully off.

Nutrition
Fueling 180 → 190
The frame

This is the half of the protocol the peptides cannot do for you. CJC/Ipa raises the ceiling on what your body will do with food and training — it does not supply the food. A gain needs a calorie surplus, and yours has to land inside a two-meal day, which is the actual difficulty. So the plan below keeps the two big meals you like and adds two easy feedings around them rather than asking you to become a five-meal person. Everything here is a starting point to tune against the scale, not a prescription.

Meal Structure
Two Big Meals + Two Easy Additions
Keep
Your two larger meals, protein-forward, exactly as you eat now. Big meals are an advantage on a gain — most people struggle to eat enough in one sitting and you do not.
Add
A shake and one around-training feeding. Neither is a meal you have to sit down for, and together they carry about a third of the day's calories. This is the whole trick: two meals cannot realistically hold 3,100 calories, but two meals plus two liquids-and-snacks can.
On rest days
The around-training feeding just becomes an afternoon snack. Do not drop it. You only train 3–4x a week, so the surplus has to be there on the other days too — that is where a lot of gains quietly fail.

How To Run It
Four Things That Decide The Outcome
The Real Lever
Adding Calories Without Adding Meals
  • Liquids do not fill you up the way food does. This is the single most useful fact for someone eating twice a day — the shake carries ~700 calories that would otherwise need a third sit-down meal.
  • Upgrade what is already on the plate rather than adding to it: whole milk instead of skim, a tablespoon of olive oil over the rice and vegetables, granola instead of plain yogurt, nut butter into the shake. Each of those is 100–150 calories that costs you no extra chewing.
  • One tablespoon of oil is ~120 calories. If the scale stalls, this is the easiest 250 you will ever add — two tablespoons across the day and you are done.
  • Protein per feeding, roughly 40–55 g. You are already protein-forward, so this is about spreading it rather than raising it — four feedings at that level lands the daily target without thinking about it.
  • Watch the caffeine on the appetite side. 300–500 mg a day genuinely blunts hunger. If you are struggling to eat, the coffee before Meal 1 is a likelier culprit than your appetite.
Training Days
Around Your 3–4 Sessions
  • Protein and carbs within a couple of hours either side of the session. The window is far wider than the internet claims — getting the food in at all matters much more than hitting a 30-minute mark. The around-training feeding exists to make that automatic.
  • Do not train fasted. On 5–6 hours of sleep and a demanding workday, training on nothing is where sessions quietly get worse. If a session lands before Meal 1, move the around-training feeding in front of it.
  • Creatine: keep doing exactly what you are doing. 5 g a day, timing irrelevant, consistency is the whole thing. It is the best-evidenced supplement you own and it pairs cleanly with everything in this stack.
  • Alcohol has a real cost on this specific goal. A few drinks across the week is fine in the big picture, but alcohol suppresses protein synthesis for the better part of a day and flattens the overnight GH pulse. If you can keep it off training days and CJC/Ipa nights, that is the cheapest recovery upgrade available.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Scale, Not To The Plan
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone and will drive you crazy otherwise.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? Add about 250 calories a day — a second tablespoon of oil and a bigger scoop of oats in the shake covers it. Reassess in another two weeks.
  • Gaining faster than ~1.5 lb a week? Pull back about 250. That pace is putting on more fat than you want given you care how it looks too.
  • Strength is the better signal. If the weights are moving up and the scale is creeping, it is working. If the scale is moving and the weights are not, you are eating well but under-recovering — look at sleep before you look at food.
Expectations
What 10 lb Actually Looks Like
  • The first 2–4 lb will come fast and will not be muscle. Creatine and a higher carb intake pull water into the muscle — that is real, it makes you look and perform better, but it is not tissue. Do not read it as being a third of the way there.
  • Real tissue arrives at roughly 0.5–1 lb a week at 21 with training and food both in place. That puts 190 somewhere in the 10–20 week range.
  • The peptide cycle is 8 weeks; the goal is longer than that. Expect to be meaningfully stronger and somewhere in the mid-180s when the vials run out, and to decide with Micah what carries forward from there.
  • If you are eating this and not gaining, you are not eating this. Not a criticism — it is just the most common outcome on a two-meal schedule. Track the food honestly for one week before changing anything else.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time — which is exactly why you mix one at a time.
Reconstitution
MOTS-C 3 mL, NAD+ 5 mL, Retatrutide 3 mL, CJC/Ipa 2 mL, KLOW 2 mL, Semax 3 mL. Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial, swirl gently, never shake.
Mix one at a time
You have eleven vials but six compounds. Open a second vial only as the first empties — around week 4 for MOTS-C and CJC/Ipa, week 3 for KLOW. The second Retatrutide vial stays dry the whole cycle, and NAD+ is on a monthly rhythm, not a volume one.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on each vial — MOTS-C 15u, NAD+ 25u, Reta 10u, CJC/Ipa 10u, KLOW 15u, Semax 10u. Six vials in a fridge door look identical at 6am, and three of them share the 10u mark.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days. If a vial has been open longer than that with doses left in it, that is the shelf life talking — not a reason to double up.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.

How To Run It Well
Start Here
Your Two Daily Windows
  • AM, fasted, before breakfast. MOTS-C 15u every weekday; add NAD+ 25u on Mon / Wed / Fri and Retatrutide 10u on Monday. Different quadrant for each, then wait 30–45 min before food.
  • PM, fasted, before bed. KLOW 15u and CJC/Ipa 10u, at least 2 hours after your last food and 30–60 min before lights out.

  • Missed a dose? Skip it and carry on. Never double up. The one exception worth planning around is Retatrutide — if Monday gets away from you, take it Tuesday and go back to Mondays the following week.
The Whole Point
Eat Like You're Trying To Reach 190
  • This stack cannot build tissue out of nothing. CJC/Ipa raises the ceiling on what your body will do with food and training; it does not replace either. On a 180→190 goal, a consistent calorie surplus with real protein is the single highest-leverage thing on this page.
  • Two larger meals a day, protein prioritized — the quality is right, the question is whether the quantity clears a surplus. Two meals is a narrow window to land a gaining calorie count in, so the honest fix is making both meals bigger, or adding a third feeding around training. The peptides take four minutes a day; this is the part that decides the outcome.
  • Retatrutide is held at 1 mg for exactly this reason. It is doing metabolic and blood-sugar work, not appetite work. If you ever notice it genuinely blunting your hunger, tell Micah — the answer is to come down, not up.
  • Keep the creatine going. It pairs cleanly with everything here and there is no interaction to think about. Your multivitamin is fine alongside all of it too.
NAD+
Push It Slowly — And It's Adjustable
  • This is the one you'll feel. NAD+ commonly causes flushing, a warm wave, or chest / gut pressure going in. It is dose- and speed-dependent, not dangerous.
  • Thirty to sixty seconds on the plunger is the single biggest thing that keeps it comfortable. Rushing it is what makes it sting.
  • 50 mg (25u) is a starting dose, not a fixed one. If it is comfortable after a couple of weeks you can step to 75 mg (37.5u). If 25u is rough, drop to 12.5u for a few doses and build back up. Tell Micah either way.
  • Hydrate. It goes down noticeably easier when you are not underhydrated — worth knowing on 300–500 mg of caffeine a day.
The Evening Shot
Where CJC/Ipa Gets Paid
  • Food blunts it. CJC/Ipa needs 2+ hours after your last food. If you ate late, skip that night rather than dosing it — a skipped dose costs you nothing, a blunted one is just wasted.
  • Then go to bed. The shot amplifies your overnight GH pulse; getting into bed 30–60 min after it, consistently, is what converts that into muscle and recovery. At 5–6 hours a night this is the compound most worth protecting — it is how you get more out of a night you cannot make longer.
  • Alcohol works against this specific compound — it flattens the same overnight GH pulse you are paying to amplify. A few drinks across the week is not a problem in itself; on 5–6 hours of sleep the thing to avoid is stacking them onto a dosing night, because that night is already the narrowest one you have.
  • Watch the late caffeine. Nothing in this stack interacts with caffeine chemically, but 300–500 mg a day taken too late undercuts the exact window CJC/Ipa is aiming at. Cutting it off by early afternoon is the cheapest upgrade available to you.
Timeline
What To Expect, Roughly
  • Weeks 1–2 — the routine is the job. Two windows a day, six vials, three marks. Expect the first few mornings to feel fussy and then stop being a thing. Sleep is usually the first shift once CJC/Ipa is in. Semax works from day one on the days you use it.
  • Weeks 3–4 — energy and recovery. MOTS-C and NAD+ tend to land here as steadier days rather than a hit — the tell is that a short-sleep morning costs you less than it used to. KLOW starts quietly closing out the small nagging stuff.
  • Weeks 5–6 — the build shows up. This is where the GH axis and the food start compounding: better session-to-session recovery, more volume tolerated, and the scale beginning to move in the right direction. KLOW's two vials run out around here.
  • Weeks 7–8 — assess. MOTS-C and CJC/Ipa finish out. Weigh in, look at where 190 actually sits, and decide with Micah what the next cycle keeps. Reta and NAD+ will still have dry vials left — they carry forward.
  • Anything that doesn't settle — a persistent injection-site reaction, NAD+ discomfort that worsens across doses rather than easing, or anything that just feels wrong — stop that compound and text Micah.
