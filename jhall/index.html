Personalized Protocol
Jarod's Twelve-Month Protocol
A year-round foundation that never pauses, and six eight-week cycles rotating underneath it.
What you asked for
Cognitive first, with 190 to 160 running underneath it. At 27 and 5'7" that is a thirty-pound cut with room to do it properly — slowly enough to keep the lean tissue, which is what the foundation is for.
You have done this before
Multiple prior cycles, including BPC-157 and NAD+, so the needles are not the hurdle here and both of those return in this year. That is why the doses open where they do rather than at a beginner's floor.
Sleep and the rest of the picture
Sleep sits at 3 out of 5 on about six hours, stress is low, and alcohol runs three to seven a week. The evening compounds are all fasted and pre-bed, which is the window those three facts share.
Run until the bottles are finished
Every vial on this page runs to empty. Nothing is thrown out on a 30-day clock — a cycle ends when its bottles do, and leftovers carry into the next block that uses the same compound.

The rules that decide whether this works

The PM block is fasted — roughly two hours after your last meal. Food, carbohydrate especially, blunts the growth-hormone pulse that CJC/Ipamorelin and Tesamorelin exist to create, and a shot taken twenty minutes after dinner is mostly wasted. Retatrutide is Monday morning, every week, and Testosterone is Monday and Thursday.

If you miss a dose

Take it when you remember unless the next one is close, then skip it — never double up. A missed Retatrutide Monday gets taken as soon as you notice; if it has been more than two days, skip that week and resume the ladder where it was rather than stepping up.
