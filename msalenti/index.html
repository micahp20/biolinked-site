Schedule

Week 1: Record baseline weight, usual meals, activity and sleep. Schedule a primary-care review.

Weeks 2–4: Track the weekly weight trend, appetite, activity and sleep. At week 4, review what feels sustainable and choose one adjustment if needed.

Weeks 5–8: Maintain the routine and review progress at week 8. Use the trend and Mario’s feedback to plan the next block.

Nutrition

Build repeatable meals around a protein source and fiber-rich foods such as beans, vegetables, fruit or whole grains. Track usual portions for the first week, then choose one change that is easy to maintain. Use regular grocery foods to keep costs down.

Workout

Start with a 10–15-minute walk on most days if comfortable. Gradually build time and add two short strength sessions per week as tolerated. Keep a consistent sleep schedule and note how sleep and stress affect appetite and recovery.
