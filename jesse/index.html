NAD+ is capped by its mixed shelf life, not by volume. The vial holds 20 doses at 25u, but reconstituted peptide keeps about 30 days — at three doses a week that's roughly 13 doses before the window closes. If you titrate up to 75 mg (37.5u) after the first couple of weeks, the vial empties right about when its 30 days are up. KLOW runs ~4 weeks and CJC/Ipa ~8, so if you want the repair and cellular layers to run the full eight, plan a second KLOW and NAD+ around week 4.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time, which is why there's no rush to mix everything at once.
Reconstitution
KLOW 3 mL, NAD+ 5 mL, CJC/Ipa 2 mL per vial. Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial, swirl gently, never shake.
Mix one at a time
You have four vials but three compounds — open the second CJC/Ipa only as the first one empties, around week 4.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on each vial — KLOW 15u, NAD+ 25u, CJC/Ipa 10u. Three vials in a fridge door look identical at 6am.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Notes & Tips
How To Run It Well
Start Here
Your Two Daily Windows
  • AM, fasted, before breakfast. KLOW 15u every weekday; add NAD+ 25u on Mon / Wed / Fri. Different quadrant for each, then wait 30–45 min before food.
  • PM, fasted, before bed. CJC/Ipa 10u, at least 2 hours after your last food and 30–60 min before lights out.
  • Weekends off. Saturday and Sunday are built in as rest days — nothing to catch up on Monday.
  • Missed a dose? Skip it and carry on. Never double up.
NAD+
Push It Slowly — And It's Adjustable
  • This is the one you'll feel. NAD+ commonly causes flushing, a warm wave, or chest / gut pressure going in. It's dose- and speed-dependent, not dangerous.
  • Thirty to sixty seconds on the plunger is the single biggest thing that keeps it comfortable. Rushing it is what makes it sting.
  • 50 mg (25u) is a starting dose, not a fixed one. If it's comfortable after a couple of weeks you can step to 75 mg (37.5u) — the half-mark between 37 and 38 on a 100-unit syringe. If 25u is rough, drop to 12.5u for a few doses and build back up. Tell Micah either way.
  • Hydrate. It goes down noticeably easier when you're not underhydrated.
The Evening Shot
Where CJC/Ipa Gets Paid
  • Food blunts it. CJC/Ipa needs 2+ hours after your last food. If you ate late, skip that night rather than dosing it — a skipped dose costs you nothing, a blunted one is just wasted.
  • Then go to bed. The shot amplifies your overnight GH pulse; getting into bed 30–60 min after it, consistently, is what converts that into recovery.
  • Alcohol works against this specific compound — it flattens the same overnight pulse. Not a lecture, just where the trade-off sits.
Timeline
What To Expect, Roughly
  • Weeks 1–2. Getting comfortable with the routine is the whole job. Sleep is usually the first thing to shift once CJC/Ipa is in.
  • Weeks 3–4. KLOW starts showing up. It rarely arrives as “that's healed” — it's more that you get through something without thinking about it. NAD+ tends to land as steadier energy and clearer mornings rather than a hit.
  • Weeks 5–8. CJC/Ipa continues on its second vial. This is where recovery between sessions and lean-mass response tend to be most obvious.
  • Anything that doesn't settle — persistent injection-site reaction, NAD+ discomfort that worsens across doses rather than easing, or anything that just feels wrong — stop that compound and text Micah.
