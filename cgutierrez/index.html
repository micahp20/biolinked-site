Christina's Three-Cycle Protocol
One goal per cycle, run back to back with no overlap. Fat loss first, because that is how you ranked it.
Your goals
Fat loss first, then longevity and anti-aging, then hormonal balance. Each one gets its own cycle, so you can always tell which compound is responsible for what you are feeling.
You have run GLP-1s
Prior semaglutide and tirzepatide, and you are comfortable injecting. Nothing here opens at a beginner dose — the Retatrutide ladder climbs a milligram a week to a 6 mg ceiling, and everything else runs at its normal working range from the first shot.
The eating side is already handled
Keto or low-carb, no medications, under PCP care. The two levers with real room in them are protein and resistance training — light exercise once or twice a week is not much of a defence for lean mass in a deficit. Both are on the Nutrition tab.
Cycle lengths are set by the vials
Cycle 1 is eight weeks — what two Retatrutide, four Tesamorelin and two MOTS-c vials cover. Cycle 2 is three weeks, a 20-day bioregulator block plus a single senolytic pulse. Cycle 3 is eight weeks, two vials of each.

Nutrition
Keto, Held Steady · 120 → 105
The frame

You are already eating the right way, so this is precision rather than change. Three things decide the outcome: a deficit small enough to keep training through, protein high enough that what comes off is fat, and enough resistance work to hold the muscle you have.

Meal Structure
Three Meals + One Snack · Protein First
Anchor
A protein-forward breakfast, every day — it sets the day's total up to be hit rather than chased at 9pm. On Monday, wait 30–45 minutes after the fasted shots before eating.
Finish eating early
Protocol-driven, not diet-driven. Tesamorelin needs two hours clear of food five nights a week, so dinner wants to be done by around 7pm if you dose at 9 or 10.
Resistance work
The biggest gap on your intake. Add two resistance sessions a week — 30 minutes is enough. In a deficit at 48, this is what tells the body to keep the muscle instead of spending it.
The snack
Between lunch and dinner. Decide what it is in advance: cottage cheese, hard-boiled eggs, almonds with jerky, a keto shake. Each is 20–30 g of protein and takes no cooking.
Alcohol
One or two a week is already fine — no change needed. Just keep it away from the three hours before bed; six hours of sleep has no room to lose any.

The Real Lever
Protein Is The Only Thing You Have To Defend
  • If a day falls apart, hit the protein and let the rest go. At 120 lb there is very little lean mass to spare.
  • Front-load it. 30 g at breakfast means a quarter of the day is done before anything else happens.
  • A shake is a legitimate meal on a Retatrutide day. When appetite is genuinely gone, 30 g in a glass beats skipping the feeding.
  • Weigh it for the first two weeks. Long enough to learn what 4 oz looks like — most people are 30–40% under when they guess.
Measuring
Use The Weekly Average, Not The Day
  • Waist at the navel in Week 1, same time of day, before food. Tesamorelin moves that number first.
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions, and read the weekly average. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone.
  • Flat average and flat waist after three full weeks? Add 1,500 steps a day first, then take about 150 calories off the fat side. Do not cut protein, and do not accelerate the Reta ladder to fix a plateau.
  • Losing faster than 1 lb a week? Add 150–200 calories back. At your bodyweight, faster than that is water and muscle.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps for a long time, which is why later cycles simply wait.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days. Two vials hold far more than 30 days of doses and get swapped on the calendar rather than on what is left in them: the Oxytocin and the PT-141.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix. Kisspeptin and PT-141 look identical in a fridge door and land in the same cycle — that is the pair worth labelling carefully. On the Reta, write the week as well, since the rung changes every Monday.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, let the alcohol dry, rotate sites. Where two draws share a morning, use opposite quadrants.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01 — The On-Ramp
Nothing Starts On The Same Day As Everything Else
  • Wk 1 — Cycle 1. Retatrutide Monday morning, MOTS-c on the weekday mornings, Tesamorelin at night. Where two draws share a morning, use opposite quadrants of the abdomen so the day stays readable.
  • Wk 9 — Cycle 2. Cortagen in the morning, Epitalon and Pinealon at night, and the FOXO4-DRI pulse that same week. The 20-day block has to run consecutively from day one, so this cycle starts all at once — but all of it is gentle.
  • Wk 12 — Cycle 3. Kisspeptin and Oxytocin first; PT-141 waits until Wk 14, because it is the only one with an acute effect. All three swap to a second vial on the Monday of Wk 16.
Note 02 — Retatrutide
The Ladder, And Where To Get Off It
  • One shot a week, Monday morning, up a milligram each week to 6 mg, then hold. 10u, 20u, 30u, 40u, 50u, 60u — the vial is mixed at 10 mg/mL so the ladder reads straight off the barrel.
  • The ceiling is a maximum, not a destination. If the waist is moving and appetite is controlled at 3 mg, stop climbing and hold there.
  • Mild nausea in the 24–48 hours after a dose is the known profile, loudest in the first two weeks and after each step up. Lighter, lower-fat meals on Monday and Tuesday make a real difference.
  • If a rung is not tolerable, go back one and stay there. Eight weeks at 4 mg beats five weeks at 6 mg followed by three weeks of nothing.
  • Constipation is the other common one and it is mostly a water and fibre problem — the 80–90 oz target and the vegetables on the Nutrition tab are the fix.
  • Never double a missed dose. If a Monday is missed, take it within 24 hours; past that, skip it and resume the following Monday at the same rung.
Note 03 — Tesamorelin & MOTS-c
One Has A Food Rule, One Does Not
  • Tesamorelin: two hours clear of food, 30–60 minutes before bed, five weeknights. Food blunts the growth-hormone pulse it exists to amplify.
  • If you ate late, skip that night. A dose given on top of food is mostly wasted, and skipping one of forty is a rounding error.
  • Give Tesamorelin four weeks before judging it. Most people notice sleep depth first, in the second or third week, and the waist later. Mild water retention or tingling in the hands early on is the known GH-axis profile and usually settles.
  • MOTS-c is the simple one: weekday mornings, fasted, 15u. Nothing to time around beyond that. What people report is steadier energy rather than a lift — which is the point in a deficit.
Note 04 — Bioregulators & Senolytics
The Ones With Nothing To Feel
  • Epitalon, Pinealon and Cortagen are Khavinson-class bioregulators. They work at the gene-expression level on one specific tissue — pineal, neural and cortical respectively.
  • There is nothing to feel on the day, and that is not a failure. They are judged weeks after the course finishes, by whether a baseline moved.
  • Run them as blocks, not as habits. Twenty nights for Epitalon and Pinealon, twenty mornings for Cortagen, then stop dead. Do not extend a block because powder is left in a vial — the consecutive course is the mechanism.
  • FOXO4-DRI is the same logic, compressed. Three doses, then done. Mild fatigue or a flu-ish feeling in the 24 hours after each dose is the known senolytic profile and settles on its own.
Note 05 — Missed Doses
Skip It, Do Not Stack It
  • Missed the Monday Retatrutide? Take it within 24 hours, then go back to Mondays. Past that, skip and resume next Monday at the same rung. Never double it.
  • Missed a Tesamorelin night? Skip it — do not take it in the morning instead. The overnight pulse is the point.
  • Missed a MOTS-c morning? Take it any time before mid-afternoon; later than that, skip it. One gap changes nothing.
  • Missed a night of a 20-day block? Continue the count the next day and finish day 20 one day later. Do not take two in a day to catch up.
  • Missed a Kisspeptin, Oxytocin or PT-141? Skip it. None of the three has a loading effect that one gap undoes.
  • Missed a whole week? Pick the schedule back up where you left it — that cycle runs a week longer and everything after shifts by a week. On the Reta, resume at the rung you were on, not the one the calendar says.
Note 06 — Timeline
What Shows Up, And When
  • Wks 1–2 — appetite first. The Retatrutide effect lands on the first or second shot and is the earliest thing you will notice.
  • Wks 2–3 — sleep depth. The most common early Tesamorelin report, and a reasonable sign the food window is being respected.
  • Wks 3–8 — the waist, then the scale. Expect the tape measure and the way clothes fit to move ahead of the number.
  • Wks 9–11 — the quiet cycle. Almost nothing announces itself. The bioregulator read comes after the block, not during it.
  • Wks 12–19 — slow, then acute. Kisspeptin and Oxytocin accumulate across eight weeks; PT-141 is loudest on the earliest doses.
