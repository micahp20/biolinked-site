Santino's Fat-Loss Protocol
Retatrutide + SLU-PP-332 — drive the cut and burn like you trained for it.
The goal
keep the fat loss moving and do it in a way that lasts. Two compounds working the same target from different angles — all in one fasted morning window, no evening shots.
How they work together
Retatrutide is the engine — a triple agonist (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon). The GLP-1/GIP arms handle appetite; the glucagon arm is the difference-maker, raising how many calories you burn at rest so you lose fat and keep your energy up. SLU-PP-332 is an exercise-mimetic — an ERR agonist that switches on the same fat-oxidation and mitochondrial pathways as endurance training. It stacks real metabolic burn on top of the Reta deficit, which is exactly how you keep the loss going without cutting calories deeper.
Why it lasts if you do it right
Reta sits at a steady, conservative 3 mg a week so your body adapts instead of stalling and crashing; SLU-PP builds mitochondrial capacity that keeps your metabolism working even as you lean out; and keeping protein up + staying active means what you lose is fat, not muscle. Run both until the bottles finish, then we reassess how you're feeling and decide the next move.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
Your Reta vial
already in solution — keep it refrigerated from day one and protected from light. Nothing to mix.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — open a fresh SLU-PP vial only as the last one empties (each one lasts you 2 weeks).
Reconstitution amounts
SLU-PP-332 only — 2 mL per 5 mg vial. Add the water slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, don't shake.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Routine
One Fasted Morning Window
Everything goes in AM fasted, one morning window — no evening shots. On Monday and Thursday you take both: do Reta first, then SLU-PP in a different abdomen quadrant. Wipe each spot with alcohol, fresh syringe every time, wait 30–45 min before eating.
Draw marks
Reta 15u (Mon + Thu), SLU-PP 20u (Mon–Fri). Two different numbers — on the days you take both, don't mix them up. Small pulls on a 1 mL syringe, so glance at the line each time before you inject.
Tip 02 — The Numbers
Same Dose, Every Week
Nothing ramps on this protocol — both compounds sit at one number the whole 8 weeks. Reta: 15 units (1.5 mg), Monday and Thursday — 3 mg for the week. SLU-PP-332: 20 units (500 mcg), Monday through Friday — 2.5 mg for the week. Weekends are off for both.
Reta comes pre-mixed
Your vial arrives already in solution — don't add bac water to it. Straight into the fridge, and draw 15u on your two days. Only SLU-PP-332 gets reconstituted (2 mL per vial).
If a week feels rough
Persistent nausea means we hold or ease the Reta dose back — message Micah rather than pushing through or self-adjusting. There's no prize for toughing it out.
Tip 03 — Why It Sticks
Lose It — and Keep It Off

Two compounds, same target, different mechanisms. Reta creates the deficit and keeps your energy up (the glucagon arm raises resting burn); SLU-PP-332 mimics endurance training — it switches on fat-oxidation and mitochondrial-biogenesis pathways, so you're building metabolic capacity while you cut instead of downshifting into it.

Keeping it off is the part you control
Two habits make the loss durable: protein at every meal (roughly 0.7–1 g per lb of goal weight) and staying active most days — that keeps what you lose as fat, not muscle, and SLU-PP's mitochondrial work makes that activity pay off more.
Tip 04 — Week-by-Week
What to Expect
Weeks 1–3 (settling in)
appetite starts to drop and portions shrink on their own. Some mild nausea in the first days after a Reta shot is normal as your gut adapts. SLU-PP is working under the hood building mitochondrial capacity — you notice that more as steady energy than a big scale move yet. Hydrate hard.
Weeks 4–8 (it compounds)
same doses, but the effect stacks — appetite control gets more reliable, the scale moves steadily, and SLU-PP's daily fat-oxidation burn adds up on top. Energy holds because neither compound is a starvation lever.
Running out
Your four SLU-PP vials land right at 8 weeks — 2 weeks each. Message Micah as the last vial gets low and we reassess how you're feeling to decide the next cycle.
Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
Hold the dose if
persistent nausea/vomiting past 24 hrs, or severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. Don't push through — we drop the dose or pause.
Losing too fast
more than ~2 lbs/week sustained is muscle-loss territory — message us and we ease a dose back.
Injection sites
a little redness at the pinhole is normal; spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain = possible infection — stop that site and message us.
Anything that feels off, message first
— don't push through or restart on your own.
