Troy's Three-Cycle Protocol
One goal per cycle, run back to back — eight weeks each, twenty-four in total. Never more than four compounds at a time.
Three goals, three cycles
Cycle 1 — Fat Loss + Muscle Building. Cycle 2 — Cognitive Enhancement. Cycle 3 — Longevity & Anti-Aging. They run in that order, one after another, with no overlap.
Why sequential
Running one goal at a time keeps the daily shot count small and makes each cycle readable — you can actually tell what worked. The heaviest week in the protocol is six shots.
The shape of it
Wks 1–8 Recomp (4 compounds) · Wks 9–16 Cognitive (2) · Wks 17–24 Longevity (4). Week counts are nominal — a cycle finishes when its bottles finish.

What is missing — and it is most of the picture

A CBC tells you about your blood. It does not tell you about your metabolism, your hormones or your heart. The count above is genuinely healthy — but it is one panel out of six, and the five that were not run are the ones this protocol is aimed at.

Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) · kidney function, liver enzymes, electrolytes, glucose and protein. The baseline everything else is read against, and the one to add first.

Lipid panel · total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides. Cardiovascular risk is invisible on a CBC.

HbA1c · three-month average blood sugar. Directly relevant to Cycle 1 — it is the number that tells you what Retatrutide is doing metabolically, not just on the scale.

Testosterone (total and free), SHBG, estradiol · the hormone baseline behind a recomp cycle. Worth having before Cycle 1 rather than after.

Thyroid — TSH, Free T4, Free T3 · the first thing to rule out for fatigue, weight and mood, and not touched by a CBC.

Vitamin D, 25-Hydroxy · the single most commonly low marker in adults, and one of the cheapest to check and easiest to fix.

Nutrition
Eating For A Recomp
The short version

Fat loss and muscle gain at the same time comes down to one thing you defend and one thing you adjust. Defend the protein. Adjust the carbs. Everything else on this tab is detail.

Daily Targets
Protein
0.8–1 g per pound of body weight, every day, no exceptions. This is the number that decides whether the weight you lose is fat or muscle. Spread it across 3–4 meals rather than loading it at dinner.
Calories
A modest deficit — roughly 300–500 below maintenance. Retatrutide will make this easy to hit and easy to overshoot; the risk on a GLP-1 is eating too little, not too much.
Carbs
The adjustable lever. Put most of them around training — the meal before and the meal after. Rest days can run lower without touching protein or fat.
Fat
Do not cut fat to the floor. It runs your hormones. Olive oil, eggs, nuts, avocado, fatty fish — roughly a quarter to a third of your calories.
Water
Half your body weight in ounces, minimum, and more on training days. Reta blunts thirst as well as appetite — add 20–30 oz on Monday and Tuesday after the shot.
Whole foods first
Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, rice, potatoes, oats, fruit and vegetables. Shakes and bars are a convenience, not a base — use them to close a protein gap, not to replace meals.
How To Run It
Four Things That Decide The Outcome
Training Days
Fueling Around The Session
  • Eat before you train. Protein and carbs, 1–2 hours out. Training a hard session in a deficit on an empty stomach is how you leave strength on the table.
  • Protein and carbs after. The post-workout meal is where the IGF-1 block does its work — give it something to build with.
  • Do not cut carbs on lifting days. Carbs are what let you hold your working weights through a deficit, and holding your working weights is what holds your muscle.
  • Lift three to four times a week, hard. No peptide in this protocol builds muscle you did not train for. The training is the stimulus; the compounds are the recovery.
Cycle 1
Eating On Retatrutide
  • Hit the protein number whether or not you feel like eating. If a full meal is too much volume, a shake covers 40 g without the bulk. This is the single biggest risk on a GLP-1.
  • Keep Monday and Tuesday meals lighter and lower in fat. That is when nausea is most likely, and fat is the macro that makes it worse.
  • Push water harder after the Monday shot. Reta reduces thirst, so you will not feel the dehydration coming.
  • Do not chase a bigger deficit because appetite is easy. The appetite suppression is the tool, not the target. Under-eating for weeks costs muscle.
Sleep
The Half Of This That Happens In Bed
  • Seven to nine hours. CJC/Ipa amplifies the GH pulse that fires during deep sleep — a short night literally wastes the shot.
  • Caffeine cutoff at noon. Caffeine has a 5–6 hour half-life, so a 3pm coffee still has a quarter of its dose working at bedtime.
  • Alcohol is the biggest single variable. It gets you to sleep faster and then wrecks the second half of the night — exactly the window the GH pulse lives in.
  • A light, early dinner sleeps better than a big late one — and it is the same rule the evening shot needs. Those two requirements point the same direction.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Weekly Average
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? Drop about 150 calories — a smaller carb portion at dinner covers it. Do not cut protein to get there.
  • Losing faster than 1–1.5 lb a week? Add the calories back. Faster than that starts costing lean tissue, which is the opposite of what the GH side of this stack is for.
  • Watch the mirror and the tape, not just the scale. On a real recomp the scale can sit still for a month while the waist drops and the lifts go up. That is the result working, not stalling.

Reconstitution
Mixing, Step By Step
💧 First Time Mixing — Do It Slowly
1 · Clean
Alcohol pad on the rubber top of both the peptide vial and the BAC water vial. Let them air dry — do not blow on them or wipe them off.
2 · Draw the water
Pull the exact BAC volume listed on the compound card into a syringe. Each compound has its own volume — check the card, do not reuse the last one's number.
3 · Down the side
Angle the needle so the water runs down the inside wall of the vial onto the powder, not straight into it. Peptides are fragile; a jet of water shears them.
4 · Never shake
Swirl gently, or roll the vial between your palms. Shaking denatures peptide. Cloudiness that clears in a minute or two is normal; give it time rather than agitating it.
5 · Label it
Write the compound and the mix date on the vial with a marker. Once mixed, keep it in the fridge and use it within about 30 days.
Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
Unmixed powder
Fridge is fine; freezer is better for anything you will not open for months. Keep it dark and dry.
Mixed vials
Fridge, always — back shelf, not the door. The door swings through room temperature every time you open it. Use within about 30 days of mixing.
Never freeze a mixed vial
Freezing and thawing a reconstituted peptide destroys it. If a vial freezes solid, it is gone.
One needle, one shot
A fresh syringe every time — for the draw and the injection both. They dull immediately and a dull needle is what makes an injection hurt.
Notes & Tips
Running Your First Protocol

The First Week
  • Stagger the introductions. Start Retatrutide on Monday and add CJC/Ipa a few evenings later. If something does not agree with you, you want to know which compound it was.
  • The injection is subcutaneous — into the fat, not the muscle. Pinch an inch of skin, go in at 45–90 degrees, push slowly, done. Abdomen is the easiest site.
  • Rotate sites. The abdomen has four quadrants — never the same one-inch spot twice in a day. Rotate the assignment daily.
  • A small bruise or a drop of blood is normal. A raised, hot, spreading red patch is not — stop and get it looked at.
Timing
Fasted Means Fasted
  • Morning shots: on waking, before food. Water and black coffee are fine. Wait 30–45 minutes before eating.
  • Evening shots: 2+ hours after your last food, before bed. Insulin blunts the GH pulse, so a late dinner and a good CJC/Ipa dose are mutually exclusive.
  • If a night gets away from you, skip it. A missed dose costs almost nothing. A dose on top of a full stomach is mostly wasted.
  • Never double up to catch up. Missed is missed — go back to the normal schedule the next day.
Sequencing
Why The Cycles Do Not Overlap
  • You can tell what worked. Ten compounds running together would make it impossible to attribute anything. Two or three at a time and every cycle gives a clean answer.
  • The daily load stays small. Most weeks are one or two shots a day. For a first protocol that is the difference between a routine you keep and one you abandon in week three.
  • Week counts are approximate. A cycle finishes when its bottles finish — a week either side is normal and nothing to correct for.
  • Cycle 1 is the one to get right. It is the heaviest and the one with the most visible result. Cycles 2 and 3 are lighter in every way.
Safety
When To Stop And Ask
  • Persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain, or pain radiating to the back on Retatrutide — stop and seek medical care. This is the one symptom set on a GLP-1 that is not "push through it".
  • Any injection site that gets hot, hard, spreading or streaked — stop that site and get it looked at.
  • Tell any prescriber you see what you are taking. Bring the compound list; do not leave it off the intake form because it is not a prescription.
  • Get the full panel drawn. Only a CBC was run — the metabolic, lipid, hormone and thyroid picture is currently unknown, and Cycle 1 in particular is easier to run well with those numbers in hand.
