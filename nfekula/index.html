Nick's Fat Loss, Energy & Build Protocol
Retatrutide to take the weight off. MOTS-c for the energy to train. An easy, conservative on-ramp for your first peptide cycle.
Two compounds, kept deliberately simple — because this is your first cycle. You've done the hard-training thing before — the D-I background is still in there. A mitochondrial peptide taken fasted in the morning — it shows up as steadier daytime energy and better output when you do get to train, which is exactly what a packed week needs. You're still training, and a body carrying inflammation doesn't repair well between sessions. KPV is the anti-inflammatory piece — it calms the low-grade, body-wide inflammation that quietly slows recovery and makes everything feel harder. Both run a front-loaded ~4-week block, five nights a week before bed until the vials are empty — a healing phase up front to get you moving well before the fat-loss compounds carry the rest of the cycle.

Protocol Overview — How These Four Work Together
Retatrutide — The Weight Loss
A triple receptor agonist (GLP-1, GIP, glucagon) — it quiets appetite and food noise so you eat less without fighting it, and the glucagon arm tends to raise energy expenditure rather than blunt it. It's the engine that gets the ~20 lbs moving. Titrated up slowly, by feel, so a first-timer finds a dose that's comfortable.
MOTS-c — The Energy & Output
A mitochondrial-derived peptide. It signals your cells to build more mitochondria and run the ones they have more efficiently, which raises energy expenditure and shows up first as steadier daytime energy and better output in the gym — the thing that's hardest to find on a 70-hour week. Fasted, five mornings a week.
Wolverine (BPC-157 + TB-500) — The Repair
A healing-and-repair blend of two peptides that target tendon, ligament, and soft-tissue recovery, plus the blood supply that feeds healing. Taken pre-bed, it works in your body's overnight repair window — so the training you can fit in builds you up instead of grinding you down. Front-loaded: 5 nights a week for about the first 4 weeks.
KPV — The Anti-Inflammatory
A small anti-inflammatory peptide. It calms the low-grade, systemic inflammation you're likely carrying — the kind that quietly works against recovery, digestion, and fat loss. Run at night beside Wolverine, it clears the inflammation while Wolverine handles the structural repair. Front-loaded: 5 nights a week for about the first 4 weeks.
Why these four together: Reta cuts what goes in and gets the weight moving; MOTS-c gives you the morning energy to keep training — and both run the long arc of the cycle. Up front, a ~4-week recovery block gets you started right: Wolverine repairs the tendons and soft tissue your training taxes, and KPV calms the inflammation that slows everything down. Knock down the inflammation and kick-start healing over the first ~4 weeks, then let the fat-loss and energy compounds carry the rest of the ~2-month cycle — before we add the dedicated muscle and focus compounds next cycle.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Single-Use
Dry vials (before reconstitution)
store refrigerated at 36–46°F. Never freeze. Protect from light. Mix one Wolverine vial and one KPV vial at a time — leave the second of each dry in the fridge until you finish the one you're on. A dry vial keeps far longer than a mixed one.
Reconstituted vials
refrigerate immediately after mixing. Each vial only has to last its own window (MOTS-c ~6 weeks, Reta ~10+ weeks, each Wolverine and KPV vial ~2 weeks — vial 1 covers the first ~2 weeks of recovery, vial 2 the next ~2), well inside shelf life. Leave each 2nd vial dry until you finish the first.
Syringe discipline
fresh single-use syringe for every draw, no exceptions. Needle reuse is the most common cause of injection-site reactions — and at ~12 shots a week it matters.
Site rotation
rotate between lower abdomen (a fingerbreadth from the navel), outer thigh, and love handles. Never the same 1-inch spot two days running. Any time you do two shots at once (Monday AM, or any Weeks 1–4 recovery night), put them in two different sites.

Tip 01 — Most Important
Start Slow — That's the Whole Plan
This is your first peptide cycle, so we built it to be simple and forgiving: fasted shots in the morning (MOTS-c daily, Reta once a week), plus a front-loaded recovery pair before bed (Wolverine + KPV) for about the first 4 weeks. Nothing here is aggressive on purpose — the two recovery compounds are gentle repair peptides, not stimulants.
The Reta especially is a go-slow compound — you start at 10 units and climb 10 units a week (10u → 20u → 30u), and only ever move up if the last week felt good. Once you hit the 30u cap, hold there and evaluate by feel. There's no prize for climbing fast. The right dose is the lowest one that quiets your appetite while you still feel like yourself — if that's below the cap, park there. Text me anytime you're deciding whether to move up.

Tip 02 — Mornings
MOTS-c First Thing, Fasted — That's Your Energy
MOTS-c goes in before you eat, first thing in the morning, Monday through Friday. That's not a random detail — fasted is where people get the steadiest energy effect from it. It's a small draw (10 units), so pull it slowly and double-check the plunger before you inject.
Don't expect a jolt like caffeine. MOTS-c is a build-up compound — it works by accumulating over the weeks, and what you'll notice is fewer afternoon crashes and more in the tank when you train, not a Day-1 rush. The first couple of weeks may feel quiet; that's normal. Keep showing up.
Tip 03 — The Real Job
Consistency Beats Everything Else Here
With a 70-hour week, the hard part isn't any single shot — it's showing up every day. MOTS-c is a build-up compound; it works by accumulating, so a missed day here and there is nothing, but skipping regularly means you paid for something you're not really running.
Download the .ics from the Schedule tab and let your phone carry the reminders — each day's AM and PM shots and units already filled in. Anchor the shots to something you already do (coffee in the morning, brushing your teeth at night) and they stop being a decision.

Getting the Most Out of It
Protein · Hydration · Muscle
Tip 05 — Protein Floor
Keep Protein Up as Appetite Drops on Reta
Reta quiets appetite, which is the point — but it also makes it easy to under-eat protein and lose muscle along with the fat. With a build goal, that's the wrong kind of weight loss. Aim for roughly 0.7–1.0 g of protein per pound of goal body weight daily, and front-load it earlier in the day before the appetite suppression peaks.
Protecting your muscle now is also what sets up Cycle 2 to build on real tissue rather than rebuilding what a sloppy cut stripped off. Eggs and cottage cheese, real meat at meals, a protein shake if you're falling short.
Tip 06 — Hydration & Fiber
Drink More Than You Think — It Heads Off the Side Effects
The two most common Reta side effects are mild nausea and constipation, and both respond well to aggressive hydration (aim for ~2.5–3 L of water a day) and adding fiber (whole foods, or a psyllium husk supplement). Get ahead of them — it's a lot easier to prevent than to fix, and it keeps them from ever becoming a reason to hold a dose.
Tip 07 — Train When You Can
Short and Consistent Beats Perfect
You know how to train — the constraint is time, not knowledge. On the weeks the gym gets squeezed out, a couple of short, hard sessions still protect muscle and keep the metabolism up far better than waiting for the perfect week that never comes. Even 20–30 minutes of heavy compound lifts a few times a week does the job while Reta handles the deficit and MOTS-c keeps the energy steady.
This first cycle is about getting the weight off and the energy back. The dedicated muscle-building tools come in Cycle 2 — so right now, the win is just staying consistent and holding onto the muscle you've got.
