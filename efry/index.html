Where you're starting
You train hard and you're carrying the receipts — knees, left wrist, lower back. You wanted to know whether peptides can actually do something for healing and recovery. That question is what this whole stack is built around, and it's the right one to lead with.
About the needle
You said you're a bit nervous about injecting, and that's completely normal — almost everyone is before the first one. Here's the honest version: these are insulin syringes, the needle is about half an inch and thinner than a strand of spaghetti, and it goes into the fat just under the skin, not into muscle. Most people describe it as less than a mosquito bite, and a lot of them say the anticipation was worse than anything they actually felt. You do not have to start with all six. Start with one, do it once, and let yourself find out it's a non-event — then add the others as you get comfortable.
How this stack works
KLOW + KPV are the repair layer — that's the joint, tendon and soft-tissue work aimed straight at the knees, wrist and back. NAD+ is your lead interest and does the cellular-energy and mental-clarity job. Semax is a focus tool you reach for on the days you want it. CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin works the GH axis overnight, which is where recovery and sleep quality actually get paid. IGF-1 LR3 is the muscle-building and localized-repair piece, run in a short 10-day block.
On fat loss
You listed fat loss as a goal, and you asked to hold off on anything GLP-1 for now — so we did, and there's no GLP-1 in this stack. Straight answer: this set supports fat loss indirectly, by improving recovery, sleep, energy and lean mass so you can train more and move better. It is not a dedicated fat-loss tool. When you're ready for that, the GLP-1 option is there and we'll add it then — no rush, and nothing here has to change first.
The timeline
Run it until the bottles finish. Most of the stack lands around 8 weeks, which is a natural point to check in and decide what to keep, what to drop, and whether you want to add the fat-loss piece.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time, which is why there's no rush to start everything at once.
Mix one at a time
You have ten vials but only six compounds — open the second KLOW, KPV, NAD+ or CJC vial only as the first one empties. Each one lasts about 4 weeks once mixed.
Reconstitution
KLOW 3 mL, KPV 2 mL, NAD+ 5 mL, Semax 2 mL, CJC/Ipa 2 mL, IGF-1 LR3 2 mL. Add water slowly down the vial wall, swirl gently, never shake.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on each vial — KLOW 15u, NAD+ 37.5u, everything else 10u. Six vials in a fridge door look identical at 6am.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within about 30 days — which lines up almost exactly with how long each vial lasts at your cadence.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The First One
Getting Past the Needle
You said you wanted to see if you can stand the needle. You can — and here's how to make the first one as easy as possible.
  • Pinch, don't stab. Pinch an inch of soft belly fat, slide the needle in at 45–90°, push slow, pull out. The whole thing takes about five seconds.
  • Let the alcohol dry. Wiping and going in wet is what actually stings for most people — give it ten seconds.
  • Don't watch the needle if you'd rather not. Plenty of people look away for the first few. It works exactly the same.
  • Start with one compound. NAD+ on a Monday, nothing else that day. One shot is a much smaller thing to talk yourself into than four.
  • It gets boring fast. Almost everyone stops thinking about it by the end of week one. The anticipation really is the hard part.
Tip 02 — The Routine
Two Windows, Weekdays Only
Everything sits in one of two windows. Build them into things you already do and there's nothing to remember.
  • AM fasted — before breakfast. KLOW 15u and KPV 10u every weekday; add NAD+ 37.5u on Mon/Wed/Fri. Different quadrant for each, wait 30–45 min to eat.
  • PM fasted — before bed. CJC/Ipa 10u, at least 2 hrs after food, 30–60 min before lights out.
  • Post-workout — during your IGF-1 block. 10u right after training, 10 days straight.
  • Weekends off. Built in on purpose — it gives receptors a break and gives you two days without thinking about it.
  • Missed one? Skip it. Don't double up. Nothing here is dependent on a perfect streak.
Tip 03 — Week by Week
What to Expect
Healing peptides are quieter than stimulants — the changes show up as things that stop bothering you rather than something you feel switch on.
  • Weeks 1–2 — easing in. Getting comfortable with the routine is the whole job. NAD+ tends to land first as steadier energy and clearer mornings. Sleep often improves early once CJC/Ipa is in — that's usually the first unmistakable sign something's working.
  • Weeks 3–4 — the aches get quieter. This is where KLOW and KPV start showing up. It rarely arrives as "my knee is healed" — it's more that you notice you got through a session, or up the stairs, without thinking about it. Wrists and smaller joints often respond before knees and lower backs, which carry more load.
  • Weeks 5–6 — recovery compounds. Shorter turnaround between hard sessions, better sleep quality, and the training starts feeling more productive. Good window to run your first IGF-1 block if you haven't.
  • Weeks 7–8 — the picture. Most vials finish together here. Best judgment isn't the scale — it's whether the knees, wrist and back are better than in week 1, and whether you're recovering faster. Message Micah and we'll decide what to rerun.
Tip 04 — Getting the Most Out of It
What You Control
The stack raises your ceiling. These are the things that decide how much of it you actually collect.
  • Protein. Repair is built out of raw materials. Roughly 0.7–1 g per lb of goal weight (~150–210 g at 210) — this matters more on a healing protocol than almost anything else.
  • Sleep is where CJC/Ipa gets paid. You rated sleep 4/5, so there's room here. The PM shot amplifies your overnight GH pulse — going to bed 30–60 min after it, consistently, is what converts that into recovery.
  • Alcohol works against this specific stack. At 3–7 drinks a week you're not doing anything unusual — but alcohol blunts the overnight GH pulse and slows tissue repair, so drinking nights are nights CJC/Ipa mostly doesn't work. Not a lecture, just where the trade-off is: shifting a couple of those to non-training, non-dosing nights buys you real return.
  • Keep training, but let the sore joints lead. Load drives the repair signal — but if the knees or back are barking, work around them for a few weeks rather than through them. You have eight weeks of healing support; use it to get back to full load, not to push through at full load.
  • Hydrate. NAD+ especially goes down easier and stings less when you're not underhydrated.
