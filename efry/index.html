Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time, which is why there's no rush to start everything at once.
Mix one at a time
You have nine vials but only five compounds — open the second Wolverine, KPV, CJC or NAD+ vial only as the first one empties. Wolverine runs about 2 weeks per vial; KPV, CJC and NAD+ about 4.
Reconstitution
Wolverine 2 mL, KPV 2 mL, CJC/Ipa 2 mL, MOTS-C 3 mL, NAD+ 5 mL. Add water slowly down the vial wall, swirl gently, never shake.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on each vial — NAD+ 25u, Wolverine 20u, and 10u for KPV, CJC and MOTS-C. Vials in a fridge door look identical at 6am, and three of these share the same mark.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within about 30 days — which comfortably covers how long each vial lasts at your cadence.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The First One
Getting Past the Needle
You said you wanted to see if you can stand the needle. You can — and here's how to make the first one as easy as possible.
  • Pinch, don't stab. Pinch an inch of soft belly fat, slide the needle in at 45–90°, push slow, pull out. The whole thing takes about five seconds.
  • Let the alcohol dry. Wiping and going in wet is what actually stings for most people — give it ten seconds.
  • Don't watch the needle if you'd rather not. Plenty of people look away for the first few. It works exactly the same.
  • Start with one compound. MOTS-C on a Monday morning, nothing else that day — at 10u it's a small, easy draw to start on. One is a much smaller thing to talk yourself into than five.
  • It gets boring fast. Almost everyone stops thinking about it by the end of week one. The anticipation really is the hard part.
Tip 02 — The Routine
Two Windows, Weekdays Only
Everything sits in one of two windows. Build them into things you already do and there's nothing to remember.
  • AM fasted — before breakfast. MOTS-C 10u every weekday, plus NAD+ 25u on Mon / Wed / Fri in a different quadrant. Then wait 30–45 min to eat.
  • PM fasted — before bed. Wolverine 20u, KPV 10u and CJC/Ipa 10u, at least 2 hrs after food, 30–60 min before lights out. Three different quadrants — never stack them in one spot.
  • The evening is where the healing happens. All three repair-side compounds are deliberately in the same window, because overnight is when your body does the tissue work.
  • Weekends off. Built in on purpose — it gives receptors a break and gives you two days without thinking about it.
  • Missed one? Skip it. Don't double up. Nothing here is dependent on a perfect streak.
Tip 03 — Week by Week
What to Expect
Healing peptides are quieter than stimulants — the changes show up as things that stop bothering you rather than something you feel switch on.
  • Weeks 1–2 — easing in. Getting comfortable with the routine is the whole job. Sleep often improves early once CJC/Ipa is in — that's usually the first unmistakable sign something's working. NAD+ and MOTS-C tend to land as steadier daytime energy and clearer mornings.
  • Weeks 3–4 — the aches get quieter. This is where Wolverine and KPV start showing up. It rarely arrives as “my knee is healed” — it's more that you notice you got through a session, or up the stairs, without thinking about it. Wrists and smaller joints usually respond before knees and lower backs, which carry more load.
  • Around week 4 — first decision point. Wolverine's two vials finish here. If the joints are clearly better, this is where you decide whether to reorder Wolverine and keep the repair running while everything else carries on underneath.
  • Weeks 5–8 — consolidation. KPV keeps the inflammation down, CJC/Ipa keeps converting sleep into recovery, and NAD+ carries the energy side once the MOTS-C vial finishes around week 6. The honest measure isn't the scale — it's whether the knees, wrist and back are better than they were in week 1, and whether you're recovering faster between sessions.
Tip 04 — Getting the Most Out of It
What You Control
The stack raises your ceiling. These are the things that decide how much of it you actually collect.
  • Protein. Repair is built out of raw materials. Roughly 0.7–1 g per lb of goal weight (~150–210 g at 210) — this matters more on a healing protocol than almost anything else.
  • Sleep is where CJC/Ipa gets paid. You rated sleep 4/5, so there's room here. The PM shot amplifies your overnight GH pulse — going to bed 30–60 min after it, consistently, is what converts that into recovery.
  • Alcohol works against this specific stack. At 3–7 drinks a week you're not doing anything unusual — but alcohol blunts the overnight GH pulse and slows tissue repair, so drinking nights are nights CJC/Ipa mostly doesn't work. Not a lecture, just where the trade-off is: shifting a couple of those to non-training, non-dosing nights buys you real return.
  • Keep training, but let the sore joints lead. Load drives the repair signal — but if the knees or back are barking, work around them for a few weeks rather than through them. You have eight weeks of healing support; use it to get back to full load, not to push through at full load.
  • Hydrate. NAD+ especially goes down easier and stings less when you're not underhydrated — and well-hydrated tissue takes an injection better across 23 shots a week.
