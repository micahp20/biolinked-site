Tiffany's Four-Cycle Protocol
Cognitive first, because that is the one you named first. Then a longevity block, then the hormonal and libido side, with sleep and stress running whenever you want it.
Where you are starting
47, early perimenopause, 5'3" at 120 lb — and 120 lb is the goal. Under your PCP's care, currently on Lexapro and iron.
This is not a weight protocol
There is no GLP-1 and no fat-loss compound anywhere on this page. You are already where you want to be on the scale, so nothing here is aimed at moving it. Eat to maintain and the protocol will not fight you.
The order, and why
Cycle 1 is cognitive, because that is your primary goal. Cycle 2 is a 20-night bioregulator block. Cycle 3 is libido and the hormonal axis. Cycle 4 is a standalone — sleep and stress, and you can start it whenever you want, including alongside Cycle 1.
First time injecting
Every dose on this page opens at the low end of its range. Nothing starts at a working dose. The first note in the Notes tab is a full walkthrough of a subcutaneous shot, start to finish.
Sleep and stress
Sleep is the lowest-scoring thing on your intake — roughly five hours, and you rated it 2 out of 5. Cycle 4 is the answer to that and it is not gated behind the other three. If sleep is what you want fixed first, run Cycle 4 from week one.

Cycle 4 · Sleep

How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water into a syringe, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it straight onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
It looks like nothing
The powder is often just a faint film at the bottom of the vial — that is normal, not a short vial, and it is most obvious on the small 10 mg Pinealon vials. Once mixed, everything on this page goes completely clear.
The Oxytocin is different — mix it into the BAC bottle
Oxytocin goes into a full 10 mL bottle of BAC water, not into its own powder vial — you do not need a separate empty vial. 1. Start with a full, sealed 10 mL BAC water bottle. 2. Draw 1 mL of BAC water out of it. 3. Inject that 1 mL into the Oxytocin powder vial, down the side wall, and swirl until fully dissolved. 4. Draw the same ~1 mL back out — it now holds all 10 mg of dissolved Oxytocin. 5. Inject it back into the 10 mL BAC water bottle. 6. The Oxytocin is now spread across the full 10 mL — that bottle is the dosing vial, 1 mg/mL, 10u = 100 mcg. Label it first and throw the empty powder vial away. Mixed at 2 mL out of habit it would be five times the intended dose in the same draw, which is why this one is worth doing slowly.
Order — one cycle at a time
Mix only the cycle you are starting. Cycle 1: Semax #1, Adamax #1 and Cerebrolysin on day one; the #2 Semax and Adamax on the Monday of Week 5. Cycle 2: Epitalon, Pinealon #1 and Cortagen on the first morning; Pinealon #2 on night 11. Cycle 3: Kisspeptin #1 and Oxytocin #1 at the start, PT-141 #1 the same week, then all three #2 vials on the Monday of Week 16. Cycle 4: DSIP #1, Selank #1 and PE-22-28 #1 at the start, then all three #2 vials at the four-week mark. Everything else stays dry and in the box.
Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps well, which is why every later cycle's vials simply wait in the box.
After mixing
Refrigerate. Keep mixed vials at the back of the fridge rather than in the door — the door swings through the widest temperature range in the whole appliance.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix. C1: Semax 5u, Adamax 5u, Cerebrolysin 15u. C2: Epitalon 12u, Pinealon 10u, Cortagen 10u. C3: Kisspeptin 5u, PT-141 5u, Oxytocin 10u. C4: DSIP 10u, Selank 5u, PE-22-28 5u. The Selank and PE-22-28 vials are both a 5-unit morning draw — label those two clearly. Kisspeptin and PT-141 look identical in a fridge and land in the same cycle at the same 5-unit draw — that is the pair worth labelling carefully.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, let the alcohol dry, rotate injection sites. Three shots is the busiest any day on this protocol ever gets, and that only happens inside the two 20-day blocks.
Let it warm up first
Take the vial out of the fridge and let the drawn syringe sit for a couple of minutes before injecting. Cold liquid stings going in; room-temperature liquid mostly does not. On a first protocol this is worth the two minutes.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01 — Start Here
Your First Injection, Start To Finish
These are subcutaneous, not intramuscular
Every shot on this page goes into the fat layer just under the skin, not into muscle. That is a completely different thing from a flu shot or a B12 shot — the needle is shorter, thinner and goes in at a shallow angle, and there is far less to it than you are probably picturing.
Where
The belly is the easiest place to start — anywhere in a palm-width band around the navel, but stay two inches clear of the navel itself. The outer thigh and the back of the upper arm both work too. Pick a different spot every time; walk around the clock face rather than going back to the same place.
The actual steps
1. Wipe the vial stopper with alcohol. 2. Draw your units. 3. Wipe the skin and let it dry completely. 4. Pinch an inch of skin between two fingers. 5. Go in at 45 degrees, all the way, in one steady motion — not slowly. 6. Push the plunger down at a normal pace. 7. Pull straight out, let go of the pinch, press with a clean finger for a few seconds. That is it.
What it actually feels like
With a 31G needle most people feel a faint pinch or nothing at all. Going in fast hurts less than going in slowly — hesitating is what makes it sting. If a shot does sting, the cause is almost always one of two things: the alcohol was still wet, or the liquid was straight out of the fridge.
A little blood or a small bruise is normal
A pinprick of blood or a faint bruise means you clipped a tiny surface vessel. It is not a problem and it does not affect the dose. Press, do not rub. If you see a bead of liquid come back out, that draw was slightly too shallow — next time go a little deeper.
Start with the easiest one
Your very first shot is Cerebrolysin at 15 units or Semax at 5 units — both tiny volumes, both in the belly, both first thing in the morning. Do that one a few times and the rest of the protocol is the same motion with a different number on the barrel.
Note 02 — Dosing
Why Everything Opens Low, And When It Moves
Every number on this page is the bottom of its range
Semax at 5u, Adamax at 5u, Cerebrolysin at 15u, Cortagen and Pinealon at 10u, Kisspeptin, PT-141 and PE-22-28 all at 5u. None of these is a working dose — they are opening doses, deliberately, because this is your first protocol and the only thing worth knowing in the first two weeks is how you personally respond.
How to move up
Run two weeks at the opening dose before changing anything. If it is well tolerated and you want more from it, the next step is doubling the units on one compound at a time — never two in the same week, or you will not know which one did what. Semax 5u→10u is the most common first step up.
How to move down
If something does not sit well, halve it rather than stopping it. PT-141 is the one most likely to need this: flushing or mild nausea in the first hour is its known profile, and it is loudest on the earliest doses. Dropping from 5u to 2–3u usually settles it, and the effect does not disappear.
The two 20-day blocks do not move
Cerebrolysin in Cycle 1 and the Epitalon / Pinealon / Cortagen block in Cycle 2 are consecutive-day courses, and the course is the mechanism. Do not extend either one because powder is left in the vial, and do not stretch it across more days at a lower dose.
Note 03 — Perimenopause
What This Protocol Is, And What It Is Not
Nothing here is a hormone, and nothing here replaces one
This is worth being clear about, because the words overlap. Kisspeptin signals your own axis from the top — it asks the hypothalamus to pulse rather than adding estrogen or progesterone from outside. Oxytocin is a neuropeptide, not a sex hormone. There is no HRT on this page and it is not a substitute for it. If HRT is a conversation you want to have, that conversation belongs with your PCP, and the two are not mutually exclusive.
Why the sleep and cognitive cycles matter here specifically
The two things you named first — the mental fog and the five-hour nights — are also two of the most common early perimenopausal complaints, and they feed each other. Short sleep produces almost exactly the cognitive picture you described, so Cycle 4 is partly a cognitive intervention even though it is labelled sleep.
Your cycle timing matters for bloodwork
If you get a hormone panel drawn during this, have it drawn on day 3–5 of your cycle and do the same on any repeat. A single draw at a random point in a perimenopausal cycle is close to uninterpretable, and two draws at different points are worse than one.
Keep your prescriber in the loop
You are under a PCP's care and on prescription medication. Tell them you are adding peptides — not for permission, just so your chart is accurate and so anything you notice gets attributed correctly. Bring this page if it is easier than explaining it.
Note 04 — Weight
You Are Not Trying To Lose Anything
120 lb is the goal, and you are at it
There is no GLP-1 and no fat-loss compound anywhere on this protocol — not because you could not have one, but because you do not want the result. At 5'3" and 120 lb the job is holding, and everything on this page is aimed at how you feel and function rather than at the scale.
If the scale does drift down, eat more
A few compounds here can nudge appetite slightly, mostly in the first week. If you drop below about 117 lb, add food rather than waiting to see. Going into a deficit while starting a cognitive and sleep protocol will undercut the exact things you are trying to fix.
Protein and iron
You are on iron, which means it came up on a panel. Keep taking it as prescribed, and keep it away from coffee and tea by a couple of hours — both cut absorption substantially. Protein at 90–100 g a day is the target for a 120 lb maintenance build; the Nutrition tab will lay out a full plan if you want one.
Note 05 — Missed Doses
What To Do When A Day Gets Away From You
The general rule
Skip it and carry on. Do not double the next dose to catch up, on anything on this page. One missed shot changes nothing; a doubled shot is the one that gives you a bad day.
Inside a 20-day block
If you miss a night of the Epitalon / Pinealon block or a morning of the Cortagen or Cerebrolysin course, add a day to the end of the block so you still complete 20. That is the one place where making it up is correct, because the count is the point.
Semax, Selank, Adamax and PE-22-28
These are flexible by design. M / W / F is a rhythm, not a requirement — if Wednesday slips to Thursday, that is fine. Selank in particular works perfectly well used on the day that actually needs it.
PT-141 and the evening compounds
If you miss a PT-141 evening, leave it — do not take it late at night. DSIP taken too close to lights-out is the one miss worth avoiding: the 1–3 hour window before bed is where it works, and taking it at 1 a.m. gets you the grogginess without the benefit.
Note 06 — Judging It
When Each Cycle Is Fair To Assess
Cycle 1 · Cognitive
Semax and Adamax are same-day compounds — you will have an opinion within the first week. Cerebrolysin is the opposite: judge it at the end of the 20-day course and again a month later, because its effect is structural and shows up after the course rather than during it.
Cycle 2 · Longevity
This is the cycle with the least to feel, and that is expected. The one thing you may notice is sleep quality in the second week of the block — Epitalon works on the gland that sets your melatonin rhythm. Everything else here is a mechanism you are buying rather than a sensation.
Cycle 3 · Hormonal
PT-141 you will know about the first evening. Kisspeptin and Oxytocin are slow accumulators — judge them at week 19, not week 13. The whole cycle runs eight weeks precisely because the two slow ones need it.
Cycle 4 · Sleep & Stress
DSIP usually shows up within three to five nights, and what changes first is depth rather than duration — you will likely wake at the same time but feel differently about it. Total hours follow later. Selank is same-day. PE-22-28 is the slow one in this cycle — it works on mood baseline rather than on a given day, so give it three to four weeks before forming a view. Track the cycle simply: write down what time you fell asleep and how you felt at 10 a.m.
