Byron's Weight, Energy & Performance Protocol
Retatrutide + MOTS-c + NAD+ — take the weight off, keep the energy up, and hold onto the athlete.
The goal
Two things at once: keep the weight off for good and get your energy back. The stack is built so the fat loss doesn't cost you the thing you actually care about — still moving like an athlete on the course and in the gym.
Where you're starting
45, with a real training history — D-II basketball behind you and a strong golf game now. That matters: your body responds to training, and the problem has been keeping weight off and holding energy, not learning how to work. This stack is aimed squarely at those two.
How they work together
Retatrutide is the weight lever — a triple agonist (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon). The GLP-1/GIP arms handle appetite; the glucagon arm raises what you burn at rest, so the loss keeps moving without you eating less and less. MOTS-c is the energy and performance lever — a mitochondrial peptide that activates AMPK, the same switch exercise flips, which is why it reads as steadier all-day energy and better output. NAD+ refills the cellular fuel your mitochondria actually run on — the piece that tends to be lowest at 45 and the one people feel first.
Why it holds
Reta titrates up 1 mg at a time so your body adapts instead of stalling. MOTS-c and NAD+ build capacity underneath — so as you lean out, your metabolism and your energy come up rather than down. Keep protein high and keep lifting, and what comes off is fat, not the muscle you spent years building.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
Reconstitution
Per vial: Reta 3 mL, MOTS-c 2 mL, NAD+ 5 mL — unchanged. Mix one vial at a time; open the second of each only as the first empties. Add the water slowly down the vial wall, swirl gently, don't shake.
Label them
three vials at three different draws — write the compound and the unit mark (10u / 37.5u / 10–40u) right on each one so a Monday morning never turns into guesswork.
After mixing
keep refrigerated and use within about 30 days — which is why you only mix one vial at a time rather than all six up front. NAD+ still finishes first (~9 weeks across both vials).
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Routine
One Morning Window
Everything on this protocol is AM — no evening shots to remember. Build it into the same ten minutes every morning and it stops being a decision.
  • Order on Monday: MOTS-c first (fasted), then NAD+, then Reta — different abdomen quadrant for each.
  • Three draws, three numbers: MOTS-c 10u, NAD+ 37.5u, Reta 10u rising to 40u. Read the barrel every time; they're easy to mix up early on.
  • Fasted for MOTS-c — wait 30–45 min before you eat.
  • Push NAD+ slowly. Fast is where the sting comes from.
Tip 02 — The Ladder
1 mg Up, 4 mg Ceiling
Retatrutide is the only thing that changes week to week. Every Monday it goes up by 1 mg until it hits 4 mg, and then it stops.
  • Week 1 is 1 mg (10u) — deliberately small. It's a starting point, not a test of toughness.
  • Only step up if the week before felt fine. Rough week? Hold the same dose an extra week and climb after.
  • 4 mg (40u) is the ceiling. Don't go past it — hold there and message Micah to reevaluate.
  • Hydrate harder than feels necessary. Reta blunts thirst, so you won't notice you're behind until you feel flat.
Tip 03 — Keeping the Athlete
Lose Fat, Not the Engine
You've got a training background most people don't — the goal is to come out of this leaner and still moving like it. Two habits do most of that work.
  • Protein at every meal — roughly 0.7–1 g per pound of goal weight. This is the single biggest thing separating "leaner" from "smaller and softer."
  • Keep lifting. Appetite drops on Reta, so the stimulus to hold muscle has to come from the bar. Two or three real sessions a week is plenty.
  • Golf counts, but it isn't resistance training. Walking 18 is great for the deficit and great for energy — it just doesn't replace the lifting.
  • MOTS-c and NAD+ pay off in the work you do. They raise your ceiling; the training is what cashes it in.
Tip 04 — Week by Week
What to Expect
  • Weeks 1–2: Reta is still low, so the scale may be quiet. What usually moves first is energy — that's MOTS-c and NAD+ landing. Some mild nausea in the day or two after a Reta shot is normal.
  • Weeks 3–4: as Reta climbs to 3–4 mg, appetite control gets obvious and portions shrink on their own. The scale starts moving steadily.
  • Weeks 5–8: holding at 4 mg, the loss compounds while energy stays up — that's the whole point of stacking the mitochondrial side underneath the appetite side.
  • Running out: NAD+ goes first at ~4.4 weeks, MOTS-c at 8 weeks, Reta around 9 weekly doses. They don't finish together — message Micah as each gets low.
