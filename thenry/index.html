Personalized Protocol
Retatrutide
Weight-management starter — one shot a week, easing up by feel.
Your starting point
One compound, one shot a week. Nothing else to juggle. You start low at 10 units and add 10 units each week, so your body meets the dose gradually instead of all at once.
You set the pace
Every week is a checkpoint. If you feel good, step up. If a week is rough, hold at the same dose or drop back down — the ladder is a guide, not a schedule you owe anyone. 60 units is the ceiling, not a target.

Step up, hold, or step down
Check in with yourself before each shot. Felt good all week → add 10 units. Felt rough → repeat the same dose. Really rough → drop back a rung. There's no penalty for taking longer.

Storage

Dry vial
refrigerate at 36–46°F. Never freeze. Stable for many months sealed — leave it dry until the week you start.
Reconstituted vial
refrigerate immediately after mixing, back in the fridge right after every draw. This vial spans roughly 7–8 weekly doses, so cold-chain discipline matters more here than on a short cycle — keep it upright, keep it dark, keep it cold.
Syringe discipline
fresh single-use insulin syringe for every shot. Never reuse. Wipe the rubber stopper and your injection site with an alcohol pad every time.
Reta Notes
Read Before Week 1
Note 01 — How the Ladder Works
Reevaluate Every Week
Before each shot, ask
How did the last seven days go — nausea, energy, appetite, how food sat? That answer picks this week's dose, not the calendar.
Good week
add 10 units. That's the ladder working as designed.
Rough week
stay at the same units for another week. Repeating a rung is normal and costs you nothing.
Really rough week
drop back down a rung and sit there. Then climb again when it settles — or don't. Plenty of people find their sweet spot well under the cap.
60 units is the ceiling
not a goal. If 30 units is doing the job, that's your dose.
Note 02 — Week by Week
What the Early Weeks Feel Like
Weeks 1–2
10–20 units. Appetite starts to quiet within a few days. Mild nausea on shot day or the day after is common — smaller meals, less fat for 24 hours after the shot, and drink more water than feels necessary.
Weeks 3–4
30–40 units. Early GI stuff usually settles. Food noise gets noticeably quieter and portions shrink on their own. This is where most people first see the scale move.
Weeks 5–6
50–60 units. Fat loss curve takes hold. Aim for 1–1.5 lbs a week — consistently faster than 2 lbs a week means you're eating too little, not that it's working better. Keep protein up.
Week 7 onward
holding at whatever dose you landed on. Vial is running low around here — text Micah before you're empty so there's no gap.
Note 03 — Missed Dose
What to Do If You Skip
If you miss your Monday, take it within 4 days and reset your weekly day to that new day. If more than 4 days have passed, skip that week entirely and pick back up the following Monday at the same dose you last took — don't double up, and don't step up on a restart week.
