Personalized Protocol
Germaine's Five-Cycle Protocol
One cycle per goal, run in order — longevity is the headline, and it gets the biggest cycle. Durations below are nominal: titrate up, then finish the bottles you were sent.
Your goals
You ranked Longevity & Anti-Aging first, then fat loss, cognitive, recovery/healing and sleep/stress. Five goals, five cycles — one per goal, so every cycle gives you a clean answer about the goal it is aimed at.
The shape of it
Cycle 1 Fat Loss · Cycle 2 Cognitive, as needed · Cycle 3 Recovery & Healing · Cycle 4 Longevity & Anti-Aging · Cycle 5 Sleep & Stress. Cycle 2 has no assigned weeks on purpose — those three vials sit in the box and you reach for them. Repair comes before longevity deliberately: fix the tissue first, then work on the clock.
You already inject
You are an experienced injector and currently mid-cycle, so nothing here is written for a beginner. Doses are normal-range rather than entry-level, with one deliberate exception below.
The Retatrutide exception
You reported minor, inconsistent stomach issues on Retatrutide. That is the one thing on this intake that changes a dose. Reta opens at 1 mg — 10 units, the standard entry rung, and climbs only on weeks that were clean. The rungs below the start — 0.75 mg and 0.5 mg — are on the card on purpose, so you have somewhere to go if a week goes badly. Full ladder is on the Reta card.
Sleep is the weak link
Sleep reads 3 out of 5 on six hours while stress reads a low 2 out of 5. That combination points at depth and duration rather than at anxiety, which is why Cycle 5 is built around DSIP and PE-22-28 rather than around a daytime anxiolytic — and why the whole cycle is evening-only.
Alcohol
3–7 drinks a week is worth naming because alcohol suppresses slow-wave sleep directly. Cycle 5 is the cycle it will interfere with most; the nights you drink are the nights DSIP has the least to work with.
Already taking
Progesterone (sleep), D3/K2, B12, creatine, protein, collagen, magnesium. Nothing on this page conflicts with any of them, and the collagen pairs usefully with the GHK-Cu in Cycle 3.

What This Changes In The Protocol
Short answer: very little — and that is the useful result

Nothing on the protocol is androgenic. The one out-of-range hormone is the free testosterone, and none of the seventeen compounds across the five cycles raises androgens. No change to any cycle.

The perimenopausal picture supports the ordering already on the page. Falling estradiol accelerates collagen and bone loss, which is exactly what GHK-Cu and Cartalax in Cycle 3 are pointed at, and it is a common driver of the sleep-depth problem Cycle 5 addresses.

Notes & Tips
Running This Well
Note 01 — The Dose You Drive
Retatrutide, And Your Stomach History

Retatrutide opens at the standard 1 mg — 10 units. Because you reported minor, inconsistent stomach issues on Retatrutide, the ladder is slower than the house default and it runs both ways: there are rungs below the starting dose as well as above it.

  • Hold two weeks at every rung. Up: 1 mg (10u) → 1.5 mg (15u) → 2 mg (20u). Down: 0.75 mg (7.5u) → 0.5 mg (5u).
  • Only step up after a clean week. If the stomach was unsettled at all, repeat the rung instead. Repeating is a normal move, not a setback.
  • Tier down without hesitating. If a rung is not tolerated, drop back one and stay there. A dose you take every week beats a bigger one you skip.
  • Monday and Tuesday, keep meals lighter and lower in fat. Slowed gastric emptying plus a heavy meal is what turns a mild effect into a bad afternoon.
  • Water. Reta blunts thirst as well as appetite, and dehydration reads as nausea.
Note 02 — Technique
Subcutaneous, And Rotate

You already inject, so this is a reminder rather than a lesson — but Cycle 3 runs every day, weekends included.

  • Every compound here is subcutaneous. Pinch, 45–90°, into the fat layer of the abdomen, love handle or outer thigh.
  • Cycle 3 is four shots a day, every day. Rotation stops being good practice and becomes necessary — work around the abdominal ring rather than reusing a spot.
  • Two shots in the same window go in opposite quadrants, not side by side.
  • Fresh syringe every time. A reused needle is blunt on the second pass and that is what causes bruising.
Note 03 — The Rule That Matters Most In Cycle 4
Bioregulators Need Consecutive Days

Epithalon, Pinealon and Cartalax are Khavinson bioregulators, and they work differently from everything else on this page. They act at the level of gene expression in one specific tissue, over a defined course, and the effect is meant to persist after the course rather than depend on staying on it.

  • Consecutive days, weekends included. A missed night in an Epithalon block is a real gap, not a rounding error.
  • You will not feel them while you take them. That is normal and expected — they are judged weeks after the course ends, not during it.
  • They stop when the course is done. Do not extend one because there is powder left or because you feel good on it. That applies to FOX04-DRI most of all — two weeks, then stop, two or three times a year.
Note 04 — Sleep
Six Hours, And What Actually Moves It

Sleep is 3 out of 5 on six hours while stress is a low 2 out of 5. That pattern says depth and duration, not anxiety — which is why Cycle 5 is built the way it is, and why Pinealon and Epithalon sit in Cycle 4, ahead of it.

  • DSIP goes in 1–3 hours before bed, not at lights-out. It is a depth compound, not a sedative, and more of it does not feel stronger.
  • Alcohol is the biggest single variable here. At 3–7 drinks a week it suppresses slow-wave sleep directly — the exact stage DSIP is working on. The nights you drink are the nights it has the least to work with.
  • The progesterone you already take stacks cleanly with all of it. Different pathway, no conflict.
  • Six hours is the other half of the problem. No compound on this page manufactures a seventh hour.
Note 05 — Sequencing
Add One Thing At A Time

Cycles run in order and do not overlap. Inside a cycle, the introductions still get staggered.

  • Cycle 1: Retatrutide first, alone, for the opening week. Add AOD-9604 and 5-Amino-1MQ from Week 2, so a stomach reaction has only one possible cause.
  • Cycle 2: Semax alone for a week, then add the Selank, then the Adamax a week after that. Adamax always after the Semax on the day, never before.
  • Cycle 3: all four together from day one. There is nothing to titrate here and no interaction to stagger for — it is the one cycle you can simply start.
  • Cycle 4: start Pinealon and Epithalon the same night and keep them unbroken; add FOX04-DRI on the weekday mornings. Once a bioregulator course starts it should not be interrupted, so begin it on a week you will actually be home.
  • Cycle 5: DSIP alone for the first week. It is the compound doing the actual work, and you want to know what it does on its own.
Note 06 — Overlap Worth Knowing
Two Forms Of Selank, Two Different Jobs

Selank appears twice on this page and it is not a duplication. Cycle 2 has the N-Acetyl Selank Amidate at 30 mg — the stabilised, longer-acting form, dosed in the morning to keep daytime focus from tipping into wired. Cycle 5 has the plain Selank at 10 mg, dosed in the early evening, where shorter-acting is the point: settle the last few hours of the day and then be gone.

  • If both cycles are ever running at once, use one or the other on a given day, not both.
  • Neither one is sedating and neither has a dependency profile.

Note 08 — When To Stop

  • A rash, swelling of the lips or face, or difficulty breathing after any shot — stop immediately and get seen.
  • Anything that feels wrong and does not settle. You have no physician on file, so the threshold for going in should be lower rather than higher.
