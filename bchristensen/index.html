Nutrition
What Repair Runs On
  • Protein — 1 g per pound of bodyweight. Tissue cannot rebuild out of nothing. This is the one that matters most.
  • Vitamin C, 500–1000 mg daily. Collagen synthesis will not run without it.
  • Water — 100+ oz. Healing tissue is thirsty tissue.
  • Sleep — 8 hours. Growth hormone and repair both run overnight. Nothing on this page outperforms sleep.

Reconstitution & Storage
Mixing, Then Keeping It Cold
💧 Mixing — One Week At A Time
Every vial
2 mL of BAC water — BPC-157, KPV and Wolverine all the same. Mix all three on Monday morning, use them through Friday.
How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Storage
Dry vials at 36–46°F, out of light, never frozen. Mixed vials go straight back in the fridge and are finished within the week. The other eight stay dry in the box until their Monday.
Label them
Write the compound and AM or PM on every vial you mix. Two of the three are morning shots and they look identical.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01
Inject Near The Injury
Subcutaneous, and as close to the injured area as you can comfortably get — local delivery is where BPC-157 and TB-500 do their best work. Never through broken skin, a wound, a surgical site or a brace line. If the area is not accessible, the abdomen works fine and the effect is still systemic.
Note 02
Injection Basics
  • Pinch the skin, 45–90°, slow push. Fresh needle every time.
  • Two morning shots go in two different spots. Never combine them in one syringe.
  • Rotate sites so no spot takes a shot two days running.
  • A small bruise or a bead of blood is normal. Spreading redness, heat or a lump that grows is not — stop and call.
Note 03
If You Miss One
Skip it and carry on with the next scheduled dose. Never double up. A missed morning does not become an extra evening shot, and a missed Friday does not move to Saturday — the weekend off is part of the design.
Note 04
What Four Weeks Looks Like
Most of the change shows up in weeks 2 and 3 — less swelling first, then range of motion, then the pain floor dropping. Keep moving within what your provider has cleared. These compounds accelerate repair of tissue that is being used; they do not replace rehab.
