The main risk
Losing Weight Too Fast

Faster is not better here, and on Retatrutide it is genuinely easy to overshoot. You are aiming for roughly 1.2 lb a week. If the scale is dropping more than 1.5 lb a week, you are eating too little and some of what you are losing is the muscle you came here to build.

The fix is always food, not dose. Eat more — particularly protein — before you consider changing the Retatrutide. And weigh yourself the same way every time: first thing, after the bathroom, before eating.

Alcohol
3–7 Drinks a Week, Against These Goals

Not heavy drinking, but worth knowing what it costs against what you specifically asked for. Alcohol suppresses overnight growth hormone release — which is the exact pulse CJC/Ipamorelin is dosed before bed to produce. A drink close to bedtime on a night you dose is money spent against itself.

It also fragments deep sleep, and you rated sleep 3 out of 5 on 7 hours — which is the thing cycle 3 is trying to fix. You do not need to quit. Keep it away from your dosing window, and cluster it into fewer nights rather than spreading it across the week.

Training

Three to four resistance sessions a week is enough, provided they are hard and progressive. The growth signal from CJC/Ipamorelin and IGF-1 LR3 needs a mechanical stimulus to point at — without training that gets progressively harder, those two compounds have nothing to act on. Time your IGF-1 LR3 blocks to your heaviest training weeks.

Notes & Tips
Your First Time — Start Here
Never injected before
The Part That Sounds Worse Than It Is

These are subcutaneous injections — into the fat layer just under the skin, not into muscle. The needle is 31 gauge and half an inch: thinner than a human hair. Most people describe the first one as anticlimactic.

How to do it: pinch an inch of skin on your stomach, two inches clear of the navel. Insert at 45 degrees, all the way. Push the plunger slowly. Count to five, withdraw, press with a clean pad. That is the whole procedure.

Rotate sites — left and right stomach, left and right love handle, moving an inch or two each time. Using the same spot builds scar tissue that slows absorption.

Reconstitution
Mixing Without Wrecking the Peptide

Aim the bacteriostatic water at the side wall of the vial, not down onto the powder, and let it run down. Then swirl gently or roll the vial between your palms. Never shake. Peptides are fragile chains and agitation breaks them — a shaken vial is a weaker vial.

Let it sit a minute or two until fully clear. Once mixed, everything goes in the fridge. Reconstituted peptides are generally good for about four weeks refrigerated.

Start order

Do not start twelve compounds on one day. If something disagrees with you, you want to know which one it was.

Important

Strattera. The cognitive block works on overlapping neurotransmitter systems. That does not make them incompatible, but it is the reason cycle 2 gets added one compound at a time — and the reason Adamax, the most stimulating of the three, goes last. Tell your prescriber what you are running.

Finasteride. Nothing on this page is a hormone or an androgen, so there is no direct interaction. Worth mentioning to your physician anyway.

Pollen allergy. No cross-reactivity with anything here. But if you ever get a rash, swelling or breathing difficulty after a dose, stop and seek care rather than working out which compound it was.

You have a primary care physician. Good — they should know about this. Worth getting baseline bloodwork before you start, so you have something to compare against in eight weeks.

What to expect
A Realistic Eight Weeks

Week 1–2: appetite noticeably down from the Retatrutide. Possible mild nausea for a day after each shot. Little visible change.

Week 2–4: scale starts moving, around a pound a week. Sleep quality often shifts first from the CJC/Ipamorelin — that is usually the earliest sign anything is working.

Week 4–8: the recomposition becomes visible. Weight down modestly, but the mirror ahead of the scale — which is the whole point of running three muscle-protective compounds alongside one fat-loss compound.

At week 8: take photos and measurements, not just weight. At your goal, +3 lb of muscle and −10 lb of fat nets out to roughly −7 lb on the scale. If you only track the scale you will undersell your own result.
