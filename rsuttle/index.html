Personalized Protocol
Randy's Two-Cycle Reset
Sixteen weeks in two eight-week blocks — strip fat and build while you do it, then rebuild sleep and take the stress down.
The target
197 lb down to 170, with the muscle you have protected on the way. That is a 27 lb move, and the first cycle is built to do the bulk of it — a GLP-1 driving appetite and burn, a growth-axis pair holding lean tissue through the deficit, and a fat-loss fragment working the fasted window.
Why sleep comes second, not first
You are sleeping 6.5 hours and rating it 3 out of 5, with stress at the same. That matters — poor sleep raises the hormones that hold fat on and blunts recovery. Cycle 2 is a dedicated block for it rather than an afterthought, because fixing sleep after the weight has started moving tends to stick better than trying to fix both at once.
The joint and muscle pain
The CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin pair in Cycle 1 works the recovery side while you are training 3–4 times a week, and better sleep in Cycle 2 does more for aches than most people expect. If specific joints are still complaining at the end of the sixteen weeks, that is the point to add a dedicated repair compound rather than now.
First time injecting
Every dose here is subcutaneous with an insulin syringe — the smallest needle in routine use, into the fat layer of the abdomen. The Notes & Tips tab walks you through it step by step. Read that before day one; it is genuinely easier than it sounds.

Overview
How the Sixteen Weeks Fit Together

Cycle 1 is the working block — one weekly Retatrutide shot on Monday, a nightly growth-axis dose Monday to Friday, and a morning fat-loss dose before training. Cycle 2 drops to an evening routine built around sleep, with Selank on hand in the day when stress needs it. Nothing overlaps between the two blocks, so you finish one before starting the next.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials, before mixing
Fridge at 36–46°F. Never freeze, keep them out of the light. Sealed, they are stable for months.
After you mix a vial
Straight back in the fridge. Only mix the vial you are currently using. A reconstituted vial has a shorter shelf life than a sealed one, so there is no upside to mixing ahead.
Mixing technique
Wipe the top with an alcohol pad, draw the bacteriostatic water, and let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not blast it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear; never shake hard.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — First Time Injecting
It Is Easier Than It Looks
What a subcutaneous shot actually is
Every dose on this protocol goes into the fat layer just under the skin — not into muscle, not into a vein. The needle is an insulin syringe: the smallest in routine clinical use, about half an inch and thinner than a pin. Most people are surprised how little there is to it.
Step by step
1. Wash your hands. 2. Wipe the vial top and the injection site with an alcohol pad and let both dry. 3. Pull the plunger to the unit mark on the card, push the needle through the vial stopper and draw. 4. Flick out any air bubble and push it back to your mark. 5. Pinch an inch of skin on your abdomen, two inches clear of the navel, and go in at 45–90°. 6. Push slowly, count to three, withdraw. 7. Needle straight into the sharps container.
Rotate the site
Move an inch or two each time — left side, right side, higher, lower. Same spot every day is what causes lumps and soreness.
Normal and fine
A brief sting going in, a pinprick of blood, a small red mark that fades within the hour. All expected.
Read the unit line, every time
The doses on this protocol are not the same number. Retatrutide climbs from 10 to 40 units; CJC/Ipa, AOD and DSIP are 10; Epithalon is 12; Pinealon and Selank are 20. Glance at the barrel before every shot rather than working from memory.
Tip 02 — Getting the Most From Cycle 1
Protein, Walking and the Scale
Eat the protein anyway
Retatrutide will quiet your appetite considerably. The risk on a GLP-1 is not eating too much, it is eating too little and losing muscle along with the fat. Aim for a solid protein serving at each meal even on the days you are not hungry — that is what the CJC/Ipa pair is there to protect.
Keep training at 3–4 a week
You are already there. Do not add volume because the weight is moving; resistance work is what signals the body to keep muscle while the deficit does its job.
Weigh weekly, not daily
Same day, same time, after the bathroom, before food. Daily weight swings on water and will make a good week look flat. Target is roughly 1–2 lb a week — 15–20 lb over the eight weeks is a realistic Cycle 1 outcome.
Alcohol
At 3–7 drinks a week you are not far off, but alcohol blunts both the fat loss and the sleep this protocol is trying to fix. Trimming toward the low end will show up in both cycles.
Tip 03 — Your Current Medications
What Stays, What to Watch
Fish oil, turmeric, digestive enzymes
All fine to continue alongside this protocol. The digestive enzymes may be genuinely useful in Cycle 1 — a GLP-1 slows stomach emptying, and some people find food sits heavier for the first week or two.
65 mg iron
Keep taking it as directed, but flag it at your upcoming bloodwork — ferritin and a full blood count will show whether the dose is still the right one. Iron is one to have measured rather than assumed.
The blood-pressure supplement you ran out of
Worth raising with your PCP before day one rather than simply leaving it lapsed, especially heading into a fat-loss block. Weight coming off often moves blood pressure on its own, which is a conversation worth having with them in advance.
Tip 04 — Red Flags
Signals to Watch For — Reach Out First

Pause and reach out if any of these show up:

· Redness, warmth or swelling at an injection site that is getting worse rather than better
· Persistent vomiting, or nausea severe enough that you cannot keep fluids down
· Severe or persistent abdominal pain, particularly upper abdominal pain that radiates to the back
· Any rash, hives or difficulty breathing after a shot — treat as an allergic reaction and stop
· Light-headedness or a racing heart that does not settle
Do not push through it
Nothing on this protocol is worth forcing. If something feels off in a way you cannot name, hold the dose and ask before the next one — and do not restart on your own after a pause.
Your PCP stays in the loop
You are under their care and that does not change here. Tell them what you are running, and take the upcoming bloodwork results to them.
