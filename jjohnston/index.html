Jeremy's Muscle & Energy Stack
Build muscle again — and fuel the 12-hour days.
The idea
You still train and eat like an athlete, but two things have crept in with age — muscle is harder to build, and the energy to run 12-hour days isn't automatic anymore. This stack rebuilds both and carries you through the long days.
How they work together
CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin is the muscle engine — a growth-hormone releasing pair taken pre-bed that restores the nightly GH pulse that fades after 40, so your training turns back into lean muscle and recovery. NAD+ and MOTS-c are the energy layer: NAD+ refuels the mitochondria and cellular-repair machinery that decline with age, and MOTS-c switches on the AMPK pathway for steady, all-day fuel — together they carry you through the long days. Retatrutide trims visceral fat and lifts metabolism so the muscle you build actually shows.
The rhythm
Mornings are fasted — MOTS every weekday, NAD+ on Mon / Wed / Fri, Reta once a week on Monday. CJC/Ipamorelin goes in pre-bed, fasted, every weekday for the nightly GH pulse. Weekends off. Run everything till the vials are empty, then reassess.
What changed
Your NAD+ is now a range rather than one fixed number — 75 to 100 mg, a 37.5 to 50 unit draw — so you can dial it to how you're feeling that morning. Start at 75 mg (37.5u); take the full 50u on the days you want the bigger hit and the flush is sitting easy. Same vial, same recon, same three mornings a week. Two fresh vials are on their way.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial At A Time
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps a long time, which is why the two reorder NAD+ vials can just wait.
After mixing
Refrigerate. Reconstitute one vial at a time — open a fresh vial only as the last one empties.
Reconstitution amounts
Retatrutide 3 mL per 30 mg vial · CJC/Ipamorelin 2 mL · MOTS-c 3 mL each vial · NAD+ 5 mL each vial. Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — don't squirt it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix: Reta 20u, CJC/Ipa 5u, MOTS-c 15u, NAD+ 37.5–50u. Add the mix date to the NAD+ vials — those are the ones the calendar retires, not the plunger.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites. Work around the abdominal ring rather than reusing one favourite spot.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Note 01 — The Daily Routine
A Morning Block + One Pre-Bed Shot
  • Two blocks a day, all fasted. AM every weekday: MOTS-c. Add NAD+ on Mon / Wed / Fri, and Reta on Monday. PM every weekday: CJC/Ipamorelin, right before bed.
  • Wait 30–45 min before eating after the morning block. Weekends are off entirely — both blocks.
  • Batch the morning shots, don't spread them out. The busiest morning is Monday at three. Draw and give them back to back in one sitting — different spots, same five minutes.
  • NAD+ goes last in the Monday / Wednesday / Friday block, so it gets its own slow minute instead of being rushed.
Note 02 — CJC / Ipamorelin
Pre-Bed, Empty Stomach
  • This is the muscle piece, and timing is everything. CJC/Ipamorelin amplifies the growth-hormone pulse your body releases in deep sleep — the same pulse that fades after 40 and makes muscle harder to build.
  • Right before bed, on an empty stomach — at least 2 hrs after your last meal. Carbs and fat spike insulin, which blunts the GH release and wastes the dose.
  • Deeper sleep is usually the first thing you notice. The muscle and recovery gains build over the weeks alongside your training.
Note 03 — NAD+
Push It Slow
  • NAD+ is the one you actually feel going in. A warm flush, chest or gut pressure, sometimes a brief anxious feeling if it is pushed fast. It is dose- and speed-dependent, not dangerous, and it passes in a minute or two.
  • Your dose is a range — 75 to 100 mg, which is 37.5u to 50u on the syringe. Pick the number by how you're feeling that morning rather than treating either end as the one correct dose.
  • Take 30–60 seconds on the plunger. That is the single biggest lever, and it matters more the closer you are to 50u. If a flush still lands strong, split the dose across two sites.
  • Start at 75 mg (37.5u) and work up. On the mornings you want the bigger hit and the flush is sitting easy, take the full 100 mg (50u). Dropping back down is not a setback — that is what the range is for.
Note 04 — Week-by-Week
What To Expect
  • Weeks 1–2 — energy comes first: NAD+ and MOTS-c during the day, deeper sleep from the pre-bed CJC/Ipa. Reta starts trimming appetite.
  • Weeks 3–5 — the GH work shows up: better recovery from training, fuller muscles, and steady fat loss from Reta so the muscle starts to show.
  • Weeks 6–10+ — the muscle-and-energy window. With the reorder in hand, the NAD+ now runs about three months rather than emptying first. Run everything till the vials are empty, then reassess.
