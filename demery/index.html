Personalized Protocol
Dayan's Two-Cycle Protocol
Eleven weeks, two cycles, back to back. Fat loss first, then the longevity block.
Your goals
Longevity and anti-aging is the primary target — the metabolism of your twenties and skin that makes its own collagen. 145 to 125 runs first, because the fat loss is what the longevity cycle then gets to work on top of.
How it runs
Cycle 1 is eight weeks — weekly Retatrutide, weeknight Tesamorelin, weekday-morning GHK-Cu. Cycle 2 is a 20-day block of five compounds that all stop by design. No overlap between the two.

Build The Plate Around These
  • Protein at every meal — eggs, chicken, fish, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese. Start the day with 30 g.
  • Bone broth or collagen powder — glycine and proline are what GHK-Cu is signalling your fibroblasts to use.
  • Vitamin C with the protein — peppers, citrus, strawberries, broccoli. Same meal, not the same supplement.
  • Whole food, minimally processed — vegetables, whole fruit, olive oil, nuts. Nothing exotic required.
  • Iron and zinc — red meat, shellfish, lentils. Both run low after delivery and both feed skin and hair.
  • Keep taking the folic acid. Nothing on this page interacts with it.
Movement, As You Recover
  • Walking is the whole programme for now — 20–30 minutes most days, built up gradually.
  • No core work, no lifting, no impact until your provider clears you at your post-op visit.
  • Once cleared, add two light resistance sessions a week. Muscle is what protects the 20 lb from coming back.

Reconstitution & Storage
Mixing, Then Keeping It Cold
💧 Mixing — One Cycle At A Time
Cycle 1
Retatrutide 3 mL · Tesamorelin 2 mL · GHK-Cu 3 mL, each per vial.
Cycle 2
Epitalon 3 mL · Pinealon 1 mL · Cortagen 2 mL · Cerebrolysin 3 mL · FOXO4-DRI 2 mL, each per vial.
How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
It looks like nothing
A faint film at the bottom is normal, not a short vial. Everything mixes clear except the GHK-Cu, which turns blue — that is the copper.
Storage
Dry vials at 36–46°F, out of light, never frozen. Once mixed, use within about 30 days. Later-cycle vials stay dry in the box until that cycle opens.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix — and on the Retatrutide, write the week too, since the ladder changes every Monday.
Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01
Before You Start — Clear It First
You are a few weeks out from a C-section. Get your post-op clearance before day 1, and if you are breastfeeding or planning to, say so before anything here is mixed — that changes the plan. Nothing on this page is urgent enough to run ahead of that conversation.
Note 02
Injection Basics
  • Subcutaneous, abdomen — pinch the skin, 45–90°, slow push. Stay clear of the incision line entirely.
  • Rotate quadrants every shot. Two draws in one morning go in two different spots.
  • Fresh needle every time, and let the alcohol dry before the needle goes in.
  • A small bruise or a bead of blood is normal. Spreading redness, heat or a lump that grows is not — stop and call.
Note 03
The First Retatrutide Weeks
Mild nausea in the 24–48 hours after the Monday shot is the known profile and usually settles by the second or third dose. Smaller meals, more water, and hit the protein number even on the days you are not hungry. If a rung is genuinely rough, drop back one and hold — a dose you tolerate for eight weeks beats one you quit in week five.
Note 04
The 20-Day Blocks Stop On Purpose
Epitalon, Pinealon, Cortagen and Cerebrolysin all run as short consecutive courses and then stop. Do not extend one because powder is left in the vial — the block itself is the mechanism. There is nothing to feel while you take them; they are judged in the weeks after.
Note 05
If You Miss One
  • Retatrutide — take it within 2 days of the missed Monday, then go back to Mondays. More than that, skip it and resume at the same rung.
  • Tesamorelin — skip it. Never double up, and never take it on a full stomach.
  • A block compound — skip the day and carry on. One missed day does not restart the block.
