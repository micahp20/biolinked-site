Personal Protocol
Micah's Personal Stack
TRT, Tirzepatide & HGH as the year-round foundation · two AM orals · a four-peptide night block
Compounds
The active stack runs in two windows: an AM block of TRT, Tirzepatide and HGH plus two orals — a BPC-157 tablet and Dihexa — and a night block of DSIP, injectable BPC-157, KPV and Ipamorelin alongside the M/W/F HCG. Everything that came off the stack is kept in Prior Protocol · Reference at the foot of this tab.
Cadence
Each has its own cadence — daily, three days a week M/W/F, or twice-weekly M/Th. The four night peptides run nightly.
Cycle
HGH runs 2 iu fasted every morning, year-round. TRT and Tirzepatide are the other two ongoing pieces — nothing on the current stack is on an 8-week clock.
Daily support
Plus a layered daily-meds + supplements stack built around an intermittent-fasting 12 PM – 7 PM eating window.

Dose Timing · Common Practice
Is the AM + PM timing OK?
Short version
dosing HGH fasted in the morning is a common, accepted approach — the two standard GH windows are fasted-AM and fasted-pre-bed, and the literature finds both work (morning aligns cleanly with your fasted-wake routine). The principle that actually protects the dose is the empty stomach: insulin/carbohydrate blunt GH, so take the morning HGH on waking before any food and hold off eating ~30–60 min. Inject each compound at a separate, rotated site.
PM block
HCG
HCG is more commonly dosed in the morning — a minority of men report nighttime stimulation / vivid dreams / lighter sleep from evening HCG. That sleep point stands on its own; if you notice it, move HCG to AM. It is now the only PM shot.
Kept compounds
TRT and Tirzepatide are timing-neutral — long ester and long half-life respectively, so the AM slot is for convenience rather than pharmacology.
For the research peptides here (HCG's adjunct use), timing guidance comes from clinics, vendors, and community practice — not controlled time-of-day trials.
