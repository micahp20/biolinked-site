Is the AM + PM timing OK?
Short version
dosing HGH fasted in the morning is a common, accepted approach — the two standard GH windows are fasted-AM and fasted-pre-bed, and the literature finds both work (morning aligns cleanly with your fasted-wake routine). The principle that actually protects the dose is the empty stomach: insulin/carbohydrate blunt GH, so take the morning HGH on waking before any food and hold off eating ~30–60 min. Inject each compound at a separate, rotated site.
PM block
Cortagen · Vesugen · HCG
HCG is more commonly dosed in the morning — a minority of men report nighttime stimulation / vivid dreams / lighter sleep from evening HCG. That sleep point stands on its own; if you notice it, move HCG to AM. Cortagen is AM-by-vendor-convention (cognitive/cortical target), but nothing prohibits evening. Vesugen is the most flexible — vendors say any time, consistency over clock.
Kept compounds
NAD+ is correctly in the AM — it's stimulating and is the one compound with a genuine “avoid the evening” signal (insomnia); sharing the AM window with fasted HGH is fine. Melanotan sits in your AM block, though many actually prefer it in the evening so the flush/nausea is slept through — a candidate to move to PM if it bothers you. TRT and Retatrutide are timing-neutral (long esters/half-life).
For the research peptides here (HCG's adjunct use, Cortagen, Vesugen), timing guidance comes from clinics, vendors, and community practice — not controlled time-of-day trials.
