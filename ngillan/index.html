Nic's Vitality + Strength Stack
Feel alive. Stay strong. Build muscle. Get back in the weight room.
This is the comeback stack — lean version
Goal you said it best: feel alive, stay strong, have energy, build muscle, get back in the weight room. Five compounds running this cycle, all AM, focused on the metabolic + cellular foundation: body comp leaning out, daytime energy floor lifted, fat oxidation amplified, muscle satellite cells re-activated. Two more (KLOW + CJC/Ipa) are queued up as later additions — we layer them in when you're ready to push training harder and want the recovery + GH pulse working alongside the metabolic base.
Retatrutide
(20u / ~2.67 mg once weekly Mon AM fasted) — triple-agonist GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon. The fat-loss + body-comp anchor. Once-weekly cadence matches Reta's long half-life cleanly. Tier up or down as needed — we'll watch how the body responds and adjust the dose without changing anything else in the stack.
MOTS-c
(1 mg M–F AM fasted) — mitochondrial-encoded peptide that activates AMPK, drives fat oxidation, protects lean mass during the cut, and lifts cellular energy. The "feel alive" lever.
5-Amino-1MQ
(~1.67 mg M–F AM fasted) — NNMT inhibitor that frees up NAD+ in adipose + muscle tissue. Accelerates the Reta-driven fat-loss curve AND activates dormant muscle satellite cells (more raw material for building back).
NAD+
(50 mg M/W/F AM, push slow) — cellular fuel for mitochondrial ATP, sirtuin activation, DNA repair. The energy floor underneath everything. After 30, NAD drops every year — restoring it noticeably lifts daytime energy and recovery capacity.
Semax
(500 mcg / 10u M/W/F AM) — synthetic ACTH(4-10) heptapeptide. Drives BDNF + NGF upregulation, modulates dopamine + serotonin signaling. The cognitive enhancement layer — while NAD lifts the mitochondrial floor and MOTS lifts the cellular energy floor, Semax lifts the mental floor. Same M/W/F days as Reta + NAD.
How the cycle runs
MOTS + 5-Amino M–F (all five days). NAD + Semax M/W/F (three days/wk). Reta Mon only (once weekly — long half-life, doesn't need M/W/F splits). Sat/Sun off everything. Heaviest day (Mon): 5 AM shots. Wed/Fri: 4 AM shots. Tue/Thu: 2 AM shots. No PM shots this cycle (CJC/Ipa is on the "also considered" list below). Under-5-minute morning routine.

Most people acclimate after 3–4 shots.
5-Amino-1MQ · 50 mg Metabolic ?NNMT inhibitor. Frees up NAD+ in fat and muscle tissue, accelerating fat oxidation while re-activating dormant muscle satellite cells — the "build muscle" lever.
Recon3 mL bacteriostatic water
ScheduleAM fasted · M–F (Sat/Sun off)
Dose10u · ~1.67 mg
Why it's hereNNMT inhibitor. NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase) is an enzyme that accumulates in adipose and muscle tissue with age — it sequesters NAD+ precursors into a methylated dead-end, suppressing fat oxidation AND keeping muscle satellite cells dormant. 5-Amino-1MQ blocks NNMT, which does two things at once: (1) frees up NAD+ to fuel fat oxidation (compounds with Reta and MOTS), and (2) reactivates dormant muscle satellite cells — the precursor cells that have to come online for new muscle to be built. This is the "build muscle" lever specifically for someone getting back to training: it gives the body more raw material to lay down lean tissue when training stimulus is applied.
Stack synergyReta creates the deficit. 5-Amino keeps the fat Reta liberates actually getting oxidized (instead of recirculating). MOTS activates AMPK. NAD raises the cellular substrate floor. The four together compound the body comp signal substantially harder than Reta alone — cleaner cut, more lean tissue retained, faster recovery between training sessions.

Storage

Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Reconstituted vials
refrigerate immediately after mixing. Stable for ~30 days reconstituted. Mix each vial only when you're ready to start using it — staggered timing keeps every vial inside its 30-day stability window.

Tip 02 — Reta Tier Up or Down
How We Iterate the Dose
Starting dose
20u (~2.67 mg) once weekly Mon AM fasted. Conservative-mid range for a triple agonist — appetite suppression real but not crushing, GI side effects manageable, body comp shift starts cleanly. Once-weekly cadence matches Reta's ~6-day half-life, so blood levels stay steady without needing a split-dose schedule.
When to tier UP
· Appetite suppression is barely noticeable past Wk 2
· Fat loss has stalled (less than 0.5 lb/wk for 2+ weeks) and protein intake is on point
· You're at 20u without any GI side effects whatsoever — the body's adapted, room to go up
Tier up path: 20u (~2.67 mg) → 25u (~3.33 mg) → 30u (~4 mg). Step up one notch, hold for 2 weeks, reassess.
When to tier DOWN
· Persistent nausea past Wk 2
· Fat loss faster than 2 lbs/wk sustained (deficit too aggressive — eat more or drop dose)
· Energy crashing or training suffering
Tier down: 20u (~2.67 mg) → 15u (~2 mg) → 10u (~1.33 mg). Pull back one notch, hold for 2 weeks, reassess.
The point
Reta isn't a fixed dose. We start at 20u, watch how the body responds for 2–3 weeks, then adjust. Message me when you have data — how the appetite feels, what the scale is doing, how energy and training are holding up.
Tip 03 — NAD Slow-Push Technique
The Whole Trick Is the Plunger Speed
NAD SubQ can cause warmth, flushing, or a brief "hot" sensation in the chest, neck, or face during or right after the shot. It's harmless — the body clearing the bolus — and usually passes in 1–5 minutes. The fix is pure technique:
Push the plunger slowly
Aim for 15–30 seconds to deliver the full 25 units. Not 3 seconds, not "as fast as the other shots." Slow push gives the tissue time to absorb the bolus gradually — less flushing, less sting.
If flushing is still uncomfortable
slow the push even more next time (30–45 seconds), or split it — deliver half, pause 10 seconds, deliver the rest. After 3–4 shots the body usually acclimates and the flushing fades almost entirely.
The 50 mg dose math
at 5 mL recon (200 mg/mL), 50 mg lands at a clean 25-unit pull (0.25 mL). Easy mark to hit on the syringe.
Tip 04 — Training + Protein for the "Build Muscle" Goal
The Peptides Multiply What You Bring
The peptides don't build muscle on their own
They amplify the signals your body already has when you put real training stress on muscle + give it real protein to rebuild with. Without those two inputs, the stack drives recovery and energy but leaves the "build muscle" goal unfunded.
Protein floor
aim for 1 g per lb of bodyweight per day, every day. Front-load earlier in the day (breakfast, mid-morning shake) since Reta will suppress appetite later. A whey or casein shake within an hour of waking is the most reliable way to hit the floor when appetite is dialed down.
Training
3–4 lifting sessions/week is the right intensity for getting back in. Compound lifts (squat, deadlift, press, row) drive the systemic anabolic signal. Start lower than you think you should — coming back to lifting hard from time off is when people get hurt. The metabolic base (Reta + MOTS + 5-Amino + NAD) supports recovery, but the dedicated connective-tissue layer (KLOW) and the overnight GH pulse (CJC/Ipa) are on the "also considered" list — if joints start barking or recovery between sessions feels slow, those are the obvious layers to add.
Hydration
2.5–3 L/day. Reta suppresses thirst signaling — you won't feel thirsty even when dehydrated. Headaches, fatigue, poor training performance — usually water deficit, not the peptide. Add electrolytes once daily (LMNT, Liquid IV, similar).
Sleep
7–8 hrs target every night. Sleep depth is the lever everything else compounds on top of — mitochondrial repair, glucose regulation, lean tissue rebuild. No CJC/Ipa this cycle yet, so you don't have the GH-pulse sleep deepening assist; that's exactly the gap that compound closes when we add it.
Tip 05 — Missed Doses
What to Do If You Skip
Reta
Mon, 1×/wk
miss Mon morning, take it Tue AM if within 24 hours; otherwise push to next Mon. Don't double up. Once a week means the dose isn't in a hurry — better to delay 1–2 days than skip a week.
MOTS / 5-Amino
M–F
miss one day, skip it. Don't double up. Resume the next scheduled day. Missing 1–2 doses across the cycle doesn't move the needle.
NAD
M/W/F
miss a scheduled day, skip it. Resume the next M/W/F day. One missed dose doesn't move the cellular energy floor.
Tip 06 — Red Flags
When to Pause
Hold the next Reta dose if
· Persistent nausea or vomiting that doesn't resolve within 24 hours
· Severe abdominal pain, especially radiating to the back — rule out pancreatitis
· Gallbladder-area pain or right-upper-quadrant pain after fatty meals
· Rapid weight loss above 3 lbs/week sustained — deficit is too aggressive, eat more
· Lightheadedness or dizziness on standing — could be blood sugar or dehydration; hydrate, eat, monitor
Hold MOTS / 5-Amino if
significant injection-site reaction (redness, heat, swelling past 24 hrs), or any allergic-type symptoms (hives, throat tightness, breathing changes). Both are well-tolerated; individual reactions are rare but possible.
Hold NAD if
severe flushing that doesn't resolve in 5–10 minutes despite slowing the plunger, chest tightness, racing heart, or anxiety-type symptoms post-injection, persistent injection-site reaction past 24 hours.
Any of these symptoms, text Micah first
