Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Rotated
Dry (lyophilized) vials
store refrigerated at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Reconstituted vials
refrigerate immediately after adding BAC water. Use within 30 days. Reconstitute one vial of each compound at a time — don't start the 30-day clock on a second vial until the first is close to empty.
Syringe discipline
fresh U-100 insulin syringe for every shot. Never reuse. Wipe stopper + injection site with alcohol pad every time.
Site rotation
SubQ abdomen rotates between 4 quadrants. Never inject the same 1″ spot two days in a row.
Protocol Notes
Execution
Order 2 — Critical
Two Different BPC-157 Courses
Never on the same day
Both are now 1 mg (20u) Mon–Fri PM for four weeks — same peptide, same dose, two separate courses from different vials. The numbers will not tell them apart, so go by which block you are in: the Phase 1 block sits inside the 12-week Order 1 protocol, the gut course is its own month. Run one or the other, never both.
Separate calendars
Order 1 and Order 2 have their own .ics downloads on the Schedule tab so the two BPC blocks never land on the same date.
Order 2 — NAD+
Push Slowly
30–60 seconds on the plunger
That is the single biggest lever on how NAD+ feels. Flushing, a warm wave or chest pressure means it went in too fast — it passes in a few minutes and eases as you go.
75 mg = 37.5u
1000 mg in 5 mL is 200 mg/mL, so 75 mg is 0.375 mL. Mon / Wed / Fri AM.
Fasted State
What Fasted Actually Means
Water and black coffee only
Nothing with calories for 30–45 minutes after the shot. Applies to SS-31, MOTS-C, Reta and the evening gut shots.
Reta
Frequency Before Dose
If energy or GI drops off
Cut frequency before you cut the dose — skip Thursday in the loading phase, or Wednesday in steady state. Only drop to 0.5 mg (5u) if that is not enough.
GI in the first 1–2 weeks
Nausea and fullness are expected early and settle. Smaller meals and slower eating do most of the work.
Bioregulator Courses
Finish the Course, Then Stop
Cartalax and BPC run in fixed blocks
Cartalax is a 20-day Phase 1 course; the Order 2 BPC-157 is a four-week course. Do not extend one because powder is left — the effect is judged in the weeks after the block, not during it.
TRT
Unchanged
~150–200 mg/wk stays as-is
Self-managed with Joe's physician. Nothing in either order changes the dose or the timing.
