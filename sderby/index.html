Protocol Overview — How These Four Work Together
Tesamorelin — Targeted Body Recomposition
A GHRH analog that nudges your body's own nightly growth-hormone pulse, which drives preferential loss of deep, hard-to-reach fat — the kind that sits deep around the core and is the last to respond to diet and training alone. Of everything in this stack, it's the compound with the most direct, named effect on targeted body recomposition. It works on a longer timeline than the others — which is exactly why the plan is to run it past 8 weeks.
Retatrutide — The Intake Side
A triple receptor agonist (GLP-1, GIP, glucagon) — it quiets appetite and food noise so you eat less without fighting it, and the glucagon arm tends to raise energy expenditure rather than blunt it. Reta decides how much comes off; Tesa decides where. Titrated up by feel so you find the dose your body is comfortable with.
KPV — The Inflammation
A small anti-inflammatory tripeptide. It calms the low-grade, systemic inflammation that quietly works against fat loss and recovery — the kind you can feel but can't always point to. Run at night, it also fits the recovery window. Lowering that background inflammation makes the deficit and the training both land better.
MOTS-c — The Energy & Output
A mitochondrial-derived peptide. It signals your cells to build more mitochondria and run the ones they have more efficiently, which raises energy expenditure and shows up first as steadier daytime energy and better output in the gym. Fasted, five mornings a week.
Why these four together: Reta cuts what goes in. MOTS-c raises what gets burned and gives you the energy to train. KPV lowers the inflammation that fights both. And Tesamorelin aims all of it at the deep, hard-to-reach fat — the trouble area you actually care about — on the longer timeline that real recomposition needs. It's a complete picture: eat less, burn more, calm the system down, and point the results where you want them.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Single-Use
Dry vials (before reconstitution)
store refrigerated at 36–46°F. Never freeze. Protect from light. Mix one KPV vial and one Tesa vial at a time — leave the extras dry in the fridge until you finish the one you're on. A dry vial keeps far longer than a mixed one.
Reconstituted vials
refrigerate immediately after mixing. Each vial only has to last its own window (MOTS-c 8 weeks, each KPV 4 weeks, each Tesa 2 weeks), all well inside shelf life.
Syringe discipline
fresh single-use syringe for every draw, no exceptions. Needle reuse is the most common cause of injection-site reactions — and at ~16 shots a week it matters.
Site rotation
rotate between lower abdomen (a fingerbreadth from the navel), outer thigh, and love handles. Never the same 1-inch spot two days running. When you're doing two shots at once (AM Monday, or any evening), put them in two different sites.

Getting the Most Out of It
Consistency · Protein · Hydration
Tip 04 — The Real Job
Consistency Beats Everything Else Here
This is a four-compound stack with a morning routine and an evening routine. The hard part isn't any single shot — it's showing up every day. MOTS-c, KPV, and Tesamorelin are all build-up compounds; they work by accumulating, so a missed day here and there is nothing, but skipping regularly means you paid for something you're not really running.
Download the .ics from the Schedule tab and let your phone carry the reminders — AM and PM, with each day's shots and units already filled in. Anchor the shots to things you already do (coffee in the morning, teeth at night) and they stop being a decision.
Tip 05 — Protein Floor
Keep Protein Up as Appetite Drops on Reta
Reta quiets appetite, which is the point — but it also makes it easy to under-eat protein and lose muscle along with the fat. That's the wrong kind of weight loss, especially at 54 when muscle is already harder to hold onto. Aim for roughly 0.7–0.9 g of protein per pound of goal body weight daily, and front-load it earlier in the day before the appetite suppression peaks.
Protecting your muscle is also what makes the gym work pay off and what keeps your metabolism from sinking as you lose. Eggs and cottage cheese, real meat at meals, a protein shake if you're falling short.
Tip 06 — Hydration & Fiber
Drink More Than You Think — It Heads Off the Side Effects
The two most common Reta side effects are mild nausea and constipation, and both respond well to aggressive hydration (aim for ~2.5–3 L of water a day) and adding fiber (whole foods, or a psyllium husk supplement). Get ahead of them — it's a lot easier to prevent than to fix, and it keeps them from ever becoming a reason to hold a dose.
