The two timing rules that matter

CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin must be fasted. A meal within about two hours blunts the growth hormone pulse and wastes the shot — that is the single easiest way to get nothing out of this protocol. IGF-1 LR3 is your choice: ten consecutive days as a block, or post-workout only on training days. Post-workout stretches two vials considerably further given you train three to four times a week. Pick one approach and stay with it rather than alternating.

If you miss a dose

Take it when you remember, unless it is nearly time for the next one — then skip it. Do not double up. The daily compounds here work on accumulated presence rather than peak concentration, so one missed day costs you very little. The exception is any consecutive-day compound in the later cycles (Epithalon in particular), where an unbroken run genuinely matters.

Notes & Tips
Running This Well
Reconstitution
Mixing Without Wrecking the Peptide

Aim the bacteriostatic water at the side wall of the vial, not straight down onto the powder, and let it run down. Then swirl gently or roll the vial between your palms. Never shake. Peptides are fragile chains and agitation breaks them — a shaken vial is a weaker vial.

Let it sit a minute or two until fully clear. Once mixed, everything goes in the fridge. Reconstituted peptides are generally good for about four weeks refrigerated, which lines up with the roughly 20-day run on most of your ordered vials.

Injection
You Have Some Experience — Here's What Changes

You have run a few cycles before, so the mechanics are not new. What is new is volume — six to seven shots a day is more than most protocols, and site rotation stops mattering in theory and starts mattering in practice.

Rotate across at least four sites — left and right abdomen (two inches clear of the navel), and left and right love handle. Move an inch or two each time. Using the same spot repeatedly builds scar tissue that slows absorption, and with this many injections you will find that out within weeks if you get lazy about it.

Group your shots. All the AM compounds can go in one sitting, all the PM ones in another. You do not need to space them out.
