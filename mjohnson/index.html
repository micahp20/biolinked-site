Michael's Stack
Fat loss, mitochondrial energy, and mental clarity — run as one daily routine.
The stack
Eight compounds — Retatrutide, Tesamorelin, MOTS-c, Semax, Adamax, SS-31, Glutathione, and NAD+ — split across a fasted AM block, one midday shot on M/W/F, and a single fasted PM shot.
The routine
Monday–Friday, weekends off. Mornings carry MOTS-c and SS-31 every weekday, then Semax, Glutathione, and NAD+ on M/W/F, and Retatrutide once a week on Monday. Adamax runs midday on M/W/F, on its own. Evenings are one shot: Tesamorelin, fasted before bed.
How long it runs
Every compound runs until its bottles are empty. Tesamorelin is the long one — 6 vials carry roughly 24 weeks. Glutathione covers ~9–10 weeks on the 4 bottles, MOTS-c and SS-31 ~6 weeks, Semax ~4–6, NAD+ ~3–4, Adamax under 2 weeks on the single vial, and the Reta vial outlasts all of them at once-weekly dosing.

The AM block
MOTS-c and SS-31 go in every weekday morning, fasted, before food or coffee with cream. Semax, Glutathione, and NAD+ join them on M/W/F, and Reta once a week on Monday. That's 2 shots on Tue/Thu, 5 on Wed/Fri, and 6 on Monday. Wait 30–45 minutes before eating.
⚑ The midday Adamax shot
Adamax is its own window on M/W/F and it has two rules. It goes after Semax, never before — Semax is in the fasted morning block, so Adamax follows later the same day. And it does not share a window with MOTS-c or NAD+, both of which live in that morning block. Practically: run the AM block as usual, then take Adamax midday, at least 2 hours later, 20u, on its own, into a fresh site. It doesn't need to be fasted.
M/W/F mornings are the long ones
Five AM shots back to back — six on Monday — is a lot to stand still for — do the small ones first (MOTS-c, SS-31, Semax) and finish with Glutathione and NAD+, the two big slow pushes. Rotate to a fresh spot for each. If you're short on time, NAD+ is the one to move later in the morning; it just needs to be fasted, not first.
The PM shot
Tesamorelin, on its own, ~2 hours after your last meal and close to bed. That empty-stomach window is what lets it stack on your natural overnight GH pulse.
Rotate the sites
With 4 shots on a Monday morning, don't stack them in one spot. Think of your abdomen as four quadrants around the navel and move at least an inch between shots — and to a different quadrant the next day.
Missed a day
Skip it and pick back up the next scheduled day. Don't double up. For the weekly Reta, if Monday slips, take it Tuesday and keep Monday as the anchor going forward.

Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Routine
Three Windows, Five Days a Week
The protocol lives in three moments of the day — two anchors plus one midday shot on M/W/F. Get those anchored and the rest takes care of itself.
  • Morning, before anything else. MOTS-c 10u and SS-31 10u every weekday. Add Semax 10–20u, Glutathione 70u, and NAD+ 37.5u → 50u on M/W/F, and Reta 10u on Monday. Then wait 30–45 minutes before food.
  • Midday on M/W/F — Adamax 20u, by itself. At least 2 hours after the morning block. It always comes after Semax, and it never goes in alongside MOTS-c or NAD+ — that's the whole reason it has its own window. Doesn't need to be fasted.
  • Evening, on an empty stomach. Tesamorelin 10u, roughly 2 hours after your last meal, close to bed.
  • Weekends off. Nothing Saturday or Sunday — that break is part of the design, not a lapse.
  • Draw marks to memorize: 10u covers most of the stack — MOTS-c, SS-31, Reta, Tesamorelin, and the low Semax dose. 20u is the top Semax dose. The two big ones are 50u NAD+ (37.5u your first week) and 70u Glutathione — push both in slowly, 30–60 seconds on the plunger.
Tip 02 — Fasted Means Fasted
Why the Empty Stomach Matters
Both windows are fasted for the same underlying reason: insulin blunts the signal you're paying for.
  • Tesamorelin at night works by triggering your own GH pulse. Eating before it — carbs especially — raises insulin and flattens that pulse. Leave ~2 hours after dinner.
  • MOTS-c in the morning activates AMPK, the low-fuel sensor. It has more to work with when your body is actually in a fasted state.
  • Black coffee is fine. Cream, sugar, and protein shakes are not — save them for after the 30–45 minute window.
  • Water is always fine, and you'll want it. Reta blunts thirst, so drink on schedule rather than waiting to feel it.
Tip 03 — How the Stack Fits Together
Eight Compounds, Five Jobs
These aren't eight versions of the same thing — each one is covering a different part of the same goal.
  • Retatrutide handles intake. Appetite and food noise drop, so the deficit happens without white-knuckling it.
  • Tesamorelin handles body composition and recovery — specifically visceral fat, plus deeper sleep and better repair overnight.
  • MOTS-c, SS-31, and NAD+ handle the cellular side. MOTS-c flips your cells toward burning fat, SS-31 repairs the mitochondrial machinery doing the burning, and NAD+ supplies the coenzyme both of them run on. Together they're why the energy holds up while you're in a deficit.
  • Glutathione handles the cleanup. Burning fat fast means more oxidative byproduct and more work for the liver — it neutralizes that load so the rest of the stack can keep working.
  • Semax — and Adamax behind it — handle your head: focus and stress tolerance, the piece most people lose first when they're eating less. They work the same BDNF axis, which is why Adamax is sequenced after Semax in its own midday window rather than stacked into the morning.
Tip 04 — Week by Week
What to Expect
  • Week 1. Appetite starts dropping and portions shrink on their own. Some mild nausea or early fullness from the Reta is normal as your gut adapts. Semax usually shows up first — a lot of people notice the focus within days. NAD+ sits at the lower 37.5u dose all week while you learn how it feels going in.
  • End of week 2 — Adamax is done. The 5 mg vial is only 5 doses: Week 1 Mon/Wed/Fri, Week 2 Mon/Wed. It empties before anything else in the stack. If you noticed something from it on top of the Semax, tell Micah before it runs out.
  • Weeks 2–4. NAD+ steps to 50u. The mitochondrial side comes online: steadier energy through the afternoon, better output in the gym. Sleep often deepens from the Tesamorelin. The scale should be moving consistently by now. NAD+ empties around week 3–4 — it's the shortest bottle in the stack.
  • Around week 6. Semax, SS-31, and MOTS-c all run out within a couple weeks of each other. You'll feel the AM block get shorter — that's expected, not a problem.
  • Weeks 6–10. Glutathione keeps going on M/W/F until the fourth bottle is gone (~week 9–10). You'll need one extra bottle of bacteriostatic water in here to mix the last of it.
  • Weeks 10–24. Tesamorelin at night and Reta on Mondays carry the back half. This is the long, quiet stretch where visceral fat keeps coming off. Check in with Micah around week 8 and we decide what to restock.
Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
  • Hold the Reta if nausea or vomiting persists past 24 hours, or for severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. Don't push through — we drop the dose or pause.
  • Losing too fast. More than ~2 lbs/week sustained is muscle-loss territory — message us and we ease something back.
  • Tingling or numbness in the hands that shows up after starting Tesamorelin, or joint puffiness — both are GH-related and mean we lower the dose.
  • Injection sites. A little redness at the pinhole is normal; spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain means stop that site and message us.
  • Anything that feels off, message first — don't push through or restart on your own.
