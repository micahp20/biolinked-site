Amelia's Four-Cycle Protocol
One goal per cycle, run back to back — healing first.
The order
Cycle 1 — Healing & Recovery, the primary goal. Cycle 2 — Cognitive. Cycle 3 — Longevity. Cycle 4 — Sleep & Stress. They run in sequence, not together.
Why sequential
One goal at a time keeps the daily shot count small and makes each cycle readable — you can tell what worked. Week counts are nominal; a cycle finishes when its vials do.
New to peptides
Every dose here opens at the low end of its range. Cycle 1 is two morning shots and one before bed, all subcutaneous.

Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Note 01 — The Routine
Cycle 1, Day To Day
  • AM: Wolverine 2.0 10u + KPV 20u, two different sites. PM: GHK-Cu 10u before bed.
  • Monday through Friday, weekends off. None of the three needs to be fasted — pick times that fit around your eating window and hold them.
  • Pinch an inch of skin, 45–90 degrees, push slow. Abdomen is the simplest site; rotate quadrants so you are never in the same one-inch spot twice in a day.
  • For a specific joint, any of the three can go SubQ near that area instead of the abdomen to concentrate it locally.
  • Missed a day? Skip it. Back to the normal schedule the next day — never double up to catch up.
Note 02 — Mixing & Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
  • Dry vials: refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
  • Mixing: use the volume on the vial table — it differs per compound, so check it rather than reusing the last one's number.
  • Add the water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it onto the powder. Then swirl gently or roll it between your palms until clear. Never shake.
  • Mix one vial at a time — open the next only as the current one empties.
  • Label every vial you mix with the compound, its draw mark and the date you mixed it. GHK-Cu mixes to a deep blue; that colour is normal.
  • Keep mixed vials refrigerated, back shelf rather than the door. Fresh single-use syringe every shot, for the draw and the injection both.
Note 03 — Sequencing
Why The Cycles Do Not Overlap
  • You can tell what worked. Twelve compounds at once would make it impossible to attribute anything. Two to four at a time and each cycle gives a clean answer.
  • The daily load stays small. Cycle 1 is three shots a day at its heaviest; Cycle 3 is often just one.
  • Healing runs first because it is the primary goal — the rest is built to follow it, not compete with it.
  • Week counts are approximate. A cycle finishes when its vials finish; a week either side is normal.
Note 04 — Baseline Labs
Worth Getting Drawn
  • Thyroid panel — TSH, Free T4, Free T3, TPO and TG antibodies. The one to have in hand, and worth repeating on the same lab so the numbers are comparable.
  • CBC with differential and Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) — the baseline everything else is read against.
  • hs-CRP and ESR — inflammation markers, directly relevant to what Cycle 1 is aimed at.
  • Ferritin, B12 and Vitamin D, 25-Hydroxy — commonly low, cheap to check, and all three affect how tissue heals.
  • Send the results over when you have them and the Bloodwork tab fills in.
