The 12-Month Protocol
A steady hormonal and GH base held all year, with six eight-week cycles rotating on top of it.

Why twelve months. Eight weeks is long enough to feel a compound and short enough that nothing runs open-ended. Stacking six of those cycles on one uninterrupted foundation gives you a full year that moves repair → optimize instead of restarting from zero every time a vial runs out.

The foundation — TRT, HGH and Retatrutide — never changes. On top of it, one mitochondrial lever (SS-31 or MOTS-C) rotates against a support compound, with glutathione or a short pineal course layered on top — so no single compound runs more than eight weeks at a stretch.

How the rotation works

The mitochondrial slot alternates all year — SS-31 in cycles 1, 3 and 5, MOTS-C in 2, 4 and 6 — so neither ever runs two blocks in a row. Underneath, the support slot cycles KLOW → NAD+ → 5-Amino-1MQ and repeats, giving all six pairings exactly once. On top of that, glutathione runs the three SS-31 cycles and a 30-dose Epithalon + Pinealon course runs cycles 2 and 4.

Why these counts

Every vial is counted until it is empty — doses in the vial divided by doses per week, nothing set aside part-used. HGH is the volume driver at 22, simply because a 24 IU vial is twelve 2 IU doses and it runs all 52 weeks. SS-31, MOTS-C and 5-Amino-1MQ each finish a cycle on one vial — forty 7.5u doses, exactly eight weeks of Mon–Fri. KLOW, NAD+ and glutathione take two per cycle. Pinealon is the outlier at twelve: a 10 mg vial is only five 2 mg doses, so a 30-dose course burns six vials, twice over. A 30 mg Retatrutide vial on its own is fifteen weeks of supply.

Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ The Basics
Mixing
Add the BAC water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — never squirt it directly onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Recon volumes
HGH 3 mL · Retatrutide 3 mL · SS-31 3 mL · MOTS-C 3 mL · KLOW 3 mL · NAD+ 5 mL · 5-Amino-1MQ 3 mL · Glutathione 5 mL · Epithalon 3 mL · Pinealon 2 mL. Testosterone is pre-mixed.
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Mixed vials
Refrigerate immediately after adding water and run the vial until it is empty. Mix one vial of each compound at a time — don't open a second until the first is done.
Syringes
Fresh U-100 insulin syringe every single injection. Never reuse.
Site rotation
SubQ shots rotate across the four abdominal quadrants. Never use the same 1″ spot two days running — over 52 weeks this matters more than it does over eight.
