Overview
How the Three Cycles Fit Together

Cycle 1 carries the primary goal — a weekly Retatrutide shot plus two metabolic compounds working insulin sensitivity and visceral fat. Cycle 2 shifts to repair and cellular energy once the weight is moving. Cycle 3 closes on cognition, sleep and libido. Each block finishes before the next begins.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials, before mixing
Fridge at 36–46°F. Never freeze, keep them out of the light.
After you mix a vial
Straight back in the fridge. Only mix the vial you are currently using — a reconstituted vial has a shorter shelf life than a sealed one.
Mixing technique
Wipe the top with an alcohol pad, draw the water, and let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial. Swirl gently until clear; never shake hard.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — First Time Injecting
Easier Than It Looks
What this actually is
Every dose here goes into the fat layer just under the skin — not into muscle, not a vein. The needle is an insulin syringe: about half an inch, thinner than a pin.
Step by step
1. Wash hands. 2. Alcohol-wipe the vial top and the site, let both dry. 3. Pull the plunger to the unit mark on the card and draw. 4. Flick out any bubble, push back to your mark. 5. Pinch an inch of skin on the abdomen, two inches clear of the navel, go in at 45–90°. 6. Push slowly, count to three, withdraw. 7. Needle straight into the sharps container.
Rotate the site
Move an inch or two each time. Same spot daily is what causes lumps.
Check the unit line every time
The doses here are not the same number — Retatrutide climbs 10 to 40, MOTS-C is 12.5, GHK-Cu 15, NAD+ 25, Selank 20, Semax 5. Glance at the barrel before every shot.
Tip 02 — Cycle 1
Protein and the Scale
Eat the protein anyway
Retatrutide will quiet appetite considerably. The risk is eating too little and losing muscle with the fat — a solid protein serving at each meal protects against that.
Weigh weekly, not daily
Same day, same time, before food. Daily swings are water and make a good week look flat.
Start moving, even a little
MOTS-C works the same pathway exercise does. A twenty-minute walk after the morning dose compounds with it — you do not need a gym for this to matter.
Tip 03 — Red Flags
When to Pause

Pause and reach out if any of these show up:

· Redness, warmth or swelling at a site that worsens rather than settles
· Persistent vomiting, or nausea severe enough that fluids will not stay down
· Severe abdominal pain, particularly upper pain that radiates to the back
· Rash, hives or difficulty breathing after a shot — treat as an allergic reaction and stop
Find a physician first
You noted you are not currently under anyone's care. Getting a primary-care doctor in place before Cycle 1 is worth doing — both for the baseline bloodwork and so someone knows what you are running.
