Why 5-Amino-1MQ — the super-charge layer: NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase) is an enzyme that accumulates in fat and muscle tissue with age. It does two bad things: (1) sequesters NAD+ precursors so the body can't efficiently burn fat, and (2) keeps muscle satellite cells dormant so lean tissue can't rebuild. 5-Amino-1MQ blocks NNMT — flipping both switches in your favor.
5-Amino + Reta — the body-comp multiplier. Reta on its own creates the calorie deficit (appetite quiet, glucagon burning) — but a chunk of the fat it liberates from cells just recirculates and gets re-stored if NAD+ is low. 5-Amino frees that NAD+ so the fat Reta liberates actually gets oxidized. The combo super-charges the rate of body-comp change you see week-over-week — faster, cleaner cut than Reta alone.
5-Amino — the muscle-protection layer. Calorie deficits normally pull some lean tissue along with fat. 5-Amino keeps muscle satellite cells active — dormant precursors that have to be online for muscle to maintain or grow. With them firing, the deficit pulls almost entirely from fat instead of muscle. You strip fat fast AND keep your lean tissue intact — the cleanest definition of recomp.

Tip 02 — Reta Tier Up or Down
How We Iterate the Dose

Starting dose: 20u / ~2.67 mg once per week (Monday AM fasted). This is the conservative once-weekly range — appetite suppression real but not crushing, GI side effects manageable, body comp shift starts cleanly. Most people land here.

When to tier UP:

· Appetite suppression is barely noticeable past Wk 2
· Fat loss has stalled (less than 0.5 lb/wk for 2+ weeks) and protein intake is on point
· You're at 20u without any GI side effects whatsoever — the body's adapted, room to go up
Tier up path: 20u (~2.67 mg) → 25u (~3.33 mg) → 30u (~4 mg). Step up one notch, hold for 2 weeks, reassess.

When to tier DOWN:

· Persistent nausea past Wk 2
· Fat loss faster than 2 lbs/wk sustained (deficit too aggressive — eat more or drop dose)
· Energy crashing or training suffering
Tier down: 20u → 15u (~2 mg) → 10u (~1.33 mg). Pull back one notch, hold for 2 weeks, reassess.
The point
Reta isn't a fixed dose. We start at 20u, watch how the body responds for 2–3 weeks, then adjust. Message me when you have data — how the appetite feels, what the scale is doing, how energy and training are holding up.
Tip 03 — Training + Protein for the "Build Muscle" Goal
The Peptides Multiply What You Bring — Bring Real Inputs
The peptides don't build muscle on their own
They amplify the signals your body already has when you put real training stress on muscle + give it real protein to rebuild with. Without those two inputs, the stack drives recovery and energy but leaves the "build muscle" goal unfunded.
Protein floor
aim for 1 g per lb of bodyweight per day, every day. Front-load earlier in the day (breakfast, mid-morning shake) since Reta will suppress appetite later. A whey or casein shake within an hour of waking is the most reliable way to hit the floor when appetite is dialed down.
Training
3–4 lifting sessions/week is the right intensity for getting back in. Compound lifts (squat, deadlift, press, row) drive the systemic anabolic signal. Start lower than you think you should — coming back to lifting hard from time off is when people get hurt. KLOW keeps tendons + connective tissue catching up to the muscle, but it's not magic — respect the ramp.
Hydration
2.5–3 L/day. Reta suppresses thirst signaling — you won't feel thirsty even when dehydrated. Headaches, fatigue, poor training performance — usually water deficit, not the peptide. Add electrolytes once daily (LMNT, Liquid IV, similar).
Sleep
7–8 hrs target every night. Sleep depth is what locks in the gains from training + the metabolic work the stack is doing during the day — mitochondrial repair, lean tissue maintenance, hormone regulation all happen overnight.
Tip 04 — Missed Doses
What to Do If You Skip
Reta (once per week, Monday)
miss Monday, take it within 24–48 hours (Tuesday or Wednesday). More than 48 hours, just push the rest of the week's anchor day to whichever day you took it, and pick back up on that day the following week. Don't double up.
MOTS / 5-Amino / KLOW (M–F)
miss one day, skip it. Don't double up. Resume the next scheduled day. Missing 1–2 doses across the cycle doesn't move the needle.
Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause

Hold the next Reta dose if any of the following:

· Persistent nausea or vomiting that doesn't resolve within 24 hours
· Severe abdominal pain, especially radiating to the back — rule out pancreatitis
· Gallbladder-area pain or right-upper-quadrant pain after fatty meals
· Rapid weight loss above 3 lbs/week sustained — deficit is too aggressive, eat more
· Lightheadedness or dizziness on standing — could be blood sugar or dehydration; hydrate, eat, monitor
Hold MOTS / 5-Amino / KLOW if
significant injection-site reaction (redness, heat, swelling past 24 hrs), or any allergic-type symptoms (hives, throat tightness, breathing changes). All three are well-tolerated; individual reactions are rare but possible.
Any of these symptoms, text Micah first
Don't push through and don't restart on your own.
