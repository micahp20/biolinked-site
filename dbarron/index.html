David's Weight-Loss Protocol
Retatrutide — drop the weight, hold your energy, and keep it off.
The goal
lose a real amount of weight — and do it in a way that actually lasts. This protocol keeps it simple: one compound, two shots a week (Monday & Thursday, AM fasted). No daily needles, no complicated routine — an easy on-ramp since this is your first peptide protocol.
How Retatrutide works — the strongest single lever there is. Reta is a triple agonist (GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon). The GLP-1 and GIP arms crush appetite and "food noise" so the calorie deficit stops being a grind. The glucagon arm is the difference-maker — it preferentially burns visceral fat and raises how many calories you burn at rest, so you lose fat and keep your energy up instead of crash-dieting into fatigue. That combination is why Reta on its own drives some of the biggest fat-loss numbers of any compound available.
Why it keeps the weight off if you do it right. Three things make this durable: (1) we titrate up slowly — start low, step the dose up week by week — so your body adapts and you don't stall or get slammed with nausea; (2) staying at an effective dose long enough resets your appetite set-point, which is what makes the loss stick after the cycle instead of rebounding; (3) keep protein up and stay lightly active so what you lose is fat, not muscle. Run it until the bottles finish, and we'll reassess how you're feeling and adjust from there.

Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — crack the second Reta vial only as the first one empties.
Reconstitution amount
Retatrutide 3 mL per vial. Add the water slowly down the vial wall; swirl gently, don't shake.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.
Execution & Tips
Read Before Day 1
Tip 01 — The Routine
Two Shots, Twice a Week
The whole protocol is Monday & Thursday, AM fasted — one shot each day, that's it. Rotate abdomen quadrants, wipe the spot with alcohol, fresh syringe every time, wait 30–45 min before eating.
Draw marks climb as you titrate
Reta 10u → 15u → 20u. Small pulls on a 1 mL syringe — glance at the line each time so you don't overshoot. Since this is your first protocol: pinch a little skin, insert at ~45–90°, push slow, done.
Tip 02 — Titrate Up Slowly
Start Low, Step Up Weekly
Do not start at the top dose. The plan climbs on purpose: 1 mg (Wks 1–2) → 1.5 mg (Wk 3) → 2 mg (Wk 4+, 4 mg/week max). Ramping lets your gut adapt, which is what keeps nausea minimal and the fat loss steady instead of a stall-and-crash.
Only step up if the current dose felt fine
If a week is rough, hold that dose an extra week before climbing — there's no prize for rushing. Once you're at the top of the ladder, message Micah and we reassess how you're feeling.
Tip 03 — Why It Sticks
Lose It — and Keep It Off

Reta hits weight loss from two directions at once. The GLP-1 / GIP arms kill appetite and food noise; the glucagon arm raises how many calories you burn at rest. So you drop weight without your energy tanking — it's not a starvation cut, which is exactly why it's sustainable.

Keeping it off is the part you control
Two habits make the loss durable: protein at every meal (roughly 0.7–1 g per lb of goal weight) and light activity / walking most days — that keeps what you lose as fat, not muscle. Staying at an effective dose long enough also resets your appetite set-point, so the new normal holds after the cycle.
Tip 04 — Week-by-Week
What to Expect
Weeks 1–2 (starter dose)
appetite starts to drop and portions shrink on their own. Some mild nausea or early fullness is normal as your gut adapts — that's the Reta working. Don't chase big scale moves yet; hydrate hard.
Weeks 3–4 (stepping up)
as the dose climbs, appetite control gets noticeably stronger and the scale starts moving steadily. Energy holds because Reta's glucagon arm keeps your burn up — this isn't a starvation cut.
Weeks 5+ (the fat-loss window)
steady, consistent loss at the top of the ladder. Keep protein and steps up. The 2 vials run ~16 weeks — run to empty, then we reassess to hold, taper, or move to maintenance so it stays off.
Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
Hold the dose if
persistent nausea/vomiting past 24 hrs, or severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. Don't push through — we drop the dose or pause.
Losing too fast
more than ~2 lbs/week sustained is muscle-loss territory — message us and we ease a dose back.
Injection sites
a little redness at the pinhole is normal; spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain = possible infection — stop that site and message us.
Anything that feels off, message first
— don't push through or restart on your own.
