What you said
You want to be thinking more clearly. That's the target this whole protocol is pointed at — and your bloodwork backs it up, because the markers most tied to mental sharpness are the ones furthest out of range. Everything else here supports that same goal from a different direction.
How it's structured
This runs as two cycles back to back, not seven compounds at once. Cycle 1 is longevity and repair — SS-31, Epithalon, and Pinealon, all evening shots, about 30 days. Cycle 2 is the cognitive and metabolic block — NAD+ and MOTS-C, both mornings, about 8 weeks. Why cycles, not all at once
Seven compounds on day one would be seven variables and no way to know what worked. Cycling means one or two new things at a time — you learn what each one actually does for you, and the injection load stays manageable while you're still new to this.
You've never injected before
That's fine — and it's why Cycle 1 leads with small evening shots. These are insulin needles into the fat of your belly, not shots into muscle. Most people describe the first one as anticlimactic. Read the Notes & Tips tab before day 1; there's a walkthrough written for a first-timer.
How long it runs
Nothing stops on a date — each compound ends when its vials do. Cycle 1 is ~30 days, Cycle 2 is ~8 weeks, and the two Reta vials carry roughly 20–22 weeks once the dose climbs. Call the whole thing about five months.
Weight

Why This Is Built As Cycles, Not One Big Stack
Seven compounds at once would tell you nothing
If everything started on day one, you'd have seven variables moving together and no way to know which one helped. Running them in blocks means one or two new inputs at a time — when your sleep improves in week 2, you'll know it was the Epithalon and Pinealon, because that's all that changed.
The order is deliberate — sleep before everything else
Cycle 1 goes first because your sleep is a 2 out of 5, and sleep sits upstream of nearly everything else you asked for. Clear thinking, stress tolerance, testosterone, and appetite regulation all degrade without it. Fixing the foundation first makes Cycle 2 land on better ground.
The injection load starts small and gets smaller
You've never injected before. Cycle 1 is three small evening shots you do in the same two minutes each night — the biggest is 30 units. By Cycle 2 you're down to nine shots a week with weekends off, and by month four it's one shot a Monday. The demanding part is front-loaded, on purpose.
Two cycles, three shared mechanisms
The cycles aren't isolated — they hand off. SS-31 in Cycle 1 repairs mitochondrial structure; MOTS-C and NAD+ in Cycle 2 then run through that repaired machinery. SS-31 fixes the hardware, MOTS-C flips the switch, NAD+ supplies the currency. Running them in that order is why Cycle 2 tends to land harder than it would alone.
The architecture in one line: Cycle 1 rebuilds sleep and repairs cells over ~30 evenings, Cycle 2 restores daytime energy and metabolic signalling over ~8 mornings-only weeks, Retatrutide quietly handles the 30 lbs underneath both on a single Monday shot, and Selank sits in the drawer for the days that need it. Four goals, four blocks, never more than two new things at a time.

Cycle 1 is the demanding one
Three shots a night, seven nights a week, for a month. Epithalon and Pinealon are dosed in consecutive-day blocks — that's how they're meant to be run, and skipping days blunts them. Stack them into one routine right before bed and it's about three minutes total.
Cycle 2 is much lighter
Nine shots a week, all in the morning, weekends off. If Cycle 1 feels like a lot, know that it gets easier.
Reta is your Monday anchor
Same day every week, morning, regardless of which cycle you're in. Add 5 units each Monday until you reach 30u, then hold there. Set a recurring phone alarm titled "Monday shot — check the ramp."
Between the cycles
When Cycle 1 empties, you can roll straight into Cycle 2 or take a week off first — either is fine. Reta keeps going through the gap either way.
If you miss a day
Skip it and resume. Never double up to catch up. For Epithalon and Pinealon, one missed night in a 30-day block is not worth worrying about.

Tip 01 — Start Here
Your First Injection, Step by Step
You've never done this before, so here is the whole thing in order. It takes about 90 seconds once you've done it twice.
  • 1. Mix the vial (once per vial, not per shot). Wipe the rubber top with alcohol. Draw your bacteriostatic water into the syringe, push the needle through the stopper, and let the water run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — not blasted onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
  • 2. Wait for it to dissolve. Most of these clear in under a minute. Put it in the fridge and write the compound + date on the vial.
  • 3. Draw your dose. Wipe the stopper again. Pull the plunger to your unit mark first (this pushes air in and makes drawing easier), invert the vial, and draw to the line. Flick out any big air bubbles — small ones are harmless subcutaneously.
  • 4. Pick your spot. Belly fat, at least two inches away from your navel. Pinch a fold of skin between thumb and finger.
  • 5. Go in. Wipe the skin with alcohol, let it dry, then insert the needle at 45–90° into the pinched fold. It goes in easily — these needles are very fine. Push the plunger down at a steady, unhurried pace.
  • 6. Out and done. Withdraw, press with a clean finger or gauze for a few seconds. No massaging. Needle straight into the sharps container.
  • What's normal: a pinprick, sometimes nothing at all. A tiny bead of blood or a small bruise happens and means nothing. Rotate spots — think of your abdomen as four quadrants and move each time.
  • Do the first one on a night you're not rushed. Nearly everyone reports the anticipation was worse than the event.
Tip 02 — The Routine
What Each Cycle Actually Looks Like
Two cycles, two different rhythms. Anchor each one to something you already do every day.
  • Cycle 1 — evenings, every night, ~30 days. SS-31 10u, Epithalon 12u, Pinealon 30u. Three shots back to back, fasted, before bed. Do them in that order — smallest to largest — and rotate to a fresh spot for each.
  • Cycle 2 — mornings, weekdays only, ~8 weeks. MOTS-C 15u Monday through Friday, plus NAD+ 50u on M/W/F. Fasted, before food or coffee with cream. Wait 30–45 minutes before eating.
  • Reta — every Monday morning, both cycles. Start 10u, add 5u each Monday, stop climbing at 30u.
  • Selank — whenever you need it. 10u, any time, no schedule.
  • Draw marks to memorize: 10u SS-31, Reta start, and Selank · 12u Epithalon · 15u MOTS-C · 30u Pinealon and your Reta cap · 50u NAD+.

Tip 05 — Week by Week
What to Expect
  • Week 1 — Cycle 1 begins. Three evening shots plus your first Reta on Monday at 10u. Expect the injections to feel like a bigger deal on night one than night four. Appetite starts easing off within days of the Reta. Nothing dramatic yet from the longevity compounds.
  • Weeks 2–3. This is where Epithalon and Pinealon usually announce themselves — sleep depth first, then the mental clarity that follows from sleeping properly. Reta steps to 15u then 20u; some mild nausea or early fullness is normal and settles.
  • Weeks 4–5 — Cycle 1 winds down. Epithalon empties around day 25, Pinealon and SS-31 around day 30. Your evenings go quiet. Reta reaches 25–30u — this is where fat loss picks up noticeably. Take stock of the sleep and clarity change here; that's your Cycle 1 verdict.
  • Weeks 6–9 — Cycle 2 begins. Everything shifts to mornings. NAD+ is the one you'll feel — warmth and pressure for a minute or two, easing as you go up the weeks. Daytime energy and afternoon steadiness are the things to watch for.
  • Weeks 10–13. NAD+ empties around week 12–13, MOTS-C around week 13. Reta continues alone. By now the weight trend should be clearly established.
  • Weeks 14–22 — Reta alone. One shot a Monday, carrying the fat-loss phase to the 205 target. Re-test bloodwork around week 12 so we can compare against the 2024 panel and plan what comes next.
Tip 06 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
  • Severe abdominal pain radiating to your back — especially with nausea or vomiting. Stop the Reta and seek care. This is the pancreatitis flag, and it matters more for you than for most clients given your liver markers and alcohol intake.
  • Hold the Reta step-up if nausea or vomiting persists past 24 hours. Stay at your current units, don't climb. Never take a second dose to make up for a missed week.
  • NAD+ discomfort that doesn't settle within a few minutes, or worsens across doses rather than easing — drop back to 25u and tell us. Pushing slower fixes this the vast majority of the time.
  • Losing too fast. More than ~2 lbs/week sustained is muscle-loss territory — message us and we ease the Reta back.
  • Injection sites. A little redness at the pinhole is normal. Spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or worsening pain means stop using that site and message us.
  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin, dark urine, or right-upper-abdominal pain — stop everything and get seen. Your baseline liver numbers make this worth naming explicitly.
  • Anything that feels off, message first — don't push through it and don't restart on your own.
