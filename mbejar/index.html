Whole foods, high protein, a modest deficit. Retatrutide will make the deficit easy — the risk is under-eating protein and losing muscle instead of fat. Hit the protein number even on the days you are not hungry.

Notes & Tips
Running This Protocol
Note 01 — Your First Injections
Never Done This Before? Start Here.
  • These are subcutaneous shots — into the fat layer, not into muscle. The belly, an inch or two either side of the navel, is the easiest spot. Pinch a fold of skin, go in at 45–90°, push slowly, come out.
  • The needle is smaller than you are picturing. A 31G insulin pin is about the thickness of a hair. Most people describe the first one as anticlimactic.
  • Let the syringe sit out of the fridge for a minute. Cold liquid stings more going in. This is the single easiest thing to get right.
  • Let the alcohol dry completely before the needle goes in — injecting through wet alcohol is what actually stings.
  • Rotate. Move an inch or two each time and work around the abdomen. Where two shots share a morning, put them in different quadrants.
  • A small bead of blood or a pink dot afterward is normal. Press with a clean finger for a few seconds. If a bruise shows up, that site just caught a small vessel — move on and use somewhere else next time.
Note 02 — The On-Ramp
Nothing Starts On The Same Day
  • Wk 1 — three compounds. Retatrutide Monday morning at 10u, KLOW on weekday mornings, Tesamorelin at night.
  • Wk 2 — add 5-Amino-1MQ on the weekday mornings.
  • Wk 9 — Cycle 2. Cortagen in the morning, Epitalon and Pinealon at night, FOXO4 pulse that same week. The 20-night block has to run consecutively, so this cycle starts together — but all of it is gentle.
  • Wk 12 — Testosterone alone. Two weeks with just the oil shot, so you get the new technique down before anything else joins.
  • Wk 14 — add PT-141. It is the only compound in that cycle with an acute effect, so it goes in last.
  • Cycle 4 — Semax first, Selank a week later.
Note 03 — Testosterone
The Oil Shot Is A Different Skill
  • It is not a peptide and it does not use an insulin syringe. Oil at 250 mg/mL will not move through a 31G pin. Use a 3 mL Luer-lock syringe with a 22–25G needle.
  • Draw 0.36 mL — that is 36 on a 100-unit barrel, or just past the 1/3 mark on a 3 mL syringe. 90 mg per shot, Monday and Thursday, 180 mg for the week.
  • Push slowly. Oil goes in slower than water. Ten to fifteen seconds, not two.
  • Rotate every shot and alternate sides — glute, quad or delt, left Monday and right Thursday. Never the same spot twice in a row.
  • Mild soreness at the site for a day or two is normal, especially on the first few. It settles as the tissue gets used to the volume.
  • One vial is about fourteen weeks. Store it at room temperature in a dark cupboard — not the fridge.
Note 04 — Retatrutide
The Ladder, And Where To Get Off It
  • One shot a week, Monday morning. 10u, 20u, 30u, 40u, 50u, 60u across Weeks 1–6, then hold 60u.
  • The ceiling is a maximum, not a destination. If the weight is moving and appetite is controlled at 3 mg, stop climbing and hold there.
  • Mild nausea in the 24–48 hours after a dose is the known profile, loudest in the first two weeks and after each step up. Lighter, lower-fat meals Monday and Tuesday help — fat is the macro that makes it worse.
  • If a rung is not tolerable, drop back one and stay there. Eight weeks at 4 mg beats five weeks at 6 mg followed by three weeks of nothing.
  • Constipation is the other common one — that is a water and fiber problem. The 100 oz and 30 g targets on the Nutrition tab are the fix.
  • Never double a missed dose. If a Monday is missed, take it within 24 hours; past that, skip it and resume the next Monday at the same rung.
Note 05 — Tesamorelin
The Food Rule Is The Whole Compound
  • Two hours clear of food, 30–60 minutes before bed, five weeknights. Carbohydrate blunts the growth-hormone pulse this compound exists to amplify.
  • If you ate late, skip that night. A dose on top of food is mostly wasted, and skipping one of forty is a rounding error.
  • This is the belly-fat compound, not the scale compound. Take the waist measurement in Week 1 or you will have nothing to compare against.
  • Deeper sleep is usually the first thing people notice, around week two or three — which matters when you are running on six hours.
