Michael's 12-Month Protocol
52 weeks — a year-round foundation with six eight-week cycles rotating on top of it.
How the year runs
Three compounds — Testosterone, HGH and Retatrutide — run every week of the year and never pause. On top of them, six eight-week cycles rotate a pair of compounds each, then weeks 49–52 close on the foundation alone.

Why these counts

Every vial is counted until it is empty — doses in the vial divided by doses per week, nothing set aside part-used. HGH is the volume driver at 22, simply because a 24 IU vial is twelve 2 IU doses and it runs all 52 weeks. On a Mon / Wed / Fri cadence an eight-week cycle is 24 doses, and the per-shot doses are sized so the vials empty against exactly that. SS-31, MOTS-C and 5-Amino-1MQ each hold 24 doses — one vial, one cycle, nothing left. Glutathione holds twelve, so that one takes two per cycle. Only NAD+ carries a surplus, since its 20-dose vial needs a second to reach 24. A 30 mg Retatrutide vial on its own is fifteen weeks of supply.

Reconstitution & Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ The Basics
Mixing
Add the BAC water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — never squirt it directly onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Recon volumes
HGH 3 mL · Retatrutide 3 mL · SS-31 3 mL · MOTS-C 3 mL · KLOW 3 mL · NAD+ 5 mL · 5-Amino-1MQ 3 mL · Glutathione 5 mL. Testosterone is pre-mixed.
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Mixed vials
Refrigerate immediately after adding water and run the vial until it is empty. Mix one vial of each compound at a time — don't open a second until the first is done.
Syringes
Fresh U-100 insulin syringe every single injection. Never reuse.
Site rotation
SubQ shots rotate across the four abdominal quadrants. Never use the same 1″ spot two days running — over 52 weeks this matters more than it does over eight.
