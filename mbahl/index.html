Why these counts

Every vial is counted until it is empty — doses in the vial divided by doses per week, nothing set aside part-used. HGH is the volume driver at 22, simply because a 24 IU vial is twelve 2 IU doses and it runs all 52 weeks. Everything else lands between two and six — at these doses one vial covers a month or more, and a 30 mg Retatrutide vial on its own is fifteen weeks of supply. The two single-cycle compounds are the smallest lines: 5-Amino-1MQ yields thirty 10u doses from a 50 mg vial and AOD-9604 twenty from a 5 mg vial, so each needs only two.

Reconstitution & Storage
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ The Basics
Mixing
Add the BAC water slowly down the inside wall of the vial — never squirt it directly onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
Recon volumes
HGH 3 mL · Retatrutide 3 mL · KLOW 3 mL · NAD+ 5 mL · SS-31 3 mL · MOTS-C 3 mL · 5-Amino-1MQ 3 mL · AOD-9604 2 mL. Testosterone is pre-mixed.
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F before reconstitution. Protect from light. Never freeze.
Mixed vials
Refrigerate immediately after adding water and run the vial until it is empty. Mix one vial of each compound at a time — don't open a second until the first is done.
Syringes
Fresh U-100 insulin syringe every single injection. Never reuse.
Site rotation
SubQ shots rotate across the four abdominal quadrants. Never use the same 1″ spot two days running — over 52 weeks this matters more than it does over eight.

Add A1c, fasting glucose, and a lipid panel to see the metabolic layer working.

If hematocrit climbs toward 50%: we lower the testosterone dose slightly and/or you donate blood — a simple, routine adjustment, not a problem.

Ongoing: repeat the full Ways2Well/Optimal DX panel at ~6 months to see the whole picture move — free T optimal, A1c back toward 5.3, lipids continuing down, DHEA-S recovering. Same lab, same panel, so the comparison is clean.
