Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, One Vial at a Time
Dry vials
refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze.
After mixing
refrigerate and use within ~30 days. Reconstitute one vial at a time — open the next only when the current one runs dry. KLOW turns clear blue once mixed (that's the copper in GHK-Cu) — normal.
Reconstitution amounts
Reta 3 mL, KPV 1 mL, KLOW 3 mL, CJC 2 mL, MOTS 3 mL, Pinealon 1 mL, Epithalon 3 mL, SS-31 2 mL, DSIP 1 mL, Selank 1 mL, Kisspeptin 1 mL, Oxytocin 10 mL, PT-141 1 mL. Add the water slowly down the vial wall; swirl, don't shake.
Every shot
fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites.

You had very pervasive vomiting on Semaglutide / Tirzepatide. Tip 04 — Sleep + Stress

Sleep is where the muscle, skin, and fat-loss work actually happens — so fixing the 1/5 is a real lever, not a side quest. CJC/Ipamorelin deepens sleep nightly; DSIP is your as-needed heavy hitter for rough nights; Epithalon (during the 20-day course) helps reset the melatonin rhythm. Tip 05 — Red Flags
When to Pause & Message Us
Hold Reta if
vomiting returns, persistent nausea >24 hrs, or severe abdominal pain radiating to the back. This is the one to watch given your history.
Injection sites

PT-141 first use
half dose (5u), and expect possible mild nausea/flushing — that's normal and passes.
Anything that feels off, message first
— don't push through, and don't restart on your own. We adjust based on what's actually happening.
