Personalized Protocol
Zachery's Cognitive Build
Sharpen the brain first. Then longevity, muscle, libido and fat loss — five cycles, run one at a time.
You listed six goals, so this is built as five separate cycles rather than one giant stack. Run them in order, finish the bottles, then move to the next. That way you always know what is doing what — if something works, you know which compound earned it, and if something does not agree with you, there is only one variable to pull. Cycle 1 is cognitive because that is what you put first, and it is the shortest, lowest-commitment way to feel whether peptides do anything for you at all.
The five cycles
1 — Cognitive (Semax, Cortagen, Cerebrolysin): ~20 days, the focus and clarity block. 2 — Longevity (Epithalon, Pinealon, FOXO4-DRI): ~20 days, the senescence + sleep-architecture block. 3 — Muscle & Recovery (BPC/TB-500, KPV, IGF-1 LR3, HGH): the long one, six months on HGH, and the only cycle meant to run continuously. 4 — Libido (PT-141, Oxytocin): as needed, not scheduled. 5 — Fat Loss (Retatrutide, 5-Amino-1MQ): as needed, when you want the last 10 lbs to move.
What your bloodwork changes
Your 5/7 panel is clean where it counts — hs-CRP under 0.5 is excellent, liver and kidney markers are unremarkable, ferritin and hemoglobin are solid. Two things stand out. Total testosterone 408 ng/dL with free testosterone 82 pg/mL sits in the bottom third of range for 38, and estradiol at 14.7 pg/mL is below the reference floor — that combination is worth a real conversation with a provider before you spend six months chasing muscle with HGH. Triglycerides 153 and remnant cholesterol 30.6 are the other flag, and they track with the 3–7 drinks a week. See the Bloodwork tab.

Your Stack, Explained
Tip 01 — The Rule That Matters Most
One Cycle at a Time
Fourteen compounds arrived together, but they are not meant to be taken together. The whole design of this protocol is sequencing, and it only works if you hold the line on it.
  • Finish the bottles in front of you before opening the next cycle. Cycle 1 is 20 days. That is the commitment.
  • One new variable at a time. If you feel great in week 2, you know exactly what did it. If something disagrees with you, there is one thing to stop.
  • Cycle 3 is the exception — HGH and BPC/TB-500 run continuously for months. Everything else has a defined end.
  • Do not front-load. The temptation with a stack this size is to start five things on Monday. That is how people end up unable to tell what worked.
Why it is built this way
You listed six goals and have no prior peptide experience. Sequencing turns that from a gamble into five clean experiments — and it spreads $1,660 across months instead of one order.
Tip 02 — The Big One
HGH — Start at 1 IU and Climb Slowly
HGH is $660 of the $1,660 and the longest commitment in the stack at roughly six months. It is also the compound most likely to give you side effects if you rush it.
  • Week 1–2: 1 IU (4 units). That is a deliberately small dose. You are checking tolerance, not chasing an effect.
  • Step up only after an uneventful week. 2 IU (8u), then hold. Most people find 2–3 IU is the sweet spot.
  • 4 IU (17 units) is the ceiling. Past that the side-effect curve gets steep and the returns do not.
  • Water retention, tingling hands and morning puffiness are dose-related and reversible. If they show up, drop back a step rather than pushing through.
  • Pick AM fasted or pre-bed and stay there. Do not split the dose or alternate.
Re-test, do not guess
GH nudges fasting glucose up. Your fructosamine is already 253 and triglycerides 153 — both upper-normal. Get a repeat panel at the 8–12 week mark rather than running six months blind.
Tip 03 — From Your Bloodwork
The Testosterone Conversation
Your 5/7 panel showed total testosterone 408 ng/dL and free testosterone 82 pg/mL — both inside the lab reference range but in its bottom third, and well under the performance range the lab itself lists (700–1100 total, 120–180 free).   • Low estradiol alongside low-normal testosterone is its own issue — E2 matters for libido, mood, joints and bone, and it is downstream of testosterone. Treating the T often fixes the E2.
  • Ask for a repeat morning draw first. Testosterone is highest early and varies day to day; one 4:27 am collection is a data point, not a diagnosis. Ask for LH, FSH and prolactin alongside it so the cause is clear.

To be clear
This is a flag, not a prescription — I am not your doctor and cannot diagnose hypogonadism from one panel. What the numbers justify is asking a qualified provider to evaluate it properly, which at 38 with these values is a reasonable request.

  • Wipe the vial top and your skin with a fresh alcohol pad, every draw.
  • Pinch a small fold of abdominal fat two inches away from your navel, insert at 45–90°, push slowly, withdraw.
  • Rotate quadrants — upper-right, upper-left, lower-right, lower-left. Never the same 1″ spot two days running.
  • On a three-shot morning use three different quadrants.
  • Push the big volumes slowly. FOXO4 at 60–80 units and Cerebrolysin at 30 both go in over 20–30 seconds, not in one press.
  • Fresh syringe every single time. Reusing a needle dulls it and hurts.
If a shot stings
Cerebrolysin and FOXO4-DRI are the two most likely to. Slower push, and let the vial come up from fridge temperature for a minute or two first.
