Why the microdose is better — you askedFair question, and the answer is about steadiness. One large weekly shot creates a spike and then a long decay: the first two days you feel over-suppressed — nausea, no appetite, food aversion — and by day six the effect has faded and hunger comes back hard. 1 mg three times a week gives you the same weekly exposure without the peaks and troughs. The Goal
Measuring 43″ → 41″ Honestly
  • Measure the waist weekly, at the belly button, first thing in the morning. Relaxed — not sucked in, not pushed out, tape level all the way around. Same day, same conditions, every week. This is your primary metric, not the scale.
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week and use the weekly average. Never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone, and on this stack the first two weeks can move in either direction for reasons that have nothing to do with fat.
  • Expect the tape and the scale to disagree at times. That is normal and it is often good news: visceral fat can come off while the scale sits still, particularly with your TRT defending lean mass. If the waist is shrinking and the weight is flat, the protocol is working.
  • Two inches is roughly 15–20 lb for most men at your size — so 41″ and 180 lb are broadly the same milestone, not two separate ones. Reaching 180 will very likely land you at or under 41″.
  • Take a photo the morning you start. Same light, same spot, same time of day. A year is long enough that you will not trust your memory of where you began, and the photo is the only thing that will settle it.
Retatrutide
Running The Microdose — And When To Move It
  • 1 mg on Mon / Wed / Fri, mornings, fasted. The three-times-weekly cadence is the entire point — it holds a steady level instead of a weekly spike-and-crash, which is what makes it both tolerable and predictable.
  • Give it 2–3 weeks before judging it. Appetite effects settle in the first week or two; the fat-loss effect is a multi-week read, not a daily one.
  • Tier up if the weight stalls and the appetite effect has clearly faded: 1.5 mg (15u), hold two weeks, then 2 mg (20u) if you still want more. One step at a time — never jump.
  • Tier down if it is costing you food: 0.75 mg (7.5u) or 0.5 mg (5u). Nausea, reflux or genuine food aversion means the dose is too high for you, not that the drug is wrong. A dose you take every week beats a dose you skip.
  • Protect the protein. You eat like a bodybuilder and that is exactly what should carry you through a 29 lb cut with your muscle intact. If Reta ever starts eating into your protein intake, that is the signal to come down — the appetite suppression is a side effect here, not the goal.
  • Hydrate and keep fiber up. Slowed gastric emptying plus a deficit is the standard recipe for constipation on this class of compound. Water and fiber solve most of it before it starts.
Food, Alcohol & Recovery
The Half This Page Can't Do For You
  • Keep the protein high — this is your biggest asset. You already eat this way. Aim for roughly 0.8–1 g per pound of goal weight (about 145–180 g) and hold it there the whole way down. On a 29 lb cut, protein is what decides whether the weight you lose is fat or muscle.
  • A moderate deficit beats an aggressive one here. With Retatrutide suppressing appetite, under-eating is easier than you would expect. 1–1.5 lb a week is the pace that keeps the loss lean; faster than 2 lb a week and you are giving back muscle you spent decades building.
  • Alcohol is the piece worth being straight about. You reported drinking daily, and it works against several things on this page at once: it blunts fat oxidation for hours after, it adds calories that do not register as food, it works directly against the appetite control Retatrutide is giving you, and it puts load on the liver. This is not a lecture and nobody is asking for zero — but every drink you take out is progress you get back, and the highest-value ones to cut are the ones on your dosing days. If you want a single concrete change: make Sunday through Thursday dry and keep the drinking to the weekend, when you are off protocol anyway. Worth raising with your TRT provider too, since alcohol and hormone therapy share liver pathways.
  • Sleep at 3/5 is the other lever. Recovery and appetite regulation both degrade on short sleep, and alcohol in the evening is one of the most reliable ways to wreck sleep architecture even when it makes you fall asleep faster.
  • Stress at 2/5 is genuinely a good starting point. Nothing to fix here — just worth naming that it is one fewer thing working against you.
Timeline
What To Expect, Roughly
  • Weeks 1–2 — settling in. Appetite suppression shows up first, usually within days. Mild GI adjustment is normal and generally fades. Expect a couple of pounds of quick change that is mostly water. Take your baseline waist measurement and photo before the first shot.
  • Weeks 3–4 — the deficit gets real. Appetite control should be steady and the scale moving at 1–1.5 lb a week. If it is flat by the end of week 3 and the appetite effect has clearly faded, this is the point to consider stepping to 1.5 mg (15u).
  • Weeks 5–12 — the long stretch. This is where most of the 29 lb comes off, at a steady rather than dramatic pace. The waist usually starts answering a few weeks behind the scale. Keep measuring weekly.
  • Around week 10–12 — the second vial. Your first mixed vial will be past its shelf life well before it is empty, so you will have opened the second by now. Worth a check-in on dose and progress at that point.
  • Running solo changes the pace, not the direction. Retatrutide alone will still drive the fat loss — what you gave up by declining the rest is the muscle-sparing and visceral-fat-specific support, so protein and training matter more here, not less.
  • Anything that doesn't settle — GI symptoms that worsen rather than ease, or anything that simply feels wrong — stop and check in before your next dose.
