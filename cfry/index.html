Courtney's Fat Loss Cycle
Two compounds, one shot day a week. Retatrutide does the heavy lifting; Cagrilintide comes at hunger from a second direction.
Why this is a step up, not a sideways move

You ran Tirzepatide at 12.5 mg and were still hungry. That is useful information, not a failure — it says the two-receptor approach was not enough on its own. Retatrutide adds a third receptor (glucagon) on top of the two Tirzepatide already covered, and Cagrilintide works through amylin, which is a different pathway again. Between them you are hitting appetite from three angles instead of two.

Because you are coming off 12.5 mg of Tirzepatide, you are not starting from zero — the ladder below opens at 2 mg rather than the 0.5 mg a first-timer would use. Take your first Retatrutide shot on the day your next Tirzepatide would have been due. No washout, no gap.

The ladder — two weeks on every rung

Weeks 1–2 — 10u (2 mg). Weeks 3–4 — 15u (3 mg). Weeks 5–6 — 20u (4 mg). From Week 7 you have a choice: hold at 20u if appetite is handled, or keep climbing — 30u (6 mg) and then 40u (8 mg) — two weeks per rung. Two weeks rather than one gives side effects time to settle before you ask for more.

Climb only when you need to

Tier up if hunger is clearly back and the scale has been flat for a full week at the current rung. Tier down one rung if nausea lasts past day two, food goes off entirely, or you cannot get protein in. 8 mg is the top of the ladder, not the goal — if 4 mg is working, 4 mg is the right dose for the rest of the cycle. The difference between this and the Tirzepatide run is that you now have room to climb; it does not mean you have to use all of it.

Why three vials for twelve weeks

One vial holds far more than a month of doses, but a reconstituted vial is good for about 30 days in the fridge — so the shelf clock is what sets the count, not the milligrams. One vial per month, three vials for the twelve weeks. Mix each one on the day you start it, not in advance.

Cagrilintide · 10 mg ?A long-acting amylin analog. Amylin is released alongside insulin and tells the brain the meal is over — a separate satiety signal from the GLP-1 one, through a separate receptor. Its own small ladder

Weeks 1–2 — 5u (0.5 mg). Weeks 3–4 — 10u (1 mg). Weeks 5–8 — 15u (1.5 mg) if the first two rungs sat well. It climbs alongside the Retatrutide but on its own schedule, and if a week feels like too much, hold both where they are rather than moving one and not the other.

Same day, same sitting, two syringes

Both go in on your chosen shot day. Draw and inject them separately — do not mix them in one barrel — but there is no reason to space them out. Two small SubQ shots, a couple of inches apart on the abdomen, sixty seconds all in.

Two vials covers the first eight weeks

Same 30-day rule: one vial per month, two vials for eight weeks. That is deliberate — the hardest stretch for appetite is the front half, and by Week 9 the Retatrutide is at a rung that carries the rest on its own. If you want to keep the Cagrilintide going past Week 8, that is a separate conversation, not something already built in.

Tips For Your Cycle
Tip 01 — Before You Start
Worth Getting A Baseline Panel
You mentioned some numbness in your hands. That is not something this page can speak to, and nothing here is a diagnosis — but it is the kind of thing worth checking with a provider rather than sitting on.
  • The usual baseline four to ask for: B12, A1C, fasting glucose and a thyroid panel.
  • Any primary care provider can order them. Urgent care and most walk-in labs can too.
  • Before the cycle is ideal; early in it is fine. The point is having a number to compare against later.
Useful either way

A baseline is worth having on its own once you are twelve weeks into a fat-loss cycle — it gives you something to measure against at the end. Send the results over and the Bloodwork tab fills in.

Tip 02 — Coming Off Tirzepatide
No Washout, No Gap
Take your first Retatrutide shot on the day your next Tirzepatide would have been due. There is no need to leave a gap, and leaving one usually just means a rough week of appetite coming back.
  • You are starting at 2 mg, not 0.5 mg — the ladder accounts for where you already are.
  • Expect the first two weeks to feel similar to what you are used to, not dramatically different. The step change tends to land around the third or fourth rung.
  • If week one feels like a lot more than the Tirzepatide did, hold at 10u for an extra week before climbing.
Tip 03 — The Injection Itself
Two Small Shots, Sixty Seconds
Both compounds are SubQ — short, thin insulin needles into the fat layer, not the muscle. It is the easiest kind of injection there is.
  • Wash hands. Fresh syringe and alcohol pad for each shot.
  • Wipe the vial top, let it dry five seconds.
  • Draw to the unit mark for that week. Flick out any big bubbles.
  • Pinch a fold of abdomen about two inches from the navel, go in at 45–90°, push slowly.
  • Two compounds means two syringes — a couple of inches apart, never mixed in one barrel.
  • Straight into the sharps container. Rotate sides week to week.
Tip 04 — Side Effects
What Is Normal And What Is Not
The common ones are digestive and they cluster in the 24 to 48 hours after the shot, especially in the week after a step up.
  • Mild nausea, early fullness, some reflux, constipation. These usually settle within a few days on a rung.
  • Eat smaller and slower. Most of the discomfort on these compounds comes from eating a normal-sized plate on a stomach that is emptying at half speed.
  • Water and fibre handle most of the constipation.
  • If nausea is still there past day two, or food goes off entirely, drop back a rung rather than pushing through.
  • Anything severe or that does not fit the list above — stop and talk to a provider, not to a protocol page.
Tip 05 — Protein Is The Thing To Protect
Eat Less, But Not Less Protein
Appetite suppression this strong makes it easy to under-eat protein without noticing, and that is how a fat-loss cycle turns into a muscle-loss cycle.
  • At around 220 lb, aim for roughly 120–150 g of protein a day even on days you are not hungry.
  • Protein first on every plate. If you only get through half the meal, make the half that lands the protein half.
  • A shake is a legitimate way to hit the number on a bad appetite day.
  • Some resistance training, even twice a week, does more to keep the weight you lose from being muscle than anything in a vial.
Tip 06 — Storage And Mixing
Mix On The Day, Keep It Cold
Dry vials are stable at room temperature. Once mixed, everything lives in the fridge and is good for about 30 days.
  • Add the water slowly down the side of the vial, not blasted onto the powder.
  • Swirl, never shake. Give it a few minutes to go clear.
  • Mix one vial at a time, on the day you start it — not all of them up front. That is what the 30-day clock is about.
  • Label each vial with the date you mixed it.
  • Keep them in the body of the fridge, not the door, and not the freezer.
