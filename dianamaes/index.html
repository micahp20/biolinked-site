How to mix it — step by stepOxytocin is reconstituted into a full 10 mL bottle of BAC water, not into its own powder vial — so you do not need a separate empty 10 mL vial for this. The BAC bottle itself becomes the finished dosing vial. 1. Start with a full, sealed 10 mL BAC water vial, and alcohol-swab the top of it and of the Oxytocin powder vial. 2. Draw 1 mL of BAC water out of the 10 mL vial. 3. Inject that 1 mL into the Oxytocin powder vial, down the side wall, and swirl gently until the powder is completely dissolved. 4. Draw that same ~1 mL back out — it now holds all 10 mg of dissolved Oxytocin. 5. Inject it back into the 10 mL BAC water vial. 6. The Oxytocin is now spread evenly across the full 10 mL — that bottle is your dosing vial, at 1 mg/mL, and every 10u draw is 100 mcg. Label and date it; the empty powder vial gets thrown away, and the same transfer gets done again for each new vial you open.

Sugar cravings

Protein at breakfast is the single highest-value change — 30–40 g within an hour of waking flattens the afternoon craving more reliably than anything you do later in the day. When a craving lands, eat protein first and reassess in ten minutes.

On metformin

Metformin and Retatrutide both work on glucose. Track fasting glucose daily for the first three weeks and take any downward trend or hypoglycemic symptoms to your prescriber — dose changes are their call, not something to adjust on your own.
