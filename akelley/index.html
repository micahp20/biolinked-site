Client Protocol
Ashley's Clarity, Sleep & Balance Protocol
Two sequential cycles across 16 weeks — cognition and sleep first, then fat loss and hormonal balance layered on top of a settled baseline.
In your words
You travel constantly through the school year, you're exhausted, and you want enhanced cognitive clarity and maximized focus plus a sleep routine that survives being on the road. That's the brief, so the two cognitive peptides lead this protocol and everything else is sequenced behind them.
Why two cycles
You listed six goals. Running eight compounds at once would be unreadable for a first-time peptide user and would make it impossible to tell what did what. So this splits into Cycle 1 (Weeks 1–8) — cognition and sleep, and Cycle 2 (Weeks 9–16) — fat loss and hormonal balance. Fix the foundation first; layer the rest onto a body that is already sleeping properly.
Cycle 1 · the foundation
Semax is the focus and clarity layer. Selank is the calm underneath it. CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin runs at night and covers deep sleep, recovery, anti-aging and lean recomposition at once. Epitalon + Pinealon run as a paired 20-night pineal pulse — Epitalon on the gland side, Pinealon on the CNS side — which is the single most useful thing here for someone crossing time zones.
Cycle 2 · the additions
Retatrutide takes the fat-loss goal, one shot a week, started at the very bottom of the ladder because 7 lb on a lean frame is a small ask. Kisspeptin-10 + PT-141 are the hormonal-balance pair — the standard BioLinked pairing for this goal, at the standard doses. Kisspeptin works upstream on the HPG axis; PT-141 works downstream in the brain. Both are backed by female-specific research and neither replaces a hormone.
First-timer dosing
This is your first time with peptides, and you're already lean at 150 with a 7 lb goal. Every dose below sits at the low end of its range, and the two cycles never overlap — you are never learning more than one new thing at a time.
Citalopram overlap
You're on Citalopram 40 mg. Both Semax and Selank touch serotonergic signalling — Semax modulates dopamine and serotonin turnover, Selank works largely through GABA/tuftsin pathways with a secondary serotonergic effect. Neither is an SSRI, an MAOI or a direct serotonin agonist, so this is an overlap to be aware of, not a block. It is the reason both start at 500 mcg rather than the more common 1 mg. How To Run It
Three Things That Decide The Outcome
The Real Lever
Eating This On The Road
  • Protein is the only thing you have to defend. If a travel day destroys the plan, hit the protein number and let the rest go. That single habit protects the muscle you are trying to keep.
  • Pack the snack, always. A protein bar and a bag of nuts in the carry-on is the difference between a 1,750-calorie day and a 2,400-calorie airport day. This is where most of the drift happens.
  • Order the protein and the vegetable, add the carb second. Almost every restaurant menu works if you build the plate in that order. You do not need to find a special restaurant.
  • Hydrate harder on flying days. An extra 20–30 oz on travel days. Cabin dehydration reads as exactly the brain fog you are trying to get rid of.
  • Move dinner, not the shot. If a work dinner runs late, eat it — and skip the CJC/Ipa that night rather than injecting on a full stomach.
Sleep & Food
What You Eat Decides How You Sleep
  • Caffeine cutoff at 12pm. Caffeine has a 5–6 hour half-life, so a 3pm coffee still has a quarter of its dose working at bedtime. On a protocol built around deep sleep this is nearly free to fix.
  • Alcohol is the biggest single variable. It puts you to sleep faster and then wrecks the second half of the night — exactly the window CJC/Ipa and Epitalon are working in. Three drinks a week, none on Epitalon nights.
  • A light, early dinner sleeps better than a big late one — and it happens to be the same rule the evening shot needs. Those two requirements point the same direction, which makes this easier than it sounds.
  • Do not go to bed genuinely hungry either. If dinner at 6:30 leaves you hollow at 9, the fix is more protein and volume at dinner, not a late snack that breaks the fasting window.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Weekly Average
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? Drop about 150 calories — smaller carb portion at dinner covers it. Do not cut protein and do not cut fat to get there.
  • Losing faster than 1 lb a week? Add 150 back. At your body weight, faster than that starts costing lean tissue, which is the opposite of what the GH side of this stack is for.
  • Expect the first week to look odd. A change in carb intake moves water weight by several pounds in either direction before any fat has changed. The trend from Week 2 onward is the real signal.
  • Most of the 7 lb comes in Cycle 2. Cycle 1 has no appetite compound in it, so the loss over Weeks 1–8 is whatever the deficit and the overnight GH pulse produce — often 2–4 lb. That is expected, not a stall.

Reconstitution
Mixing, Step By Step
💧 First Time Mixing — Do It Slowly
Cycle 1 amounts
Semax 2 mL · Selank 2 mL · CJC/Ipa 2 mL · Epitalon 3 mL · Pinealon 1 mL per vial. Those give 5 mg/mL on the first three, 16.7 mg/mL on the Epitalon and 10 mg/mL on the Pinealon — which is what makes every draw 10u except Epitalon at 12u and Pinealon at 20u.
Cycle 2 amounts
Retatrutide 3 mL · Kisspeptin-10 2 mL per vial · PT-141 2 mL. Reta lands at 10 mg/mL (so 10u = 1 mg and each extra 5 units is another 0.5 mg), Kisspeptin and PT-141 both at 5 mg/mL for a flat 10u.
How
Wipe both stoppers with alcohol. Draw the water into a syringe, then let it run slowly down the inside wall of the vial — do not squirt it straight onto the powder. Swirl gently until clear. Never shake.
It looks like nothing
The powder is often just a faint film at the bottom of the vial — that is normal, not a short vial, and it is most obvious on the little 10 mg Pinealon vials. Once mixed, everything on this page goes completely clear.
Order
Day 1: mix Semax #1, Selank #1, CJC/Ipa #1, the Epitalon and Pinealon #1. Leave the three #2 vials dry until roughly Week 5, and the other three Pinealon vials dry until each is needed — roughly every five nights. Cycle 2 starts fresh at Week 9: mix Reta, Kisspeptin #1 and PT-141 on that first Monday.
Storage & Handling
Keep It Cold · Keep It Clean
❄ Refrigerated, Protected, Labeled
Dry vials
Refrigerate at 36–46°F, protected from light. Never freeze. Unmixed powder keeps for a long time, which is why the three spare vials just wait.
After mixing
Refrigerate and use within about 30 days. Each 20-dose vial is exactly four weeks at five a week, so it empties right as the window closes — the timing is built that way on purpose. The Retatrutide is the exception: you use 13 mg of a 30 mg vial across Cycle 2, so it is bounded by the 30-day window, not the powder. Keep it cold and discard the remainder rather than stretching it.
Label them
Write the compound and its unit mark on every vial you mix. Cycle 1: Semax 10u, Selank 10u, CJC/Ipa 10u, Epitalon 12u, Pinealon 20u. Cycle 2: Reta 10→20u, Kisspeptin 10u, PT-141 10u. Semax and Selank look identical in a fridge door, and so do Kisspeptin and PT-141.
Travelling
Carry-on only, in the insulated pouch. Keep them in the original vials. A few hours at room temperature will not ruin a mixed vial — a checked bag at altitude might.
Every shot
Fresh single-use syringe, wipe the stopper and your skin with alcohol, rotate injection sites. Three PM shots in Weeks 1–3 means three different spots — that is the busiest the protocol ever gets.
Notes & Tips
Running Your First Protocol
Note 01 — Technique
Subcutaneous, Not Intramuscular
  • Every shot on this page is subcutaneous — into the fat layer just under the skin, not into muscle. You have injected before, so the mechanics are familiar; the difference is that these needles are much smaller and go in much shallower.
  • Pinch, insert at 45–90°, push slowly, withdraw. Abdomen is the easiest site — two inches out from the navel, anywhere around the ring. Outer thigh and the back of the upper arm also work.
  • Rotate. Move an inch or two from the last spot each time and work your way around the ring. Repeating the same square inch is what causes lumps and tenderness.
  • A small bruise or a red dot is normal and fades in a day or two. Injecting cold liquid straight from the fridge stings more — let the syringe sit out for a minute first.
  • Air bubbles are not a danger subcutaneously at these volumes, but tap them up and push them out anyway — a bubble displaces liquid, so it costs you dose accuracy.
Note 02 — Citalopram
The Serotonergic Overlap, Explained
  • Citalopram is an SSRI — it keeps serotonin in the synapse longer by blocking its reuptake. That is a direct, sustained effect on one neurotransmitter system.
  • Semax is not an SSRI. It is an ACTH(4–10) fragment whose primary action is raising BDNF and NGF; its effect on dopamine and serotonin turnover is modulatory and indirect, not reuptake inhibition and not agonism at serotonin receptors.
  • Selank works mainly through GABA and tuftsin pathways, with a secondary influence on serotonin metabolism. Again, no reuptake inhibition, no direct receptor agonism.
  • What that means practically: these are different mechanisms touching an overlapping system. That is a reason to start low and add one variable at a time — not a reason to avoid them. Both are at 500 mcg, half the dose commonly used.
  • Add them one at a time. Start Semax on Day 1 and hold Selank until Day 4 or 5. If something feels different, you will know which compound it came from. Hold both at 500 mcg for at least two weeks before considering 20u.
Note 03 — Travel
Running This From A Hotel Room
  • The AM shots are the flexible ones. Semax and Selank do not need to be fasted, so they fit around any breakfast, any time zone, any schedule. Take them when you wake up wherever you are.
  • The PM shot is the one to protect. CJC/Ipa needs 2+ hours clear of food. On a night with a late work dinner, skip it — do not inject on a full stomach and do not push it to 1am.
  • Anchor Epitalon to local bedtime, not to the clock you left behind. During the 20-night pulse take it right before sleep in whatever time zone you are actually in. That is precisely the signal you want it giving the pineal gland.
  • Pack for the trip, not the week. Three mixed vials, the syringes you need plus two spares, a strip of alcohol pads, and the insulated pouch. Carry-on. Hotel mini-fridges are usually fine — set them to the coldest setting that is not freezing.
  • If a hotel has no fridge, the insulated pouch with a refreshed gel pack holds overnight without trouble. A single warm night will not ruin a vial.
Note 04 — Missed Doses
Skip It, Do Not Stack It
  • Missed an AM shot? Take it whenever you remember, as long as it is before mid-afternoon. Later than that, skip it — Semax late in the day can push bedtime back.
  • Missed a PM shot? Skip it entirely. Never double up the next night. One missed CJC/Ipa dose out of forty changes nothing.
  • Missed an Epitalon night? Just continue the count the next night — you will finish night 20 one day later. Do not take two in a day to catch up.
  • Missed the Monday Retatrutide? Take it within 48 hours — Tuesday or Wednesday morning is fine, then shift that week's Monday back to normal the following week. Past Wednesday, skip it and resume the next Monday at the same dose. Never double a Reta dose.
  • Missed a Kisspeptin or PT-141? Skip it. Kisspeptin is a weekday habit and one gap changes nothing; PT-141 is twice a week and the next one is never more than three days out.
  • Missed a whole travel week? Pick the schedule back up where you left it. The vials last as long as they last; the cycle simply runs a week longer.
Note 05 — Timeline
What Shows Up, And When
  • Week 1–2 — sleep and focus. These are the fastest signals in the stack. Deeper sleep from the CJC/Ipa and Epitalon combination, and the Semax effect on mental clarity, both typically show up here.
  • Week 2–4 — the travel test. The real read on Epitalon is how you feel after the first trip that crosses time zones during the pulse. That is the thing it is actually for.
  • Week 4–6 — recovery and body composition. Slower. Usually noticed in how clothes fit and in energy through the afternoon before it appears on the scale.
  • Week 6–8 — the scale. Cycle 1 has no appetite compound in it, so expect roughly 2–4 lb from the deficit and the GH pulse alone. That is the correct pace for someone already lean, and it is not a stall.
  • Week 9–12 — Cycle 2 opens. Reta's appetite effect is the fastest signal in the second cycle and usually shows up on the first or second shot. PT-141's acute effects — flushing, mild nausea in the first hour — are most noticeable on the earliest doses and fade. Kisspeptin is the slow one: mood and drive accumulate over weeks.
  • Week 13–16 — the finish. Reta at its 2 mg ceiling for the last four weeks is where most of the remaining fat loss lands. Across both cycles the 7 lb should be done or close to it.
  • Semax and Selank are subtle by design. Neither is a stimulant. The effect people describe is a higher floor rather than a peak — less fade, less edge, easier to hold attention. If you are waiting for something dramatic you will miss it.
Note 06 — Cycle 2
Three New Compounds, One At A Time
  • Stagger the introductions, same as Cycle 1. Start Kisspeptin on the Monday of Week 9 on its own. Add Retatrutide the following Monday. Add PT-141 in Week 11. Three new compounds in one week means you learn nothing about any of them.
  • Retatrutide is the one with a real side-effect curve. Nausea, early fullness and slower digestion in the first 24–48 hours after the shot are the common ones, worst on the first dose of each new step. Lighter, lower-fat Monday and Tuesday meals help more than anything else.
  • Reta blunts thirst as well as appetite. Dehydration on a GLP-1 reads as fatigue and headache and gets blamed on the compound. Push water hard on Monday and Tuesday.
  • PT-141 has a known acute profile: flushing, warmth and mild nausea inside the first hour, strongest on the earliest doses. Taking it in the evening rather than the morning is deliberate for that reason.
  • Kisspeptin is the quiet one. No acute effect to feel, nothing to titrate — it works upstream on the HPG axis and the change accumulates. Judge it at Week 14, not Week 10.
  • Neither hormonal compound replaces a hormone. Kisspeptin acts on your own pituitary signalling and PT-141 acts on melanocortin receptors in the brain — separate from the estrogen and progesterone pathways entirely. That is why this pair, rather than anything hormonal, is the answer to a high SHBG.
