Personalized Protocol
Ashley's Four-Cycle Protocol
One cycle per goal, run back to back — eight weeks each, thirty-two in total. Never more than four compounds at a time.
In your words
You travel constantly through the school year, you're exhausted, and you want enhanced cognitive clarity and maximized focus plus a sleep routine that survives being on the road. You also listed fat loss, muscle, longevity and hormonal balance. Six goals is a lot to chase at once — so they are not chased at once.
One goal per cycle
Your goals collapse into four groups, and each group gets its own eight-week cycle. Cycle 1 — Muscle Building + Fat Loss. Cycle 2 — Cognitive Enhancement. Cycle 3 — Sleep & Stress. Cycle 4 — Hormonal Balance. They run in that order, one after another, with no overlap.
Why this is the easier way to do it
Two reasons. You can actually tell what works. Fourteen compounds running together would make it impossible to attribute anything — run them four at a time and every cycle gives you a clean answer. And the daily load stays small: the busiest week in the whole protocol is 20 shots, and most weeks are 6 to 13. For a first-time peptide user that is the difference between a routine you keep and one you abandon in week three.
The shape of it
Wks 1–8 Muscle + Fat · Wks 9–16 Cognitive · Wks 17–24 Sleep & Stress · Wks 25–32 Hormonal. Three of the compounds — Cortagen, Epitalon and Pinealon — are Khavinson bioregulators that run as 20-day consecutive blocks rather than for a full eight weeks, so they sit at the front of their own cycle and then stop. Everything else runs the cycle out.
First-timer dosing
This is your first time with peptides, and you are already lean at 150 with a 7 lb goal. Every dose below sits at the low end of its range — clearest on the NAD+, which starts at the 50 mg entry dose rather than the 100 mg most stacks use; on both amidates, which open at 500 mcg and only double at Week 11; and on the Retatrutide, which is capped at 2 mg instead of the usual 4.
Citalopram overlap
You are on Citalopram 40 mg. Both the Semax and Selank amidates touch serotonergic signalling — Semax modulates dopamine and serotonin turnover, Selank works largely through GABA/tuftsin pathways with a secondary serotonergic effect. Neither is an SSRI, an MAOI or a direct serotonin agonist, so this is an overlap to be aware of, not a block. It is the reason both open at 500 mcg (5u) and only step to 1 mg at Week 11. The biggest advantage of four separate cycles is that you never have more than four vials open at once. Nutrition
Fueling 150 → 143 Across 32 Weeks
The frame

You are already lean. Seven pounds on a 5′10″ frame is a small, slow project, and the way to lose it without losing muscle or sleep quality is a modest deficit with the protein held high — not a hard cut. The other job this plan has is cognitive: your brain runs on stable blood sugar and adequate sleep, so the timing below is built to protect both. These same targets run through all four cycles — they do not change from Week 1 to Week 32. What changes is how hard you have to work to hit them, and that is hardest in Cycle 1 while the Retatrutide is running. Everything here is a starting point to tune against the weekly average, not a prescription.

Weeks 1–8 — eating on Retatrutide

The targets above do not change; your appetite does. Reta quiets hunger, which sounds helpful and is the main way lean people on a GLP-1 accidentally lose muscle instead of fat. Three rules for Cycle 1. One: hit the protein number whether or not you feel like eating — if a meal is too much food, a shake covers 40 g without the volume. Two: Reta blunts thirst as well as hunger, so add 20–30 oz on Monday and Tuesday after the shot. Three: keep Monday and Tuesday meals lighter and lower in fat — that is when nausea is most likely, and fat is the macro that makes it worse.

How To Run It
Three Things That Decide The Outcome
The Real Lever
Eating This On The Road
  • Protein is the only thing you have to defend. If a travel day destroys the plan, hit the protein number and let the rest go. That single habit protects the muscle you are trying to keep.
  • Pack the snack, always. A protein bar and a bag of nuts in the carry-on is the difference between a 1,750-calorie day and a 2,400-calorie airport day. This is where most of the drift happens.
  • Order the protein and the vegetable, add the carb second. Almost every restaurant menu works if you build the plate in that order. You do not need to find a special restaurant.
  • Hydrate harder on flying days. An extra 20–30 oz on travel days. Cabin dehydration reads as exactly the brain fog you are trying to get rid of.
  • Move dinner, not the shot. If a work dinner runs late, eat it — and skip the CJC/Ipa that night rather than injecting on a full stomach.
Sleep & Food
What You Eat Decides How You Sleep
  • Caffeine cutoff at 12pm. Caffeine has a 5–6 hour half-life, so a 3pm coffee still has a quarter of its dose working at bedtime. On a protocol built around deep sleep this is nearly free to fix.
  • Alcohol is the biggest single variable. It puts you to sleep faster and then wrecks the second half of the night — exactly the window CJC/Ipa and Epitalon are working in. Three drinks a week, none on Epitalon nights.
  • A light, early dinner sleeps better than a big late one — and it happens to be the same rule the evening shot needs. Those two requirements point the same direction, which makes this easier than it sounds.
  • Do not go to bed genuinely hungry either. If dinner at 6:30 leaves you hollow at 9, the fix is more protein and volume at dinner, not a late snack that breaks the fasting window.
Adjusting
Tune It To The Weekly Average
  • Weigh 2–3 mornings a week, same conditions — after the bathroom, before food. Use the weekly average, never a single day. Daily weight swings 2–4 lb on water alone.
  • Flat average after two full weeks? Drop about 150 calories — smaller carb portion at dinner covers it. Do not cut protein and do not cut fat to get there.
  • Losing faster than 1 lb a week? Add 150 back. At your body weight, faster than that starts costing lean tissue, which is the opposite of what the GH side of this stack is for.
  • Expect the first week to look odd. A change in carb intake moves water weight by several pounds in either direction before any fat has changed. The trend from Week 2 onward is the real signal.
  • Most of the 7 lb comes in Cycle 1. That is the only cycle with an appetite compound in it. Expect 4–6 lb by Week 8, and expect the rest to come slowly across Cycles 2–4 from the deficit alone. A flat month in Cycle 3 is not a stall.

Notes & Tips
Running Your First Protocol
Note 01 — Technique
Subcutaneous, Not Intramuscular
  • Every shot on this page is subcutaneous — into the fat layer just under the skin, not into muscle. You have injected before, so the mechanics are familiar; the difference is that these needles are much smaller and go in much shallower.
  • Batch them, do not spread them out. The busiest morning in the protocol is four shots, in Weeks 9–11. Draw and give them back to back in one sitting rather than remembering four separate times — different spots, same five minutes.
  • Pinch, insert at 45–90°, push slowly, withdraw. Abdomen is the easiest site — two inches out from the navel, anywhere around the ring. Outer thigh and the back of the upper arm also work.
  • Rotate. Move an inch or two from the last spot each time and work your way around the ring. Repeating the same square inch is what causes lumps and tenderness.
  • A small bruise or a red dot is normal and fades in a day or two. Injecting cold liquid straight from the fridge stings more — let the syringe sit out for a minute first.
  • Air bubbles are not a danger subcutaneously at these volumes, but tap them up and push them out anyway — a bubble displaces liquid, so it costs you dose accuracy.
Note 02 — Citalopram
The Serotonergic Overlap, Explained
  • Citalopram is an SSRI — it keeps serotonin in the synapse longer by blocking its reuptake. That is a direct, sustained effect on one neurotransmitter system.
  • The Semax amidate is not an SSRI. It is an ACTH(4–10) fragment whose primary action is raising BDNF and NGF; its effect on dopamine and serotonin turnover is modulatory and indirect, not reuptake inhibition and not agonism at serotonin receptors.
  • The Selank amidate works mainly through GABA and tuftsin pathways, with a secondary influence on serotonin metabolism. Again, no reuptake inhibition, no direct receptor agonism.
  • What that means practically: these are different mechanisms touching an overlapping system. That is a reason to start low and add one variable at a time — not a reason to avoid them. Both are at 500 mcg, half the dose commonly used.
  • Add them one at a time. Start the Semax Amidate first and hold the Selank Amidate until the following week. If something feels different, you will know which compound it came from. Hold both at 500 mcg (5u) for the first two weeks, then step to 1 mg (10u) at Week 11.
Note 03 — Travel
Running This From A Hotel Room
  • Cycle 2 is the easiest to travel with. Everything is a morning shot, and three of the four run Mon/Wed/Fri only — Tue and Thu are one Cortagen shot, and after Wk 11 nothing at all. Cycle 3 is easier still: everything is at bedtime.
  • Cycle 1 is the one with a rule. CJC/Ipa needs 2+ hours clear of food, and Cycle 4's mornings need to be fasted. On a night with a late work dinner, skip the CJC/Ipa — do not inject on a full stomach and do not push it to 1am.
  • Anchor the Cycle 3 block to local bedtime, not to the clock you left behind. During the 20-night pulse take Epitalon and Pinealon right before sleep in whatever time zone you are actually in. That is precisely the signal you want to be giving the pineal gland.
  • Pack for the trip, not the protocol. Only the current cycle's vials travel — at most four — plus the syringes you need with two spares, a strip of alcohol pads, and the insulated pouch. Carry-on. Hotel mini-fridges are usually fine; set them to the coldest setting that is not freezing.
  • If a hotel has no fridge, the insulated pouch with a refreshed gel pack holds overnight without trouble. A single warm night will not ruin a vial.
Note 04 — Missed Doses
Skip It, Do Not Stack It
  • Missed an AM shot? Take it whenever you remember, as long as it is before mid-afternoon. Later than that, skip it — Semax late in the day can push bedtime back.
  • Missed a PM shot? Skip it entirely. Never double up the next night. One missed CJC/Ipa dose out of forty changes nothing.
  • Missed a night of a 20-day block? Epitalon, Pinealon and Cortagen all work the same way — just continue the count the next day and finish day 20 one day later. Do not take two in a day to catch up.
  • Missed the Monday Retatrutide? Take it within 48 hours — Tuesday or Wednesday morning is fine, then go back to Monday the following week. Past Wednesday, skip it and resume the next Monday at the same dose, not a higher one. Never double a Reta dose.
  • Missed a Kisspeptin, Testagen, Oxytocin or PT-141? Skip it. None of the four has a loading effect that one gap undoes — Kisspeptin and Testagen are slow accumulators, and PT-141 and Oxytocin are never more than three days from the next one.
  • Missed a NAD+, amidate, Cortagen or DSIP? Skip it. Never double a NAD+ dose — a doubled dose is exactly how a manageable flush becomes an unpleasant one. For Cortagen, just continue the count the next morning; you will finish day 20 one day later.
  • Missed a whole travel week? Pick the schedule back up where you left it — that cycle simply runs a week longer, and everything after it shifts by a week. Nothing about the sequence breaks.
Note 05 — Timeline
What Shows Up, And When
  • Cycle 1, Wks 1–2 — sleep, then appetite. Deeper sleep from the CJC/Ipa is usually the very first thing you notice in the whole protocol. Reta's appetite effect lands on the first or second shot.
  • Cycle 1, Wks 5–8 — the scale. Reta at its 2 mg ceiling is where most of the fat loss lands. At 0.5–0.75 lb a week expect 4–6 lb by Week 8, with the rest coming slowly over the later cycles.
  • Cycle 2, Wks 9–11 — focus, and the flush. Semax Amidate clarity typically shows up inside the first week or two, often inside 40 minutes of the first dose. Expect the NAD+ flush on the first few Mon/Wed/Fri mornings — it fades as tolerance builds. Cortagen will not announce itself at all; bioregulators never do.
  • Cycle 2, Wks 12–16 — the Cortagen read. Bioregulators are judged a few weeks after the course. If cognition is better here than it was in Week 10, that is the compound you are feeling. The NAD+ effect reads the same way: afternoons that do not collapse, rather than a lift you can point at.
  • Cycle 3, Wks 17–19 — the travel test. DSIP moves sleep depth quickly. The real read on the pineal pair is how you feel after the first trip that crosses time zones during the block — that is what it is actually for.
  • Cycle 4, Wks 25–32 — the slowest cycle. PT-141's acute effects are loudest on the earliest doses and settle. Kisspeptin, Testagen and Oxytocin all accumulate: judge them at Week 30, not Week 26.
  • Both amidates are subtle by design. Neither is a stimulant. The effect people describe is a higher floor rather than a peak — less fade, less edge, easier to hold attention. If you are waiting for something dramatic you will miss it.
Note 06 — Starting A Cycle
Stagger The Introductions Every Time
  • Never start more than two new compounds in the same week. This is the rule that makes a four-cycle protocol worth running — if everything starts at once you learn nothing about any of it.
  • Wk 1 — Cycle 1. Start CJC/Ipa and MOTS-c; add Retatrutide the following Monday. Reta is the compound with a real side-effect curve, so give it a clean week to be judged in.
  • Wk 9 — Cycle 2. Start the Semax Amidate and Cortagen; add the Selank Amidate in Wk 10 and NAD+ in Wk 11. NAD+ last on purpose — it is the one with a physical sensation, and you want to know it is the NAD+ you are feeling. Both amidates also step from 5u to 10u at Wk 11; stagger those two steps by a few days as well.
  • Wk 17 — Cycle 3. Start DSIP plus the Epitalon and Pinealon block together. The block has to run consecutively from night one, so this cycle is the exception — all three at once, which is fine because all three are gentle.
  • Wk 25 — Cycle 4. Start Kisspeptin and Testagen; add PT-141 and Oxytocin in Wk 27. PT-141 is the only one here with an acute effect, so it goes in once the quiet ones are established.
  • None of the hormonal compounds replaces a hormone. Kisspeptin acts on your own pituitary signalling, PT-141 on melanocortin receptors, Testagen at the tissue. That is why this group, rather than anything hormonal, is the answer to a high SHBG.
Note 07 — NAD+
The One You Will Feel Going In
  • Flushing, warmth, chest or gut pressure, sometimes an urge to get up and move. That is the NAD+ and it is normal. It is dose- and speed-dependent, not dangerous, and it passes within a few minutes of finishing the injection.
  • Thirty to sixty seconds on the plunger. This is the single biggest lever you have. Pushing a NAD+ dose fast is what turns a mild warm wave into a genuinely unpleasant few minutes. Slow is the whole technique.
  • You are starting at 50 mg (25u), not 100. That is half what most stacks open with, chosen because this is your first cycle. Tolerance builds — by the third or fourth dose most people barely notice it.
  • Only step up if you want to. If 50 mg is completely comfortable after two weeks, 75 mg is 37.5u. There is no requirement to move; 50 mg three times a week is a real dose.
  • Do it last in the morning block. Semax, Selank and Cortagen are quick. Give the NAD+ its own slow minute at the end rather than rushing it because you are already four shots in.
